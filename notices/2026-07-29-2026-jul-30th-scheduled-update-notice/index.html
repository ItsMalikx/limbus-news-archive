2026, Jul. 30th Scheduled Update Notice

July 29, 2026
Balance

Greetings, Dear Manager.

The server will enter scheduled maintenance from 10:00 [AM] through 12:00 [PM] on Jul. 30th, 2026 (KST).

Please make note of the maintenance schedule; we recommend that you refrain from entering combat or making in-app purchases and close the application before maintenance begins so that no data is lost.

Please refer below for details about the update.

Bug Fixes & Improvements

▶ Fixed an issue where the Abnormality "Fairy-Long-Legs" would still trigger its Choice Event even after being defeated

▶ Fixed an issue where certain spine animation resources would play incorrectly in the Victory screen

▶ Fixed an issue where, in the Refraction Railway Line 2 BokGak boarding UI, the size of the "CLEAR" stamp (which appears over a reward when it is claimed after clearing the Terminus) would appear too small in certain Sections, making it difficult to see whether a reward has been claimed or not

※ This is a visual-only issue; the rewards in question were being obtained as they normally would. This fix only addresses the visibility issue with the "CLEAR" stamp in the Line boarding menu.

※ If you have successfully claimed the reward in question, a "COMPLETE" stamp would appear over the reward in the Lost & Found menu.

▶ Fixed an issue where "When used for the first time in an Encounter... ... consume fewer E.G.O resources" effect did not activate when certain relevant E.G.O resources were in the negatives
→ Relevant E.G.O : Shadow-vested Bladesinger [着影揮刀] Meursault

▶ Fixed an issue where, when "Dawn Office Fixer Sinclair" enters the "Volatile E.G.O::Waxen Pinion" state in the first turn of an Encounter while equipped with a Façade, the turn would pass abnormally

▶ Fixed an issue where the coinflip animation of "N Corp. E.G.O::Fell Bullet Yi Sang" and "N Corp. E.G.O::Contempt, Awe Ryōshū" Skill "I Shall Fire / Anytime" could malfunction under specific conditions
→ Specific Condition: When "N Corp. E.G.O::Fell Bullet Yi Sang" is equipped with a Façade, but "N Corp. E.G.O::Contempt, Awe Ryōshū" is not

※ This is a visual-only issue; the coinflip damage calculation was working as intended. This fix only addresses the coinflip animation issue.

Maintenance & Application Update Compensation

- Content: Lunacy x300
- Recipients: All users whose game accounts were created before Aug. 6th, 2026, 10:00 AM (KST)
- When: After Jul. 30th (Thu), 2026, 12:00 (KST)
- Can be claimed once per account

Relevant Compensation can be claimed until Aug. 6th (Thu), 2026, 10:00 (KST) via the in-game Mailbox (mail available for 7 days).

Notes

- Please update your game client to the newest version on the respective store (Google Play/App Store/Steam).
- Forcibly shutting down the game while it is downloading resources may cause the download to fail, so please be careful not to close the application during the download.
- You will not be able to play <Limbus Company> during maintenance. Please understand.
- The maintenance schedule may suddenly change depending on the status of maintenance. If such a change occurs, we will let you know in a follow-up notice.
- A resource download may be prompted after the build update. If you're using a mobile device, it is recommended to proceed with a Wi-Fi connection.
- If you're playing with a guest account, we recommend that you link your account with other platforms to prevent possible account loss.
- The maintenance compensation will be provided once per account. Please note that the distribution may be slow due to network delays or other causes.
- The update gifts are valid to claim for 7 days. Please redeem them within this timeframe.

Thank you.
