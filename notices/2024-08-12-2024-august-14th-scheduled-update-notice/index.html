2024, August 14th Scheduled Update Notice

August 12, 2024
Balance

Greetings, Dear Manager.

The server will enter scheduled maintenance from 10:00 [AM] through 12:00 [PM] on Aug. 14th, 2024 (KST).

Please make note of the maintenance schedule; we recommend that you refrain from entering combat or making in-app purchases and close the application before maintenance begins so that no data is lost.

Please refer below for details about the update.

This week's Scheduled Update will be conducted on 10AM(KST), Wednesday.

However, weekly resets will occur at 6AM(KST), Thursday as usual, regardless of the Scheduled Update. Please note this information for your gameplay plans.

Bug Fixes & Improvements

▶ Fixed an issue where, under certain circumstances, Counter Skills could be used repeatedly

▶ Fixed an issue where a boss enemy's Passive Skill was behaving incorrectly

※ Relevant EffectGain 1 Clash Power for every 10 Bleed (max 5)
※ IssueThe enemy gains up to 9 Clash Power
contrary to the cap listed in the description

▶ Fixed an issue where additional Skill Slots could be gained in certain Focused Encounters of the Murder on the WARP Express event

▶ Fixed an issue where making a certain choice in Stage 5-36 Choice Event did not allow the player to enter the subsequent combat Encounter

※ Relevant Choice : "We don't know what'll happen if we do. Let's stay put."

▶ Translation: Addressed various typos, grammatical issues, awkward sentence structures, and mistranslations in Murder on the WARP Express story cutscenes

▶ Raised the BGM volume for Murder on the WARP Express boss Encounter

▶ (EN Only) Fixed an issue where Kurokumo Clan Wakashu Hong Lu's Skill 1 changed its name when Uptying to Uptie Tier 4

- Before : Uptying from Tier 3 → 4 would cause Skill 1 name to change from Cleave → Hack

- After : Uptying from Tier 3 → 4 no longer changes Skill 1 name from Cleave → Hack

▶ (EN Only) Fixed an issue where Uptying Molar Office Fixer Outis from Uptie Tier 3 to 4 caused incorrect areas to be highlighted despite no changes occurring there

Known Issues

▶ An issue where an incorrect resource loads in the cutscene after clearing 6-48

▶ An issue where viewing the Trait Keywords popup in 'Detailed Information' in a Combat Encounter then closing out with an ESC input caused the popup to linger in other Detailed Information UI as well

▶ An issue where attempting to scroll down a long description of an E.G.O Gift obtained from a Mirror Dungeon Choice Event would not properly scroll the text

▶ An issue where an E.G.O Skill with a special VFX in Murder on the WARP Express combat Encounter did not activate properly

▶ An issue where, in Stage 5-18, the camera movements would not behave as intended upon entering the stage
※ It behaves as intended in subsequent Encounters

▶ An issue where the cutscene post Stage 6-44 clear does not load properly

▶ An issue where attempting to enter the Murder on the WARP Train Stage immediately after clearing the Timekilling Time Intervallo causes the game to softlock
※ You can enter the Murder on the WARP Train Stage by restarting the game.

The above issues will be addressed via a future Scheduled Update, and we will inform them via Scheduled Update Notices as soon as they are fixed.

We apologize for the inconvenience.

Notes

- Please update your game client to the newest version on the respective store (Google Play/App Store/Steam).
- Forcibly shutting down the game while it is downloading resources may cause the download to fail, so please be careful not to close the application during the download.
- You will not be able to play <Limbus Company> during maintenance. Please understand.
- The maintenance schedule may suddenly change depending on the status of maintenance. If such a change occurs, we will let you know in a follow-up notice.
- A resource download may be prompted after the build update. If you're using a mobile device, it is recommended to proceed with a Wi-Fi connection.
- If you're playing with a guest account, we recommend that you link your account with other platforms to prevent possible account loss.
- The maintenance compensation will be provided once per account. Please note that the distribution may be slow due to network delays or other causes.
- The update gifts are valid to claim for 7 days. Please redeem them within this timeframe.
