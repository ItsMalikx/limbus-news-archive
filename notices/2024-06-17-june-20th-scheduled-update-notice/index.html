June 20th Scheduled Update Notice

June 17, 2024
Mirror DungeonBalanceMain Story

Greetings, dear Manager.

The server will enter scheduled maintenance from 10:00 [AM] through 12:00 [PM] on Jun. 20th, 2024 (KST).

Please make note of the maintenance schedule; we recommend that you refrain from entering combat or making in-app purchases and close the application before maintenance begins so that no data is lost.

Please refer below for details about the update.

Bug Fixes and Improvements

▶ Fixed an issue where a UI issue would occur when Threadspinning or Uptiying after the in-game daily reset occurs while the game is running

▶ Fixed an issue where, in Stages with mandatory Sinner participants, not meeting the condition would show a popup with the incorrect Sinner name

▶ Fixed an issue where, when the Chapter 6.5's Boss' certain Skill fails to deal damage to an ally, the struck ally would still get Staggered if the ally's HP was lower than an unbroken Stagger Threshold

▶ Fixed various typos in the Intervallo IV: Timekilling Time cutscenes

▶ Fixed a CG image error in Story Stage 6.5-3

▶ Fixed an issue where certain characters had incorrect colors in Story Stage 6.5-12

▶ Fixed an issue where one of the Stage 6-48 boss' Skills used an incorrect Keyword

▶ Fixed an issue where, in rare occasions, using the Refresh function in the Mirror Dungeon Shops caused the "Anything else to buy?" text to repeatedly appear over one another

▶ Addressed the issue where the unique Mirror Dungeon Events for the Mirror Dungeon Floor Theme Pack - 'Hell's Chicken' appeared less often than it was supposed to

▶ (EN Only) Fixed an issue where the incorrect translation was applied to the Mirror Dungeon Floor Theme Pack - 'Timekilling Time' Mirror Dungeon Event
※ Affected Choice Event: The event in which the player has to choose between following the youth or the old man in the T Corp. streets
- Before : Who should wield the sword?
- After : Who will step up?

▶ Fixed an issue where, in the 'The Unloving' Mirror Dungeon Floor Theme Pack, the players were able to continue with the battle even after the allies are wiped out due to the boss gimmick

▶ Fixed an issue where, in Mirror Dungeon Events where choices appear sequentially, the first line of text gets skipped immediately

Known Issues

▶ An issue where, in Intervallo IV: Timekilling Time cutscenes, certain characters' sprites don't load properly and instead appear as white rectangles

▶ An issue where, the Timekilling Time Floor Theme Pack E.G.O Gifts don't appear in the Mirror Dungeon E.G.O Gift Compendium

The above issues will be corrected via a future Scheduled Update, and we will inform you through a Scheduled Update Notice as soon as they are addressed.

We apologize for the inconvenience.

Maintenance & Application Update Compensation

- Content: Lunacy x300
- Recipients: All users whose game accounts were created before 10:00 Jun. 27th, 2024 (KST)
- When: After Jun. 20th, 2024 (Thu) 12:00 (KST)
- Can be claimed once per account

Maintenance Compensation can be claimed until Jun. 27th (Thu) 2024, 10:00 (KST) via the in-game Mailbox. (Mail available for 7 days)

Notes

- Please update your game client to the newest version on the respective store (Google Play/App Store/Steam).
- Forcibly shutting down the game while it is downloading resources may cause the download to fail, so please be careful not to close the application during the download.
- You will not be able to play <Limbus Company> during maintenance. Please understand.
- The maintenance schedule may suddenly change depending on the status of maintenance. If such a change occurs, we will let you know in a follow-up notice.
- A resource download may be prompted after the build update. If you're using a mobile device, it is recommended to proceed with a Wi-Fi connection.
- If you're playing with a guest account, we recommend that you link your account with other platforms to prevent possible account loss.
- The maintenance compensation will be provided once per account. Please note that the distribution may be slow due to network delays or other causes.
- The update gifts are valid to claim for 7 days. Please redeem them within this timeframe.

Thank you.
