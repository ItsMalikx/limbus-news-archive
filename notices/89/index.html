    Mirror Dungeon 2 Hard - Reward Collection Adjustments

    July 13, 2023
    Mirror DungeonBalanceRewards

    Hello, this is Project Moon.

    With the scheduled update on July 20th (KST), Mirror Dungeon 2 Hard's Reward collection frequency will be adjusted.

    <Mirror Dungeon 2 Hard - Reward collection adjusted>

    Before
    - Reward Coll. per week: 3x per week
    - Currency Consumed Upon reward Coll.: Enkephalin Module x 6
    - Battle Pass EXP Gained (Max): With Bonus : 75, Without Bonus : 50

    After
    - Reward Coll. per week: 1x per week
    - Currency Consumed Upon reward Coll.: Enkephalin Module x 18
    - Battle Pass EXP Gained (Max): 150 + # of remaining Bonuses
    - Notes: Collecting Rewards will consume all remaining Weekly Bonuses

    <intent>

    We intend to alleviate the stress and time investment involved with playing Mirror Dungeon 2 Hard by reducing the number of repeated runs while maintaining the same amount of rewards.

    Though the number of times in which the rewards can be collected per week has been reduced, the number of Enkephaline Modules consumed and the amount of Mirror Dungeon 2 Hard rewards remains the same.

    Please note that the Weekly Bonus and Hard Difficulty rewards will not be reset upon this update.

    Collecting a single Hard Difficulty reward between 6:00 ~9:59 (KST) on July 20th will reduce the number of collectable Hard Difficulty rewards to 0.

    Please take care to refrain from collecting your Weekly Bonus and Hard Difficulty rewards during that time period.
