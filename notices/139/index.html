Notice: Identified a Game Crashing Issue in Combat

October 19, 2023

Hello, this is Project Moon.

We have identified an issue where the game freezes
under the following conditions in combat.

- When, in Refraction Railway Line 2, restart the stage with
'retry stage' option while using an E.G.O skill. Then, equip the
same E.G.O Skill used immediately before retrying this stage,
in the E.G.O Skill equipment menu.

- Occasionally when equipping a Defense Skill

- Occasionally when running the Mirror Dungeons

We are currently investigating and fixing the above issue,
And will notify you with an additional notice with detailed
information on its causes and error fix schedule once it is corrected.

We apologize for causing you
inconvenience to your enjoyment of our game.
