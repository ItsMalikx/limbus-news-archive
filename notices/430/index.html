    2025.07.17(KST) Scheduled Update Additional Notice

    July 14, 2025
    EventsNew Content

    Hello, this is Project Moon.

    We would like to inform you about information that was missing from our July 14th Scheduled Update notices.

    Please continue reading for more information.

    ▶ Bug Fixes & Improvements
    - Fixed an issue where, in certain circumstances, UI controls become unavailable when entering Mirror of Immortality

    ※ This issue occurred with the July 10th Mirror of Immortality Hard Difficulty.
    With the unlock conditions for the Hard Difficulty being changed to "Clear Stage 7-36", we identified that this issue occurs in certain managers who have yet to clear Stage 7-36 and have not played Mirror of Immortality Normal Difficulty.
    ※ Please note that this issue was identified by the time we were writing the July 14th Scheduled Update, and will be fixed with the July 17th Scheduled Update.

    ▶ Walpurgis Night Extractions
    ※ Vergilius Announcer, previously distributed via the 2nd Anniversary Event, will be added to the list of extractables available from Walpurgis Night.
    ※ With the addition of Vergilius Announcer to Walpurgis Night extractions, the Announcer can now be obtained from Yisang Exchange as well.

    <Extractions in which Vergilius Announcer is either featured or obtainable from Yisang Exchange>

    1. The 6th Walpurgis Night New Target Extraction
    2. The 1st & 2nd Walpurgis Night Target Extraction BokGak

    We sincerely apologize for the confusion caused by our incomplete notice.
