2025, Jan. 16th Scheduled Update Notice

January 13, 2025
Balance

Greetings, Dear Manager.

The server will enter scheduled maintenance from 10:00 [AM] through 12:00 [PM] on Jan. 16th, 2025 (KST).

Please make note of the maintenance schedule; we recommend that you refrain from entering combat or making in-app purchases and close the application before maintenance begins so that no data is lost.

Please refer below for details about the update.

Bug Fixes & Improvements

▶ Fixed an issue where the E.G.O Gift 'Lucky Pouch' affected Full-Stop Office Fixer Heathcliff's Defense Skill 'Taking Cover'

▶ Fixed an issue where, when Heathcliff's voicelines were supposed to be limited in certain Stages, the 'Target Spotted' effect voiceline would still play

▶ Fixed an issue where the E.G.O Gift 'Rusty Commemorative Coin' could cause Outis E.G.O - Magic Bullet to malfunction upon Coin Reuse when 'The Sixth Magic Bullet' buff activates

▶ Adjusted the effect activation limit description for [If this unit is out of Ammo, gain X Ammo at the next Turn Start] effect of the E.G.O Gift 'Torn Bandolier'
- Before: Once per Encounter
- After: Once per Encounter per Identity (Currently, it says 'Once per Encounter for each Identity; however, for consistency, the description will be changed to reflect the above)

※ The description used to say "Once per Encounter", but its actual effects were working as per the updated text; the E.G.O Gift's functions do not actually change.
※ The above description change was applied with the Jan. 9th, 2025 Scheduled Update, but was missing from the Update Notice. We apologize for the inconvenience.

▶ Fixed an issue where refusing to gain an E.G.O Gift after winning an Encounter in the Mirror Dungeon of the Fathoms stages could cause the game to softlock

▶ Fixed an issue where the game could softlock when exiting 'The 4th Walpurgis Night' Team Loadout menu via the back button([ ← ])

▶ Fixed an issue where the Buff Icon applied to the #1 Deployed Sinner was switched between The 2nd and the 4th Walpurgis Night Team Loadout menus

▶ Fixed an issue where The 4th Walpurgis Night Event Tutorial Images loaded incorrectly

Bug Fixes & Improvements

▶ Fixed an issue where, in the Identity Uptie Preview UI, if the Identity has more than 5 Skills that get enhanced, some Skills may be missing from the Preview UI

▶ Changed the special characters appearing in some of the Keyword names
※ Affected Keywords: Shimmering [Bloodfiend], Concentration[Sniper]

▶ Deleted dummy text(Marksman's Bullet) from the description of the buff 'Ready to Fire'

▶ (EN Only) Fixed a typo for the E.G.O Gift 'CQC Manual'
- Before: CQC Maual
- After: CQC Manual

▶ (EN, JP Only) Fixed typos in the Mini Episode - The 5th Walpurgis Night

Known Issues

▶ An issue where the 'Target Spotted' effect activates even when Full-Stop Office Fixer Heathcliff is Corroded

▶ An issue where the 'Melee Support' effect activates even when Full-Stop Office Rep Hong Lu is Corroded

Known Issues after the 2025. 01. 16 Scheduled Update

▶ (Android, IOS) An issue where a new option for Push Notifications is added to the 'Settings - General' menu, but does nothing
※ We added a Push Notification function to the game, but realized an issue where it does not work as intended. So, we have removed it from this week's update.

Maintenance & Application Update Compensation

- Content : Lunacy x300
- Recipients : All users whose game accounts were created before 10:00 Jan 23rd, 2025 (KST)
- When : After Jan. 16th, 2025 (Thu) 12:00 (KST)
- Can be claimed once per account

Maintenance Compensation can be claimed until Jan 23rd (Thu) 2025, 10:00 (KST) via the in-game Mailbox. (Mail available for 7 days)

Notes

- Please update your game client to the newest version on the respective store (Google Play/App Store/Steam).
- Forcibly shutting down the game while it is downloading resources may cause the download to fail, so please be careful not to close the application during the download.
- You will not be able to play <Limbus Company> during maintenance. Please understand.
- The maintenance schedule may suddenly change depending on the status of maintenance. If such a change occurs, we will let you know in a follow-up notice.
- A resource download may be prompted after the build update. If you're using a mobile device, it is recommended to proceed with a Wi-Fi connection.
- If you're playing with a guest account, we recommend that you link your account with other platforms to prevent possible account loss.
- The maintenance compensation will be provided once per account. Please note that the distribution may be slow due to network delays or other causes.
- The update gifts are valid to claim for 7 days. Please redeem them within this timeframe.

Thank you.
