Schemacraft Beta: Known Issues & Survey Preliminary Notice

November 26, 2026
Balance

Hello, this is Project Moon.

We would like to inform you of known issues identified in "Schemacraft Beta", updated Nov. 26th, 2026, and the schedule for the upcoming Schemacraft Beta survey.

Please refer below for more information.

<Known Issues>

▶ An issue where an Identity's action frequency may vary within the same shared turn depending on their Skill animation length

[Cause]
- While an Identity's Skill animation is playing, their action gauge for triggering their next action remains paused in the current version of Schemacraft.
- Because the sum of "Skill animation length" and "the time it takes for the action gauge to refill" determines the interval between an Identity's actions, Identities with shorter Skill animations are more likely to take an additional action within the same shared turn.
- Despite not being displayed on each Identity's information page, Skill animation length was affecting the Identities' action economy.

[Affected Areas]
- All units, both allies and enemies, are guaranteed at least 1 action per shared turn. Units with fast Speed values may even take 2 or more actions per shared turn.
- This animation length issue comes into play when an Identity takes, or plans to take, a second action within the same shared turn.
- The ability to take an extra action has a direct effect on an Identity's damage output and status application, which affects balancing.
※ However, as Skills with multiple Coins generally deal more damage in a single action, the action economy isn't the sole determinant of an Identity's power.

[Improvement Direction: Combat Action Economy Adjustments]
We are planning to move the next action preparation trigger from "After the Skill animation is completed" to "After a certain amount of time has passed since the Skill animation began".
Through this, we can remove animation length from the equation, making it so that action economy is determined only by a verifiable factor, the Speed value.
※ If an Identity has a short animation, it will generally wait for a period of time from the start of its animation before the next action is prepared.
※ The time loss due to long animations will be factored into calculating how long it takes for an Identity to prepare their next action.

If an Identity has a long animation, the next action will generally start preparing before the animation ends.

1. Changes to Gameplay Feel
(1) You may notice Identities on standby after using Skills with brief animations.
(2) Identities who have longer animations will now take their next action earlier than they used to.
(3) Shorter animations will have less effect on the action economy, and Identities with faster Speed values will now have a clearer advantage.

※ The length and tempo of each Encounter will generally remain the same.
※ You may notice the above (1) ~ (3) changes on enemy units as well.

2. What Remains the Same
(1) Using an E.G.O will not delay the Identity's next action.
(2) Defense Skills that ended without receiving an attack will not be subject to waiting periods.

<Upcoming Schemacraft Beta Survey>

We will be conducting a survey to gather feedback from our Managers on their experiences with the Schemacraft Beta. Through this survey, we will identify possible areas and directions for improvement.

- Survey Date: Dec. 3rd (Thu), 2026 (KST)

We will inform you again via a separate notice when the survey is released.

We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience.
Thank you.
