June 22nd Scheduled Update Notice

June 19, 2023
Balance

Greetings, Dear Manager.

The server will enter maintenance from 10:00 [AM] through 12:00 [PM] on June 22nd, 2023 (KST) for system optimizations and content updates. Please make note of the maintenance schedule; we recommend that you refrain from entering combat or making in-app purchases and close the application before maintenance begins so that no data is lost. Refer below for details about the update.

Update Contents

1. Bug Fixes and Improvements

Battle Announcer - Adjusted frequency of voiceline for attacks with type/affinity disadvantage
Before: Voiceline played for all disadvantageous attacks
After: Voiceline played for unopposed attacks that are disadvantageous

Added subtitle for certain line of dialogue during a cutscene after battle in 4-54

- 4-48 Enemy Info for certain enemies - Fixed ‘UNKNOWN’ showing for passives

- Fixed errors with acquisition conditions and acquisition date display for ticket decoration items ‘Rainy Day’ and ‘Dongbaek’

- Fixed certain enemy status effect icons not being shown in 4-47

- Fixed the artwork for an Uptie Tier-3 Identity set as its pre-Uptie version forcibly changing to the post-Uptie one when EXP tickets are used on it

- Fixed erroneous description for Imagined T Corp. Class 3 Collector's passive - Golden Time
※ Changed Text: At the end of the turn, if the HP is between 12% and 24%, instantly heal up to 65% of Max HP, and recover from stagger. (One-time only)
※ Actual functionality had no issue.

- Fixed status effect icons missing for Imagined T Corp. Class 3 Collector
1) Interlocking Time
2) Borrowed Time

- Fixed dummy data being displayed for the passives section for certain enemies in the stage info for 4-54

- Improved Sinner dialogue box display in the checks screen
Before: The dialogue box of the manually selected Sinner disappears in a short time
After: The dialogue box of the manually selected Sinner remains until the check is initiated (The box temporarily disappears when hovering the cursor over to select other Sinners)

- Fixed an issue where the descriptions for Coins 1 and 2 of Kurokumo Wakashu Hong Lu's skill 'Cloud Cutter' didn't match their actual effects

Maintenance & Application Update Compensation

- Content: 300 Lunacy
- Recipients: Every user whose game account was created before 09:59 June 29th, 2023 (KST)

Notes

- Please update your game client to the newest version on the respective store (Google Play/App Store/Steam).
- Forcibly shutting down the game while it is downloading resources may cause the download to fail, so please be careful not to close the application during the download.
- You will not be able to play <Limbus Company> during maintenance. Please understand.
- The maintenance schedule may suddenly change depending on the status of maintenance. If such a change occurs, we will let you know in a follow-up notice.
- A resource download may be prompted after the build update. If you're using a mobile device, it is recommended to proceed with a Wi-Fi connection.
- If you're playing with a guest account, we recommend that you link your account with other platforms to prevent possible account loss.
- The maintenance compensation will be provided once per account. Please note that the distribution may be slow due to network delays or other causes.
- The update gifts are valid to claim for 7 days. Please redeem them within this timeframe.

Thank you.
