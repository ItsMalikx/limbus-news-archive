Bug Fixes & Improvements

▶ Added an option to toggle the visual effects of certain Skills of "The House of Spiders: The Ring Nursefather Hong Lu" during certain battles
→ Relevant Skills: "Installation Art no. 3: Improvised Ribcage", "Closing Time - Installation Art no. 1: Your Flesh and Bones as the Gallery's Seats"
※ The ON/OFF toggle function will be added under Settings - General.

▶ Fixed an issue where the positions of the Spine illustration effects for "Haute Couture::Le Noir Footwear Hall Ryōshū" would not match between the Team Loadout menu and the Theater

▶ Fixed an issue where certain effects of specific E.G.O Awakening Skills would activate differently than intended while in the E.G.O Corrosion state
→ Relevant E.G.O: Unbrilliant Glory Gregor, Shadow-vested Bladesinger [着影近刀] Meursault
→ Relevant Effects: "When used for the first time in battle, consume fewer E.G.O Resources", "When used for the first time in an Encounter, consume less SP"
- Before: The relevant effects activated when the E.G.O was first used while in the E.G.O Corrosion state
- After: The relevant effects no longer activate when the E.G.O is first used while in the E.G.O Corrosion state

▶ Fixed an issue where certain voice lines from the "Repudiation Hong Lu" E.G.O would play during "Malkuth's Reign - Imperfect Manifestation"

▶ (The Shadowed) Fixed an issue where the Skill Keywords of a certain unit would be displayed as "UNKNOWN"

▶ (The Symbolic) Fixed an issue where the effects of "Mordant" would activate differently than intended
→ Relevant Effect: Enemies standing on these tiles have Movement Speed -30%
- Before: Reduced the Movement Speed of allies instead of enemies
- After: Reduced the Movement Speed of enemies, as described

▶ (The Symbolic) Fixed an issue where, after defeating a certain unit, its default animation would play instead of its corpse sprite

▶ Fixed an issue where scrolling would not function smoothly on the "Sapling of Light" UI during a certain battle

▶ Fixed issues that occurred when viewing Main Story Chapter 10 stories in the Theater
※ Fix 1: Fixed an issue where items obtained in the Symbolic would be applied
※ Fix 2: Fixed an issue where certain filters would not be applied during specific story segments
※ Fix 3: Fixed an issue where certain units would move abnormally during specific story segments

▶ Fixed an issue where the relevant links would not function on the Terms of Service agreement UI displayed after launching the game
