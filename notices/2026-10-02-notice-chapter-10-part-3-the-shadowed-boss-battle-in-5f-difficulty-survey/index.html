    Notice: Chapter 10 - Part 3 The Shadowed Boss Battle in 5F Difficulty Survey

    October 2, 2026
    Main StoryBalance

    Hello, this is Project Moon.

    After monitoring various feedback following the Oct. 1st Scheduled Update, we decided that Main Story Chapter 10 - Part 3 The Shadowed Boss Battle in 5F needed certain difficulty adjustments.
    In order to determine the specifics of the adjustment, we will be conducting a combat difficulty survey.

    - Limbus Company Main Story Chapter 10 - Part 3 The Shadowed Boss Battle in 5F difficulty survey
    - Survey period: Until Oct. 8th (Thu) 10:00 AM (KST)
    ※ Please note that the contents of the survey may contain spoilers for players who have not accessed that battle yet.

    https://forms.gle/ZyNvgKVZrL5SaJix8

    We will review potential difficulty adjustments based on the submitted responses. We ask that Managers answer as candidly as possible regarding their experience while progressing through the Story.

    To collect and review the responses promptly and make adjustments for a smoother gameplay experience, the survey will remain available only until Oct. 8th (Thu), 10:00 (KST). We ask for your kind understanding.

    We will discuss potential adjustments internally based on the survey results. When announcing the difficulty adjustments, we also intend to disclose selected portions of the responses so that Managers can understand the reasoning behind the changes.

    We will work to reduce the gap between our internal testing environment and Managers' actual gameplay experiences, providing a smoother and more enjoyable gameplay.

    We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience.
