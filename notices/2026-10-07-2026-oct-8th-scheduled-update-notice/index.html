2026, Oct. 8th Scheduled Update Notice

October 7, 2026
Main StoryMirror DungeonBalanceRewards

Greetings, Dear Manager.

The server will enter scheduled maintenance from 10:00 AM through 12:00 PM on Oct. 8th, 2026 (KST).

Please make note of the maintenance schedule; we recommend that you refrain from entering combat or making in-app purchases and close the application before maintenance begins so that no data is lost.

Please refer below for details about the update.

※ As Mirror Dungeon 8 is scheduled for release, maintenance for the Oct. 15th Scheduled Update will take place from 06:00 through 12:00 (KST).

When the Oct. 15th update maintenance concludes at 12:00, the number of Mirror Dungeon Weekly Bonuses and any ongoing Mirror Dungeon run records will be reset.

Any Mirror Dungeon run records remaining from before Oct. 15th, 06:00 will be reset, and their rewards will become unobtainable. Projection Level progression and reward claims for Mirror Dungeon 7, "Mirror of Names and Spiders", will also become unavailable.

Please keep this in mind and ensure that you claim all Projection Rewards before the Oct. 15th Scheduled Update maintenance.

Lunacy Returned for Using the Stage Continue Function

As the description explaining the gimmick of the boss unit on 5F of Main Story Chapter 10 - Part 3 has been updated with a more direct and intuitive explanation, we will be returning any Lunacy spent on the Stage Continue function for that Encounter prior to the description change.

- Eligible Recipients
: All Managers who have used the "Stage Continue" function in the boss fight featured on 5F of Main Story Chapter 10 (Part 3) during the following period: 2026.10.01 12:00 (KST) ~ 19:30 (KST)

- Lunacy Restoration
: All Lunacy spent on the "Stage Continue" function in the boss fight featured on 5F of Main Story Chapter 10 - Part 3

- When
: After the Oct. 8th Scheduled Update maintenance

Major Changes

1. In battles that transition from the Symbolic to the Shadowed, retrying the Stage will now allow Managers to resume from the Shadowed.
- Managers can proceed to the Team Loadout menu, change their lineup, and then resume the battle in the Shadowed.

2. (The Symbolic) A unique assistive item will now become available after dying to a certain boss unit Main Story Chapter 10 - <Part 3>.
※ After dying to the relevant boss in the Symbolic, speak to the NPC at the Fount to obtain the one-time use item against the said boss.

Mobile Platform Optimization

As announced in our previous [Preliminary Notice: Oct. 8th Mobile Platform Optimization Update], memory usage optimization will be deployed with the Scheduled Update on Oct. 8th.

1. Resources for combat and visual effects of Main Story Chapter 10 - Part 3 will be optimized.
2. Fixed an issue where certain visual resources would carry over to the scenes following unique cinematic and battles
3. Fixed an issue where unnecessary resources were being loaded.

These improvements are expected to reduce the frequency of crashes.

However, even after these improvements, crashes may still occur in certain battles on devices with 4 GB of RAM. We will continue working on further optimizations to provide a more stable gameplay experience on mobile devices.
