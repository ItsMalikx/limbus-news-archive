    Notice: Version 1.36.2 Deployment on Mobile Platforms

    February 1, 2024

    The fix for the Version 1.36.1 issue [game soft-locking in certain Focused Encounters] has been completed, and the review process for the soft-locking issue fix build (1.36.2) has been completed. The fix will be deployed on each respective app store on Feb. 2nd, 2024, at 12:00 (KST).

    [Fix Deployment ETA]

    - iOS, Android : 2024.02.02 (Fri) 12:00 (KST)
    - Steam version of the game was updated on 2024.02.02 (Fri) 01:30 (KST)

    There will be no maintenance for this update, and you can download the new version of the app via the Google Play Store or the App Store.

    Please note that update downloads may become available at different periods depending on your location & device. If you cannot see the update in your stores, please try again later.

    The previous version 1.36.1 will remain playable until 2024.02.02 (Fri) 18:00 (KST). Please make sure to update the game before then.

    Once the game has been updated, the bottom right side of the Title screen should say 'Version 1.36.2'.

    We apologize for the inconvenience.
