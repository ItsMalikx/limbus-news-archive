New Content

1. In-combat Speech Bubble option added
An option to view speech bubbles of allied Sinners' voicelines will be added.

※ This option can be turned on or off via Settings - General - In-Combat Dialogue Subtitles "On/Off"

Major Changes

※ The following function was updated with the May 28th, 2026 Scheduled Update. However, as it was missing from the respective Scheduled Update Notice, it has been included in this notice.

1. Luxcavation Consecutive Battle UI has been updated.
- In-game UI has been changed to allow for easier user interaction for setting the number of consecutive battles between 1~10.
- The number of consecutive battles can now be adjusted by increments of 1. Press + to increase it by 1, and - to reduce it by 1.
- Select the number at the center to input the number of consecutive battles you would like to enter for Luxcavation.

※ Inputting a number greater than 10 will automatically adjust the number to 10.

Bug Fixes & Improvements

▶ Fixed an issue where, when the Keyword "Rising Fever" of "The House of Spiders: The Middle Nursefather Outis" is at 0 Stack, and "Time to Unpack!" cannot be used in that turn and is used in the following turn, "Rising Fever" cannot be gained normally and is instead gained at the next Turn Start
※ Please note that in order to avoid affecting the difficulty of the Refraction Railway, "The House of Spiders: The Middle Nursefather Outis Peccatulum Invidiae" will still gain "Rising Fever" at Turn Start.

▶ Fixed an issue where the description of an effect regarding The House of Spiders: The Middle Apprentice Ishmael was missing from the "String Severence" Keyword of the "Saikai" Passive effect of The Blade of the House of Spiders Ryōshū
- Before: Inflict 1 additional Burn Potency using Base Skills
- After: "Defense Level +2" and inflict 1 additional Burn Potency using Base Skills
※ Please note that The Blade of the House of Spiders Ryōshū's Passive, "Saikai", is already functioning as intended. Only the text omission will be fixed.

▶ Fixed an issue where, when E.G.O Corrosion occurs at the timing when a Skill Slot is increased by the "Saikai" Passive effect of The Blade of the House of Spiders Ryōshū, the E.G.O Corrosion-related Skill generated in that Skill Slot would remain even on the turn after E.G.O Corrosion ended

▶ Fixed Skill timing for "Inevitable Termination" triggered by "Spacetime Afterimage Collapse" Passive effect of The Blade of the House of Spiders Ryōshū
- Before: When Clashing with a target faster than this Identity, "Inevitable Termination" would activate after using a Base Attack Skill
- After: "Inevitable Termination" can now be used before using a Base Attack Skill, matching the text

▶ Fixed an issue where related effects would remain on the screen if The House of Spiders: The Index Nursefather Yi Sang dies while possessing the Skill "Furioso-Replica" on its Dashboard

▶ Fixed an issue where a certain cutscene would not occur during the progression of Chapter 8.5 "Spring Cultivation"

▶ Fixed an issue where some Standing Illustrations would not be displayed in the cutscene after clearing a Stage in Main Story Chapter 9

▶ Fixed an issue where a certain background music would not play during Stage 8.5-4

▶ Fixed issues that occurred in Refraction Railway Line 6
※ Relevant Issue 1: Fixed an issue where, in Refraction Railway Line 6, allied units that were defeated in Section 1 and are not judged as being in a defeated state, even though they are unable to be deployed in Section 5
※ Relevant Issue 2: Fixed a cutscene that occurs when a certain unit is defeated in Section 2, matching the intended behavior
※ Relevant Issue 3: Fixed an issue where a unit summoned in Section 2 could remain in the next Wave
※ Relevant Issue 4: Fixed an issue where the description text of the pop-up UI displayed when battle entry was unavailable, such as due to selecting an Identity deployed in the previous Section, was not updated from its Refraction Railway Line 5 version

▶ Fixed an issue in the Mirror Dungeon EXTREME Theme Pack "Efflorescing Greenery" where the boss unit appearing in Wave 3 would also Stagger allies who are needed to perform the mechanic at the start of a certain pattern

▶ Fixed an issue in Wave 1 of the Mirror Dungeon EXTREME Theme Pack "Code Purple" where the number of defeated units would remain in the UI displaying the number of enemy units appearing in the next Wave

▶ Fixed an issue where the damage statistics screen, viewable from the results window after combat ends, would display values higher than the actual amount of damage dealt during battle
※ This issue occurred because, with the Nov. 6th, 2025 Scheduled Update, internal system optimization was applied to the damage calculation method for damage caused by buffs. However, this optimization had also resulted in an issue where damage from buff effects was recorded multiple times. With this fix, damage caused by buffs will no longer be recorded multiple times in the statistics logic.

▶ Adjusted the floors on which certain Theme Packs appear in the Mirror Dungeon Hard Difficulty of the Mirror of Names and Spiders
※ Relevant Theme Packs: LCB Regular Check-up, Nocturnal Sweeping
- Before: Floor 5
- After: Floors 4 and 5
※ Please note the adjustment is limited only to the number of floors in Hard Difficulty where the said Theme Pack may appear.

▶ (Steam) Fixed an issue where the Settings UI for Settings - Graphics - Resolution would not function properly during combat
