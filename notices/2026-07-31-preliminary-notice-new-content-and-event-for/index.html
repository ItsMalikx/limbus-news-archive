    Preliminary Notice: New Content and Event for 2026.08.20 (KST)

    July 31, 2026
    EventsMain StoryNew Content

    Hello, this is Project Moon.

    We sincerely apologize for postponing Chapter 10's release date to September 17 and for failing to keep to the update schedule announced in the roadmap.

    As we have announced in our last notice regarding this matter, we will be releasing new content so that Managers may enjoy the game while waiting for the next Main Story Chapter. Starting from August 20, Reflectrial][lairtcelteR - Lei Heng and a new Attendance Check event will be added to the game.

    ※ This image is for demonstration purposes only. Certain elements may be different in the final release.

    "Reflectrial][lairtcelteR - Lei Heng" is permanent content, and will become accessible after clearing all Stages of Main Story Chapter 8. Rewards include Decorative Tickets, Banners, Threadskins, upgrade currencies, and Free Lunacy.

    In addition, Managers who clear "Reflectrial][lairtcelteR - Lei Heng" before the September 17 Scheduled Update will also be given a Nominable Announcer Ticket [Yisang Exchange], a newly added item.

    Use the "Nominable Announcer Ticket [Yisang Exchange]" to obtain an announcer of your choice that is currently available in the Yisang Exchange (excluding Walpurgis Night Announcers).

    Forthcoming Announcers, such as the upcoming House of Spiders Announcer pairs and other new Announcers, will also be added to the nominable list, which makes them obtainable by using the "Nominable Announcer Ticket [Yisang Exchange]."

    The following is the list of obtainable Announcers using the "Nominable Announcer Ticket [Yisang Exchange]" as of July 31, 2026:

    List of obtainable Announcers (As of Jul. 31) : Aeng-du, Jun, Ricardo, T Corp. Temp Special Investigators, Jia Xichun/Wei, Lei Heng, Kira, Bamboo-hatted Kim/Aeng-du, Matthias/Kira

    ※ Please note that the nominable list does not include Event Announcers, Announcers no longer available for purchase, Announcers currently available in the Dispenser, and Walpurgis Night Announcers.

    Additional information on the "Nominable Announcer Ticket [Yisang Exchange]" will be announced via a separate notice on August 3, when a new Announcer will be added to the game.

    ※ This image is for demonstration purposes only. Certain elements may be different in the final release.

    The Attendance Check event is set to last for 4 weeks, starting from the August 20 Scheduled Update. 2 weeks of attendance will yield a series of rewards, including the Nominable Announcer Ticket [Yisang Exchange].

    - Managers who clear "Reflectrial][lairtcelteR - Lei Heng" before the September 17 Scheduled Update will be able to obtain a total of 2 Nominable Announcer Ticket [Yisang Exchange] from these events.

    Additional information on "Reflectrial][lairtcelteR - Lei Heng" and the Attendance Event, which will open on August 20, will be announced via a separate notice on August 17.

    Once again, we sincerely apologize for the delay in Chapter 10's release schedule and for the disappointment we have caused to the Managers who have been waiting for the next Main Story.

    Thank you.
