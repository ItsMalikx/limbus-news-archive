    Notice: Issues After the 7/9 Update and Concurrent Player Milestone Reward

    July 9, 2026
    Rewards

    Hello, this is Project Moon.

    As mentioned in our previous notice, we would like to provide additional information regarding the error pop-ups occurring during gameplay, as well as other known issues that have since been confirmed.

    Please refer below for more information.

    [Error Pop-up Issue]

    1. Issue Cause
    - Although our servers had been prepared to handle traffic up to approximately 150% of the previous maximum capacity,
    - A sudden influx of traffic exceeded the limit and caused temporary connection issues.

    2. Measures Taken
    - To reduce server load, we have gradually applied temporary limits to incoming requests as an emergency measure.
    - Game service has now stabilized and should be available without issue.
    - We will continue improving our server and traffic-handling capacity to handle sudden request spikes so that the game remains serviceable even when the traffic exceeds our expectations.

    [Notice: Known Issues after Jul. 9th Scheduled Update]

    ▶ (EN Only) An issue where the "Dawn Office Fixer Faust" Keyword "Loneliness at High Noon" has an incorrect description
    → Relevant effect: On Use: Consume up to 100 Scorchfield and deal +1% damage for every Scorchfield consumed (max 150%)
    ※ The actual effect is "deal +1.5% damage for every Scorchfield consumed (max 150%)", and the Keyword is behaving as intended. Only the description is incorrect.

    ▶ An issue where a certain effect on the E.G.O Gift "Wing-shaped Waxen Candle" would activate even when the conditions are not met
    → Relevant effect: Combat Start: Dawn Office Fixer Sinclair heals 10 SP

    ▶ An issue where "Solemn Lament Yi Sang" E.G.O Awakening Skill's SFX would not stop playing even when all the enemies are defeated mid-skill cutscene

    ▶ (KR Only) Typos in certain Identities' flavor texts
    → Relevant Identities and effects
    1. "Dawn Office Rep Gregor" Keyword "From Dawnbreak to Sunset"
    2. "Dawn Office Fixer Faust" Passive "Heatproof Coat"

    ▶ An issue where, if the user forfeits a stage simultaneously as it ends, the selected Announcers' defeat voicelines would play over one another
