Season Update (Part 1) Information

1. Season 7 Egoshard Carryover
Half of Season 7 Egoshard-related currencies held by Managers will be carried over into Season 8. Egoshard-related currencies that are not carried over will be converted into their corresponding Thread-related currencies.
※ When "Season 7 Uptie & Threadspinning only Shard (Universal)" obtainable through the "Projection Rewards" on "Mirror Projection / Achievements" is claimed, half of the claimed amount will be carried over into Season 8, while the remainder will be converted into Thread (rounded up).
※ If the Season 7 currency obtainable from Refraction Railway Line 6 is claimed in Season 8, half of that currency will be carried over to Season 8. Currencies that are not carried over will be converted to Thread-related currencies (rounded up).

2. Progressive Main Story and Pass Updates
Main Story Chapter 10 - Part 1 and Season 8 Limbus Pass - [PUNCTUM] (Part 1) will be added to the game.

3. Accessing Main Story Chapter 10
▶ Main Story Chapter 10, "The Gaze Bearing", can be accessed immediately after clearing Main Story Chapter 1, "The Outcast".
▶ The following measures will be applied to ensure that all Managers can progress smoothly through Chapter 10.
- Certain Meursault Identities will be available as Support Identities. These Identities will be set to Uptie Tier 4, with their E.G.O set to Threadspin Tier 4.
- Managers can borrow Identities set by a friend. However, you can select only one between Meursault Support Identity and an Identity borrowed from a friend.
- Identities below Uptie Tier 3 will be adjusted to Uptie Tier 3. E.G.O will not receive any corresponding adjustment.
▶ Chapter 10 continues the story following Chapter 9.5-2, "Mnestic Experience". For the smoothest experience, we recommend progressing through all preceding Stories beforehand.
▶ Clearing Main Story Chapter 10 will not automatically mark any preceding Stories that have not yet been completed as cleared. Please clear every Stage through Chapter 9.5-2, "Mnestic Experience".
- Contents whose unlock requirements have not been fulfilled, including Mirror Dungeons, Refraction Railway, Luxcavations, and Reflectrial][lairtcelfeR, will remain unavailable.
- Future content added with the completion of Main Story Chapter 10 as its unlock condition will likewise remain unavailable.

4. New EXP Luxcavation
Clearing a certain stage in Chapter 10 - Part 1 will unlock EXP Luxcavation #10.

※ Details
EXP Luxcavation #10 has separate, rotating Slash, Pierce, and Blunt Stages. They each open on the following days:
- Slash: Mon, Tue, Sun
- Pierce: Wed, Thu, Sun
- Blunt: Fri, Sat, Sun

Each Attack Type Stage has first-time clear rewards, and clearing each Attack Type unlocks the Encounter Skip option for the said Attack Type Stage.

Please note that EXP Luxcavation #10 will unlock only after clearing every Stage through Chapter 9.5-2, "Mnestic Experience", as well as a certain Stage in Main Story Chapter 10 - Part 1.

Major Changes

1. The Stage Continue function will be expanded to support Encounter Stages in Main Story Chapter 10.
※ EX Clear can still be achieved with Stage Continue in certain Main Story Chapter 10 Stages.

※ However, once Stage Continue has been used and progress has been saved after winning the ensuing Combat Encounter without retrying, losing, or forfeiting, the resources spent on Stage Continue will not be returned even if that Stage is subsequently abandoned. Additionally, any Lunacy spent on Stage Continue will not be eligible for refund.

Bug Fixes & Improvements

▶ (STEAM) Improved the UI so that pressing the ESC key during Story progression will display the Skip UI without requiring the user to open a separate Menu UI

▶ Added a feature that allows Managers to view the previous or next Identity or unit from the Identity Info UI using the left and right arrow buttons, the directional keys, or by swiping across the illustration area
※ This feature is available from the Identity Deployment screen and the in-combat Information UI.

▶ Fixed an issue where, while progressing through a Dungeon of the Fathoms with Dark Mode enabled for the UI, text in the UI displayed after all allied Sinners were defeated would not be displayed correctly
