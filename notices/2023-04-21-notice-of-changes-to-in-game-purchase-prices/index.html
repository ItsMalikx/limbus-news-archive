    April 21, 2023
    BalanceRewards

    Greetings, Dear Manager.

    In accordance with the global price adjustments of the App Store, the prices of in-game purchases will change with the May 4th Scheduled Update as listed in the table below.

    APlease note that USD prices will remain the same, as updates to prices on the App Store are based on the current U.S. prices.

    The contents of the packs remain the same, and the changed prices apply for all platforms (Google Play, App Store, Steam).

    Because <Limbus Company> provides its services through the same version for all regions, please understand that prices for in-game purchases may change again in the future due to (USD-based) store policies.

    The changed prices will apply at 12:00 on May 4th (KST) after the scheduled update.
    We will let you know if the prices change again before this adjustment is applied to the live service.
