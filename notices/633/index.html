Major Changes

1. Added a feature that allows Managers to manually select the CG illustrations displayed on loading screens.

- This feature can be accessed by selecting the "Custom Settings Button (circling arrows button)" - "Set Loading Art" in the upper-right corner of the Window screen.

- Managers can select up to 10 Identity or Story illustrations, or configure the game to display all illustrations at random.

- The unlock conditions for each type of CG illustration are as follows.
(1) Story Illustrations: Unlocked upon clearing the final Stage of the corresponding Story Chapter
(2) Identity Illustrations: Unlocked upon first obtaining the Identity and upon reaching Uptie Tier 3

※ CG Illustrations from collaboration events are unavailable regardless of unlocking and finishing the corresponding chapter.

2. Added a feature that allows Managers to delete or redownload Story voice data.

- Story voice data can be deleted or redownloaded under "Settings" - "Audio".

- The voice data for a Story can be deleted or redownloaded after clearing that Story's final Stage.

- Deleting Story voice data will free an amount of storage space corresponding to the size of the data.

※ As of the Sep. 3rd Scheduled Update, this feature will support only the voiced Main Stories from "Prologue: Selva Oscura" to "Chapter 9: The Unsevering." Deleting all supported Story voice data will free approximately 2 GB of storage space.

※ Please note that support for managing the voice data of Mini Episodes will be added in a future update.
