"4th Match Flame Ryōshū" E.G.O Threadspinning Tier 5 Update

An additional Threadspinning Tier will be added, allowing the "4th Match Flame Ryōshū" E.G.O to be enhanced up to Tier 5.

[Required Currency]

Threadspinning Tier 5 requires "Spinchains", a Threadspinning-exclusive currency.

"Spinchains" can be crafted using each Sinner's Egoshards, Uptie & Threadspinning-only Shards, and Threads.

"Spinchains" can be crafted at a lower cost by using the Egoshards of the Sinner corresponding to the E.G.O.

[Example: Spinchains Crafting Ratio for "4th Match Flame Ryōshū"]

Ryōshū's EgoshardsSpinchains = 1 : 1
Uptie & Threadspinning-only ShardsSpinchains = 1 : 1
Other Sinners' EgoshardsSpinchains = 2 : 1
ThreadsSpinchains = 2 : 1

※ "Spinchains" craftable from the Threadspinning screen is a separate currency and will not be accumulated in the Inventory.

※ Please note that the amount of "Spinchains" used will differ depending on the E.G.O's Threadspinning Tier.

※ If you do not meet the required amount of "Spinchains" needed for Threadspinning Tier 5, you will not be able to proceed with the Threadspinning.

※ Tier 5 Threadspinning will be added to the other E.G.O gradually.

Issue Fix for a Certain Effect in "Reflectrial][lairtcelfeR" that were Not Functioning as Intended

An issue has been identified where a certain effect among the passives held by the boss unit in "Reflectrial][lairtcelfeR" was functioning differently from its text.

※ Relevant Effect : At 10+ Clashes against the same enemy, randomly gain or lose Clash Power by (Clash Count/10) in a Clash (rounded down)

- Before : An issue was identified where Clash Power would be gained only, by (Clash Count/10)

- After : Fixed the issue so that, Clash Power can now be gained or lost by (Clash Count/10), matching the description

※ If this effect were to remain as-is, the boss unit would always win Clashes as the number of Clashes increases.

This issue will be fixed to alleviate the unfairness in combat progression.

We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience this error has caused to your content progression.

Restoration Regarding an Issue Where, in the Nocturnal Sweeping BokGak Reward Exchange Menu, a Certain Reward Item is Shown and Exchanged in Incorrect Numbers and Rates

From Apr. 30, 2026, 12:00 to 14:03 (KST), an issue was identified where the "[Season 7] Egoshard Crate" in the Nocturnal Sweeping BokGak Reward Exchange screen granted 1 Crate instead of 5. This issue has since been fixed.

Based on our records, Managers who exchanged for the aforementioned item during the affected period will receive the missing amount through the Mailbox with the May 14 Scheduled Update (Mail available for 1 year).

※ Claiming the missing amount after a new Season begins will carry it over at a reduced amount according to the number of Seasons that have passed.
Please note that any currency not carried over will be converted into Thread Crates.
