2025, Mar. 27th Scheduled Update Notice

March 24, 2025

Greetings, Dear Manager.

The server will enter scheduled maintenance from 10:00 [AM] through 12:00 [PM] on Mar. 27th, 2025 (KST).

Please make note of the maintenance schedule; we recommend that you refrain from entering combat or making in-app purchases and close the application before maintenance begins so that no data is lost.

Please refer below for details about the update.

Major Changes

1. (Steam) Added a [Restore Purchase] button in the Paid Items Shop pop-up window

If a transaction failure occurs due to a Steam error, you can use this button to re-attempt to receive the item.
※ Purchase restoration time may vary depending on the user.

Bug Fixes & Improvements

► Fixed an issue where, in certain situations during the Mirror Dungeon run, Achievements could not be completed
※ At Floor 6 or higher in the Mirror Dungeon, win an Encounter after every ally except for one is dead (including Backup/Support Identities)
- Before: this Achievement could not be completed in Encounters triggered via Choice Events
- After: this Achievement can now be completed in Encounters triggered via Choice Events
※ Raise 1 enemy's Tremor Potency to 99 (in Focused Encounters, a Part)
- Before: this Achievement could not be completed if the Tremor type was changed due to Amplitude Conversion
- After: this Achievement can now be completed even if the Tremor type was changed due to Amplitude Conversion

► (EN, JP Only) Fixed an issue where the Unlock Conditions text for Theater - Detour Tales - 'Nocturnal Sweeing' were missing

► Improved Mirror Dungeon Shop E.G.O Gift list arrangement UI

► Fixed an issue where Coin effects texts did not load properly when using Unbreakable Coins that cracked after losing a clash

► Fixed an issue where the E.G.O Gifts in possession list UI partially did not load properly in Main Story Dungeon Stages

► Fixed an issue where the total damage counter for 2 or more Coins Corroded E.G.O Skill use scenes counted only part of the actual damage dealt
※ This is a visual-only issue; the actual damage output is not changed.

► Addressed the issue where a boss enemy in Ch. 7.5 - 'LCB Regular Check-up' could load an incorrect Sprite

► Fixed an issue where the spotlight animations in Special Banner - LCB Regular Check-up loaded in incorrect places

Bug Fixes & Improvements

► Partially adjusted a line from Yi Sang in Stage 7.5-24 of Nocturnal Sweeping
- Before: There is an apt saying, oft invoked in my homeland. A fox may pretend to the throne—but only with the blessing of the tiger.
- After: There is an apt saying, oft invoked in the east. A fox may pretend to the throne—but only with the blessing of the tiger.

► Fixed an issue where a part of The Barber of La Manchaland Outis' Support Passive was not working as intended
※ Relevant effect: 1 ally with the fastest Speed: if the enemy is killed After Attack, heal 10 HP (2 times per turn)
- Before: this effect actually activated three times per turn
- After: changed the effect to match the description - now it activates 2 times per turn, as written

► (EN Only) Fixed an issue where certain Keywords in Seven South Section 4 Faust's Combat Passives were replaced with Unknown

► (KR Only) Addressed the issue where Keyword highlights in Skill/Passive descriptions of certain Identities and E.G.O were missing

► Adjusted E.G.O Gift 'Glyph of Glass Shards'
- Description: When clashing against targets with Deathrite[Haste], gain (Deathrite[Haste] on the target / 10) Coin Power instead (max 2)
- Actual effects: When clashing against targets with Deathrite[Haste], gain (Rupture on the target / 10) Clash Power instead (max 3)
※ The actual effects did not match the description, and since the actual effects were not the intended effects either, the E.G.O Gift will be changed as follows:
- New effects: When clashing against targets with Deathrite[Haste], gain (Rupture on the target / 10) Coin Power instead (max 2)

Maintenance & Application Update Compensation

- Content: Lunacy x300
- Recipients: all users whose game accounts were created before 10:00 Apr. 3rd, 2025 (KST)
- When: After Mar. 27th (Thu) 2025, 12:00 (KST)
- Can be claimed once per account

Maintenance Compensation can be claimed until Apr. 3rd (Thu) 2025, 10:00 (KST) via the in-game Mailbox. (Mail available for 7 days)

Notes

- Please update your game client to the newest version on the respective store (Google Play/App Store/Steam).
- Forcibly shutting down the game while it is downloading resources may cause the download to fail, so please be careful not to close the application during the download.
- You will not be able to play <Limbus Company> during maintenance. Please understand.
- The maintenance schedule may suddenly change depending on the status of maintenance. If such a change occurs, we will let you know in a follow-up notice.
- A resource download may be prompted after the build update. If you're using a mobile device, it is recommended to proceed with a Wi-Fi connection.
- If you're playing with a guest account, we recommend that you link your account with other platforms to prevent possible account loss.
- The maintenance compensation will be provided once per account. Please note that the distribution may be slow due to network delays or other causes.
- The update gifts are valid to claim for 7 days. Please redeem them within this timeframe.

Thank you.
