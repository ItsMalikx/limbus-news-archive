Major Changes

1. Added a fast-forward speed setting for E.G.O animations
The fast-forward speed activated by clicking or tapping during an E.G.O animation can now be selected in the settings menu.
- Available speeds: x2.0, x2.5, x3.0 (default: x2.0)

※ Previously, fast-forward played at x2.0 speed on PC and x3.0 speed on mobile.
After the update, the selected speed will apply on both platforms.
※ The sound effects in certain E.G.O animations will also play at the selected speed.

2. Added a Synergy Effect to "4th Match Flame" E.G.O

A Synergy effect will be added to the following E.G.O:
- Relevant E.G.O: 4th Match Flame Yi Sang, 4th Match Flame Ryōshū, 4th Match Flame Rodion

※ Activation conditions for Synergy effects may vary for each E.G.O.
※ Synergy effects will also be added to existing E.G.O with Threadspin Tier 5 in future updates.

Bug Fixes & Improvements

▶ Revised how the activation conditions for the Passive and Support Passive of "The Thumb East Soldato II Sinclair" are checked
- Before: Identities that have Unique Ammo types with the effect "This Ammo's count cannot be gained or lost due to external effects" were also counted
- After: Only Identities that use "Ammo" that can be gained or increased due to external effects will be counted
※ Previously, Identities with special Ammo whose Stacks could not increase were counted, even though their Ammo did not actually increase. As a result, the once-per-Encounter limit from Sinclair's Passives was wasted. This issue will be corrected with this update.

▶ Fixed an issue where the "Nth Magic Bullet" effect gained from using the "Magic Bullet Outis" E.G.O would also be applied to "Assume Wedge Formation" used by "Wedge Office Rep Outis" at Attack End
※ This effect will now apply only to the "Magic Bullet Outis" E.G.O Skill.

▶ Fixed an issue where "Unknown" would appear in the Skill information window for the "Move-in Reg. Gregor" E.G.O

▶ Fixed an issue where text and dates would appear incorrectly in the chapter UI for Main Story Chapter 10 stages if 9.5-2 "Mnestic Experience" had not been cleared

▶ Corrected the text of two choices in the Mirror Dungeon Theme Pack "The Wedge in the Wake of Dawnbreak"
- Before: Gain a Keywordless E.G.O Gift
- After: Gain a Pierce E.G.O Gift

▶ Corrected the text of the E.G.O Gift "Sword Laced with Grief"
- Before: Charge
- After: Deep Tears
※ The effect was functioning normally; only the text will be corrected.

▶ Fixed an issue where the Coin UI for certain Skills would behave abnormally during Combat

▶ Fixed an issue where the image displayed when hovering over the first banner on the user information card in the Window would not appear

▶ (EN Only) Updated the wording for "Keyword Identities," such as "Charge Identities"
- Before: Keyword Identity (e.g., Charge Identity)
- After: Keyword ID (e.g., Charge ID)
※ The effects will not change; this is a text-only change for readability and consistency with plural forms.
