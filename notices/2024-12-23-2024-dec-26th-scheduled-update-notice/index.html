2024, Dec. 26th Scheduled Update Notice

December 23, 2024
Identities & E.G.ONew ContentBalanceRewards

Greetings, Dear Manager.

The server will enter scheduled maintenance from 10:00 [AM] through 12:00 [PM] on Dec. 26th, 2024 (KST).

Please make note of the maintenance schedule; we recommend that you refrain from entering combat or making in-app purchases and close the application before maintenance begins so that no data is lost.

Please refer below for details about the update.

Bug Fixes & Improvements

▶ Fixed an issue where Dawn Office Fixer Sinclair's Defense Skill 'Rue' did not work as written in its description
※ Relevant Effect : When in an Unstable E.G.O state, Base Power +M for every N Burn on target (max L)
- Before: This effect also activated when the unit was not in an Unstable E.G.O state.
- After: Adjusted so that the effect activates only when the unit is in an Unstable E.G.O state, to match the description

▶ Fixed an issue where the damage indicator for the damages dealt by the third Coin of The Manager of La Manchaland Don Quixote's Empowered Skill 3 'Ascendant Sancho Hardblood Arts - La Sangre' would show 0 damage dealt

▶ Addressed the issue where, after using E.G.O Skills with 2 or more Coins, the camera would not reset properly after the animation is over, causing the affected characters to appear as though they are leaning to the side

▶ Fixed an issue where some effects of the E.G.O Gift 'Cask Spirits' did not activate
※ Relevant Effect: Turn End: at more than 40 Poise Potency, consume 15 Poise Potency to Discard 1 Skill of the lowest rank in all of that unit's Skill Slots (once per turn for each character)

▶ Fixed an issue where the game softlocks in a particular circumstance where 1) the player owns an E.G.O Gift that activate only for allies that meet its conditionals, but 2) the ally that meets its conditionals does not exist at Turn Start

▶ Fixed an issue where units could get 'Charmed' by Mirror Dungeon Abnormality 'Faelantern' with high Speeds due to E.G.O Gifts with effects that modify their Speed at Turn Start

▶ Fixed an issue where gaining an E.G.O Gift in the first Choice Event Node after entering the Mirror Dungeon does not show the 'E.G.O Gift GET!" window

▶ Fixed an issue where a passive effect of the boss of Intervallo III - Miracle in District 20, 'I CAN'T BAG THE GIFTS!!' does not activate on certain Parts

▶ Adjusted the icon of the item 'Takeoff Module - E.G.O Spinning [WAW]
※ The icon has been adjusted to reflect the Tier of the E.G.O that can make use of this item.

Notes

- Please update your game client to the newest version on the respective store (Google Play/App Store/Steam).

- You will not be able to play <Limbus Company> during maintenance. Please understand.
- The maintenance schedule may suddenly change depending on the status of maintenance. If such a change occurs, we will let you know in a follow-up notice.
- A resource download may be prompted after the build update. If you're using a mobile device, it is recommended to proceed with a Wi-Fi connection.
- If you're playing with a guest account, we recommend that you link your account with other platforms to prevent possible account loss.
- The maintenance compensation will be provided once per account. Please note that the distribution may be slow due to network delays or other causes.
- The update gifts are valid to claim for 7 days. Please redeem them within this timeframe.
