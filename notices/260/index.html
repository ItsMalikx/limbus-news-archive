July 31st New Content - Ch.6.5 Timekilling Time BokGak

2025-07-28

-----------------------------------------------------

Greetings, Dear Manager.

With the July 31st 2025 (KST) Update, new content [Ch 6.5 - Timekilling Time BokGak] will be added to the game.

- Duration : 2025/7/31 12:00 (KST) ~ Before 8/28 (KST) Scheduled Update

- Event Rewards Exchange : 2025/7/31 12:00 (KST) ~ Before 9/4 (KST) Scheduled Update

Requirements
------------

- Timekilling Time Event Banner: Clear Main Story 0-4 (Tutorial)
- Timekilling Time (Normal Difficulty): Clear Main Story 6-48
- Timekilling Time (Hard Difficulty): Clear Main Story 7-36

Description
-----------

- When the event starts, the [Ch. 6.5 - Timekilling Time BokGak] Event Page will open.

- During the event period, you can earn [Pocket Watch] by clearing the Timekilling Time BokGak Event Stages & the Mirror Dungeons.

- Voices will be added to the Timekilling Time story, and you can access the Timekilling Time Hard Difficulty Stages during the event period.

- You can redeem various rewards from the [Reward Exchange] by spending the event currency that you have collected.

Event Gameplay
--------------

► Timekilling Time Story
Voices will be added to the story of Timekilling Time.

► Timekilling Time Combat Stages
You can obtain [Pocket Watch] every time you complete a combat Encounter of ‘Timekilling Time’. Each Encounter has a first-time reward; with it, you can obtain even more [Pocket Watch].
However, if you had previously cleared the Timekilling Time Stages, you cannot obtain additional first-time rewards.

The final Combat Stage of the Timekilling Time event costs an Enkephalin Module.
That final Stage will have a higher Enkephalin-to-[Pocket Watch] ratio, so it will be much more effective as a means of collecting [Pocket Watch].

► Timekilling Time Combat Stages (Hard Difficulty)
Timekilling Time Hard Difficulty will become available during the Event period.
Clearing Main Story Stage 7-36 will unlock the Hard Difficulty.

Earn even more [Pocket Watch] by playing through the Hard Difficulty. You can earn first-time Clear rewards (including the Event currency), EX-Clear rewards, and Chapter Progression by clearing the Hard Difficulty Stages as well.

► Mirror Dungeon
During the Event Period, [Timekilling Time BokGak Theme Pack] will have a fixed appearance on Floor 4~5 of the Mirror Dungeon Hard Difficulty. The said Theme Pack features exclusive Choice Events and E.G.O Gifts.

※ The Preexisting [Timekilling Time Theme Pack] will not be changed.

※ The same Theme Pack will not appear twice in the same run once completed.

※ Timekilling Time Theme Pack will no longer have a fixed appearance once the Event ends, and may appear on different floors.

Clearing a Mirror Dungeon run will net you [Pocket Watch].
Selecting the [Timekilling Time] or [Timekilling Time BokGak] Theme Packs will net you even more [Pocket Watch].
The Theme Pack in the Hard Difficulty yields much more Event currency than the Normal Difficulty.

※ Please note that the Weekly Bonus does not affect the amount of Event currency gained.

※ Please note that, if you have started a Mirror Dungeon run before the event started, you will not gain event currency from that run.

► Main Story
Managers who have not met the Event unlock conditions can obtain [Pocket Watch] as a first-time reward from Main Story Combat Encounters.

Upon your first time clearing an Encounter, you can obtain [Pocket Watch] proportional to the amount of Enkephalin Spent, regardless of whether you’ve met the EX Clear Conditions or not.

※ You cannot obtain the event currency from stages that have already been completed.

※ You cannot obtain [Pocket Watch] from Luxcavations or the Refraction Railway.

Event Currency
--------------

In rare cases, clearing the ‘Timekilling Time’ Stages, the Mirror Dungeon, and the Main Story Stages will also yield [Pocket Watch Bundle].

[Pocket Watch Bundle] has the highest chance of dropping from the final Stage of the Timekilling Time BokGak Event.
However, the drop chance is the same across all other Encounters.

[Pocket Watch Bundle] is automatically converted to 20 [Pocket Watch] in the Results or the Rewards screen upon acquisition.

Event Bonus Units
-----------------

Obtain more [Pocket Watch] by completing Event Encounters or the Mirror Dungeon while equipped with Event Bonus E.G.Os.

<Event Bonus E.G.Os>

► 40% Bonus
- E.G.O [WAW] : Crushbound Past Meursault, Everlasting Faust
- 3★ : T Corp. Class 3 VDCU Staff Outis, District 20 Yurodivy Hong Lu, T Corp. Class 3 Collection Staff Don Quixote

► 20% Bonus
- 2★ : District 20 Yurodivy Ryōshū, T Corp. Class 2 Collection Staff Rodion

※ The Event Currency Bonus from each Unit is additive. (Rounded up)

※ Event Currency Bonus maxes out at 120% Bonus.

※ Event Bonus Units don’t actually have to participate in a battle; you can earn the bonus currency simply by having them in your Team loadout.

※ Event Currency Bonus applies to first-time clear rewards and Mirror Dungeon clear rewards as well.

Event Rewards
-------------

At the [Rewards Exchange], you can spend [Pocket Watch] you’ve collected to purchase various rewards.

You may find the rewards from the previous Timekilling Time (Ticket Deco, cosmetic Banners, 1 Event Reward Identity) here.

In addition, a new Timekilling Time Announcer will be added to the list of redeemable rewards.

※ You can no longer obtain [Pocket Watch] after you obtain enough [Pocket Watch] to purchase every item in the [Reward Exchange].

※ You cannot obtain duplicates of special rewards (Ticket Deco, cosmetic Banners, 1 Event Reward Identity) if you already own them.

※ [Pocket Watch] will no longer be obtainable once the Event closes after the 2025/08/26 (KST) Scheduled Update.

※ [Reward Exchange] will be open for 5 weeks, from 2025/07/31 12:00 (KST) ~ 2025/9/4 (KST) Scheduled Update.

※ Even after the event fully ends, you will be able to experience the Timekilling Time Combat Encounters or view its story anytime at Drive and Theater - [Detour Tales].
