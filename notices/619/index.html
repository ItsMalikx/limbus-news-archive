2026, Jul. 2nd Scheduled Update Notice

2026-07-01

--------------------------------------

Greetings, Dear Manager.

The server will enter scheduled maintenance from 10:00 [AM] through 12:00 [PM] on Jul. 2nd, 2026 (KST).

Please make note of the maintenance schedule; we recommend that you refrain from entering combat or making in-app purchases and close the application before maintenance begins so that no data is lost.

Please refer below for details about the update.

Bug Fixes & Improvements
------------------------

► Additional fix for crashes upon entering battles on certain devices
※ A verified plugin for GPU driver stabilization on AOS devices has been implemented, improving the per-device Graphics API selection logic.

► Fixed an issue where, in the Mirror Dungeon, when the user possesses a certain E.G.O Gift and "The House of Spiders: The Thumb Apprentice Heathcliff" Substitutes in while "The House of Spiders: The Thumb Nursefather Rodion" is on the field, the game would become unresponsive
→ Relevant E.G.O Gifts
Sanguine Fragrance Descends; Cleanup Agent Gear Set C; Bloody Flesh, Fleshy Blood; Hardblood Glaive; Lightning Axe; Flower in the Mirror; Moon in the Water; The End of all Evil; Desperado; Sorrowful Exhale; Raincloud; Uniform - Liu Assoc.; Carousel Figurine

► Fixed an issue where certain effects of the "Shadow-vested Bladesinger [蒼影揮刀] Meursault" E.G.O would not trigger
→ Relevant Effect : On Hit with a Base Skill, inflict 2 Bleed on target (once per Skill)
※ The effect in question was working as intended upon the E.G.O's release on Jun. 11th, but has been identified to have stopped working after the Jun. 25th Scheduled Update. This issue will be resolved with this update.

► Adjusted Blade of the House of Spiders Ryōshū's Passive "Saikai [再会]" so that a certain effect does not activate in certain additional Encounters
→ Relevant Effect: If any of The House of Spiders allied Identities are on the field, transfer HP damage taken to The House of Spiders Identity that has the lowest current HP percentage

► Fixed an issue where some resources for "The Thumb" Identities are implemented unnaturally
→ Relevant Identities : The House of Spiders: The Thumb Apprentice Heathcliff, The House of Spiders: The Thumb Nursefather Rodion, The Thumb East Soldato II Sinclair
※ The unnaturally implemented resources refer to a certain design pattern on the currently released "The Thumb" Identities' Illustrations and SD resources, and will be fixed.

► Fixed an issue where, when The House of Spiders: The Middle Nursefather Outis has become immobilized in specific circumstances due to effects such as forced Stagger, the Identity's SD resources would not load properly

► (EN Only) Fixed an issue where a certain part of The House of Spiders: The Middle Nursefather Outis's "Afternoon Greeting" voiceline was incorrectly translated
- Before : Hm... Got the fixer mag and comic books she asked for, got some fruits— ……
- After : Hm... Got the Fixer mag and comic books Kiddo asked for, and some healthy fruits for my precious buddy— ……

► Fixed an issue where a certain effect of the E.G.O Gift "Hoarfrost Footprint" would not trigger
→ Relevant Effect : When an allied Identity wins a Clash against an affected enemy, inflict 2 Sinking Potency; when an allied Identity is hit by an affected enemy, inflict 3 Sinking Potency.

► Fixed an issue where the Spine-animated Uptie cutscene that appears when upgrading a 3★ Identity to Uptie 3 would not load properly

► Improved the mechanics of items that can instantly upgrade an Identity to Uptie 4 to show the Uptie 3 Spine-animated Uptie cutscene

► Fixed an issue where, in certain story cutscenes, Ryōshū's Standing illustration would appear at incorrect locations

Known Issues
------------

► An issue where the color filter applied to certain Encounters does not work properly when a certain Identity's VFX is activated

Maintenance & Application Update Compensation
---------------------------------------------

- Content : Lunacy x300
- Recipients : All users whose game accounts were created before Jul. 9th, 2026, 10:00 AM (KST)
- When : After Jul. 2nd (Thu), 2026, 12:00 (KST)
- Can be claimed once per account

Relevant Compensation can be claimed until Jul. 9th (Thu), 2026, 10:00 (KST) via the in-game Mailbox (mail available for 7 days).

Notes
-----

- Please update your game client to the newest version on the respective store (Google Play/App Store/Steam).
- Forcibly shutting down the game while it is downloading resources may cause the download to fail, so please be careful not to close the application during the download.
- You will not be able to play <Limbus Company> during maintenance. Please understand.
- The maintenance schedule may suddenly change depending on the status of maintenance. If such a change occurs, we will let you know in a follow-up notice.
- A resource download may be prompted after the build update. If you're using a mobile device, it is recommended to proceed with a Wi-Fi connection.
- If you're playing with a guest account, we recommend that you link your account with other platforms to prevent possible account loss.
- The maintenance compensation will be provided once per account. Please note that the distribution may be slow due to network delays or other causes.
- The update gifts are valid to claim for 7 days. Please redeem them within this timeframe.

Thank you.
