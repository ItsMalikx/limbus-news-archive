[Adjustments to Preexisting E.G.O Gifts]

※ The following changelog is based on the base enhancement tier versions of each E.G.O Gift.
※ Please note that any effects not listed in the following changelog have not been changed.

White Gossypium
- Before : Turn Start: If there are Staggered enemies, un-Stagger enemies. Then, inflict (8 + Stagger LV x 4) Bleed Potency and (8 + Stagger LV x 4)/2 Offense Level Down on them.
- After : Turn Start: If there are Staggered enemies, un-Stagger enemies. Then, inflict (8 + Stagger LV x 4) Bleed Potency and (8 + Stagger LV x 4)/2 Offense Level Down on them this turn and next turn.

Patrolling Flashlight
- Before : 1) Turn Start: if the affected Identity has a Skill that gains or consumes Charge Count or 'Unique Charge', the Identity's Skill 1 gains Clash Power +1 and deals +10% more damage while that unit has Charge Barrier
2) Turn End: if the affected Identity does not have Charge Barrier, apply 2 Charge Barrier next turn
- After : 1) Turn Start: The affected Identity gains 2 Charge Barrier
2) If the affected Identity has a Skill that gains or consumes Charge Count or 'Unique Charge', the Identity's Skill 1 gains Clash Power +1 and deals +10% damage while that unit has Charge Barrier

Dreaming Electric Sheep
- Before : After defeating an enemy by using an Envy Affinity or Slash damage Skill, raise the unit's Slash Skill Power by 2 for the next 2 turns.
- After : If an enemy is killed by Attack End of a Slash Skill or an Envy Skill: For 2 turns, starting next turn, raise the Slash Skill Power by 2 for the unit who used the Skill

Gathering Skulls
- Before : After defeating an enemy by using a Pride Affinity or Pierce damage Skill, raise the unit's Pierce Power by 2 for the next 2 turns.
- After : If an enemy is killed by Attack End of a Pierce Skill or a Pride Skill: For 2 turns, starting next turn, raise the Pierce Skill Power by 2 for the unit who used the Skill

Today's Expression
- Before : After defeating an enemy by using a Sloth Affinity or Blunt skill, raise the unit's Blunt Skill Power by 2 until the end of next turn.
- After : If an enemy is killed by Attack End of a Blunt Skill or a Sloth Skill: For 2 turns, starting next turn, raise the Blunt Skill Power by 2 for the unit who used the Skill

Manor-shaped Music Box
- Removed: Effects apply only to the #1 Deployed Identity
- Before : When defeating an enemy with Sinking, 1 enemy with the least Sinking Count gains +2 Sinking Count
- After : On Kill, 1 enemy that has the least Sinking Count gains +2 Sinking Count

Bug Fixes & Improvements

▶ Updated Lobotomy E.G.O::Lamp Gregor's passive effect "Guardians of the Forest" to no longer apply to Assist Units

▶ Fixed an issue where The House of Spiders: The Index Nursefather Yi Sang's per-Unlock stage VFX were not loading

▶ Fixed an issue where the game would crash under certain circumstances
※ Affected Identities : LCE E.G.O::AEDD Gregor, Lobotomy E.G.O::Red Eyes & Penitence Ryōshū

[Issue Cause]

- At 2+ Gloom or Envy Resonance, LCE E.G.O::AEDD Gregor's Passive effect applies +2 Charge Count next turn on allies with earlier Deployment order than this unit.
- On April 2nd, we deployed a fix for the following issue: "Lobotomy E.G.O::Red Eyes & Penitence Ryōshū's unique keywords "Red Eyes" and "Penitence" are displayed twice each." With this fix, when LCE E.G.O::AEDD Gregor's Passive effect applies to Ryōshū, it applies to the corresponding buff stages instead of on the incorrect stages.
- The issue occurs when Lobotomy E.G.O::Red Eyes & Penitence Ryōshū uses its "Serious Skullbuster" Skill to fully consume all Red Eyes and Penitence stacks, but still gains higher-stage versions of Red Eyes and Penitence Stacks due to LCE E.G.O::AEDD Gregor's Passive effect

[Issue Fix]

- With this fix, even after Lobotomy E.G.O::Red Eyes & Penitence Ryōshū uses its "Serious Skullbuster" Skill, Lobotomy E.G.O::AEDD Gregor's Passive will now apply stage 1 versions of Red Eyes and Penitence buffs, respectively

▶ Adjusted the activation timing of E.G.O Gifts that increase the "Max Charge Count Cap"
※ Relevant E.G.O Gifts : Charge-type Gloves, Hearts-powered Jewel

[Issue Cause]

- The mechanism behind the effect that raises the "Max Charge Count Cap" is an invisible buff added at "Turn Start" to each unit that raised their max Charge Counts.
- This meant that effects that gain Charge Count at "Encounter Start/ Upon entering the Encounter for the first time" gained Charge Counts before the Max Charge Count Cap was raised, meaning that they would hit the Charge Count cap before the cap is expanded by the above effect

[Issue Fix]

- Adjusted the activation timing of effects that increase "Max Charge Count Cap" to trigger before "Encounter Start" or "Upon entering the Encounter for the first time"

▶ Fixed an issue where certain effects of the E.G.O Gift "Pure-white Canvas" did not work
※ Relevant effect: If said Identity or target has 3+ types of negative effects, heal 6 SP instead
※ We have identified an issue where the "If said Identity has 3+ types of negative effects" conditional does not trigger and it will be fixed with this update. Please note that other effects on this E.G.O Gift are functioning normally.

▶ Fixed an issue where the game could become unresponsive when "Retreat" or "Substitution" occurs while fighting Hidden Boss invasions in Mirror Dungeon EXTREME

▶ Fixed an issue where a Mirror Dungeon EXTREME Hidden Boss's Skill does not work as intended
※ Relevant effect : Viewing the Tableau

▶ Fixed an issue where, while using Façade Projection, "play a random animation" timing does not take animations from Skill 2 animation pools

▶ (EN Only) Fixed an issue where, in Stage 9-37 and 9-43, a certain enemy unit had an incorrect Passive description
※ The effects were working as intended; this is a translation-only fix.

▶ Fixed an issue where certain enemies featured in Refraction Railway Line 1 BokGak have Skill effects that did not match their descriptions (updated descriptions to match the behavior)
※ Relevant enemies : Everything There of an Inquisitor, Proceeding Inquisitor
※ Relevant Skills : Adjust Shell
- Before : "[On Hit without Cracking] Heal 10% HP" effect did not trigger.
- After : Removed said description

※ In order to ensure a consistent combat experience, we will be adjusting the description to match its behavior.

▶ Fixed an issue where, in specific gameplay environments, certain combat backgrounds could fail to load

▶ Fixed an issue where, when previewing rewards in the Inbox under certain conditions, the in-game camera could malfunction

▶ Updated the in-battle Mirror Dungeon Adversity effect icon to match the current Mirror Dungeon concept

▶ Fixed an issue where, in the "Mirror Projection / Achievement - Achievements" menu, the Achievement Progress bar was affected by "Hidden" Achievements

▶ Improved in-battle Announcer UI visibility in bright backgrounds
