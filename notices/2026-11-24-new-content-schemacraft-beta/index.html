New Content - Schemacraft Beta

November 24, 2026
Main StoryNew ContentRewards

Greetings, Dear Manager.

With the Nov. 26th, 2026 (KST) Scheduled Update, new content ["Schemacraft Beta"] will be added to the game.

▶ Unlock Conditions Clear all Stages of Chapter 9.5 "Mnestic Experience" and Chapter 10 "The Gaze Bearing"

Description

- With the Nov. 26th Scheduled Update, the "Schemacraft" button on the Drive tab will be activated.

- You may earn Schemacraft Pass EXP based on your progress through each run in Schemacraft Beta.

- "Schemacraft Beta" does not cost any Enkephalin or Enkephalin Modules to play.

※ Playing through "Schemacraft" will not raise your Limbus Pass levels or EXP. Activities within Schemacraft will not count toward clearing Limbus Pass Missions either.

※ The version of "Schemacraft" to be updated on Nov. 26th will be in a playable Beta form and will be consistently adjusted and improved following the initial update.

※ All images featured in this notice are for demonstration purposes only. Final content after the update may look different.

Gameplay

▶ Schemacraft Beta
- Clear all Stages of Chapter 9.5 "Mnestic Experience" and Chapter 10 "The Gaze Bearing" to unlock "Schemacraft Beta".

- Progress through the nodes, recruit Identities and E.G.O, and fight your way through its Encounters using its new auto-battle feature.

- Gain Exclusive Pass EXP based on your Schemacraft progress and earn various rewards by raising the Pass level.

- Once a run has started, select "Pause and Exit" to leave the gameplay mode. You can then choose to either continue that run with the same deck or restart with a new Team.

※ In "Schemacraft" mode, Identities and E.G.O Uptie/Threadspin tiers vary depending on whether you have acquired them outside the mode.

- Identities
Owned: Can upgrade up to Uptie 4
Not Owned: Can upgrade up to Uptie 3
- E.G.O
Owned: Applies your current Threadspin Tier
Not Owned: Applies Threadspin Tier 1

※ Unobtained COLLABORATION - LIMITED E.G.O will not appear.

- Relevant E.G.O → Command : Meltdown Faust, To Remain Oneself [TEE] Hong Lu, Tidal Elegy Ishmael, Unbrilliant Glory Gregor

Content Progression

▶ Starting Team
- Spend the starting Cost to select up to 4 Sinners and their Identities as your Starting Team.

- Starting Cost for Manual Team is 6 Cost; for Randomized Team, 10.

- Should you select the "Reshuffle Randomized Team" option, other previously randomized Teams will be listed under the "Randomized Teams" section.

※ Per-ldentity Cost varies based on each Identity's release date and rarity.

※ Randomized Teams will automatically equip the Identities within the starting Cost budget of 10, which is 4 higher than starting as a Manual Team.

※ Randomized Teams will obtain +40% more Schemacraft Pass EXP.

※ You may Reshuffle your starting Randomized Team 3 times between each daily reset. Certain progress milestone options in each completed Schemacraft run may recharge the Reshuffle function. The total number of Reshuffles, however, cannot exceed 3.
※ While Identities you do not own can be equipped, they may only be upgraded to Tier 3 within the gameplay mode.

▶ How to Progress
- Movement between rooms in "Schemacraft" is determined by a dice roll. Each room may contain combat Encounters or Respites.

- The dice rolls from a range of 1~3, and the resulting number decides the upper limit of how many nodes (called Representations) you may travel.

- Each roll of the dice after the 5th roll in each Schema zone will give empowering effects to the enemies, called "Schema Deformation".

▶ Combat Information
- Before each battle, you may preview the list of possible Pack rewards and the average level of featured enemies.

- The Pack list is chosen at random. The name of each reward Pack indicates the relevant Keyword, faction, or Sinner for the Identities it features.

※ While Elite Encounter Representations yield better rewards than Regular Encounters, their featured enemies will receive certain empowering effects.

▶ Entering Representations
- You may rearrange your Team's Deployment Order prior to entering each Encounter.

- Initially, Combat Encounters in "Schemacraft" will only feature 4 allied Identities. As you progress through each Schemacraft run, however, your team will slowly expand up to 8 fielded Identities at once, with 4 Backup Units.

- You cannot begin an Encounter unless you have set Deployment Orders for all Sinners in your roster.

- In "Schemacraft", the Sinners' SP values carry over between Encounters.

- Sinners defeated in an Encounter enter the next at 0 SP.

- Backup Identities receive bonus SP upon entry, with a baseline of positive modifiers.

※ Identities who exclusively use Minus Coins, however, will receive negative SP modifiers upon Substitution. (Lobotomy E.G.O::Sunshower Heathcliff, The One Who Shall Grip Sinclair)
※ Identities whose Coin types change based on their SP will receive appropriate SP modifiers for their SP value upon Substitution. (Lobotomy E.G.O::In the Name of Love and Hate Don Quixote, Lobotomy E.G.O::The Sword Sharpened with Tears Rodion)

▶ Defense Strategy
- Configure how each Identity uses Defense Skills.

- During combat, Identities automatically use Defense Skills according to their individual settings.

- Each Identity follows the Default setting unless changed.

- Use "Set All to Default," "Apply Selected Identity's Settings to All," or "No One Defends" at the bottom of the screen to update every Sinner's Defense Strategy at once.

▶ Briefing
- Review ally and enemy information, including each side's average level, before starting a battle.

- Enemy information includes the number of initial enemies and reinforcements, along with their levels and weaknesses.

- The total resources required to use E.G.O are shown alongside the amount of resources Identities can generate.

Combat Progression

▶ Combat Progression
- In "Schemacraft," Combat Progression is automated.

- Each ally and enemy acts in an order determined by its Speed range. Units use Attack Skills from their Skill stash to Clash or make Unopposed Attacks, and use Defense Skills according to their Defense Strategy.

- A shared turn ends once every ally and enemy has acted at least once. Faster units may act multiple times within a single turn.

- All effects on allies and enemies follow this shared turn count.

※ Use the Show/Hide UI button in the upper-right corner to toggle the battle interface.

※ E.G.O resources do not carry over between battles. Instead, at the start of each battle, you gain (number of Attack Skills in the Sin Affinity / 2) E.G.O resources for each respective Affinity, rounded down. This counts from all Identities in your Team, including Backup Units.

※ Sapling of Light abilities cannot be used in Schemacraft battles.

▶ Using E.G.O
- Once the required resources are available, you can use E.G.O at any time to help turn the battle in your team's favor.

- Click or tap an E.G.O icon to queue it for activation. Its target is selected automatically.

- Click or tap a queued E.G.O icon again to cancel its use.

- Drag an E.G.O icon onto an enemy on the battlefield to target that enemy.

- Drag an E.G.O icon downward to Overclock it.

- You can retry or forfeit a stage during battle.

※ Each battle allows up to 2 retries.

※ Forfeiting a stage also consumes 1 retry. Losing or forfeiting with no retries remaining ends the Schemacraft run.

▶ Battle Report
- Review a detailed report after each battle.

Battle Rewards

▶ Choosing a Reward Pack
- After winning a battle, choose a Reward Pack from those available at that Representation.

- Reward Packs can offer Identities associated with their featured keyword, faction, or Sinner, with at least 1 matching Identity guaranteed.

- If none of the offered Packs are suitable, spend a Reshuffle to replace 1 Reward Pack with another from the remaining pool.

- Clearing Elite and Boss Representations in each Schema zone grants slightly better rewards than clearing Regular Encounter Representations.

▶ Regular Encounter Representation
- Selecting a Reward Pack reveals a list of Identities and E.G.O. Select the indicated number of rewards from this list.

- Spend a Reshuffle attempt to refresh the offered Identities and E.G.O once.

- Team → Recruit the Identity to the current team. If that Sinner is already on the team, replace their current Identity. Obtaining a duplicate Identity raises its current Uptie Tier by 1 or sets it to the Uptie Tier shown on the reward, whichever is higher.
- Sliver → Convert the reward into 1-3 Slivers for that Sinner, based on the reward's displayed Uptie Tier, instead of adding or replacing an Identity. If that Sinner has already reached the Uptie Tier cap, receive Universal Slivers instead. You can use Universal Slivers at Rest Nodes.
- E.G.O rewards are equipped in the corresponding E.G.O Risk Level-based Slot of a Sinner on the team. If that Sinner is absent, the E.G.O is stored. Selecting another E.G.O of the same Risk Level allows the existing E.G.O to be replaced.

※ If adding a Sinner would exceed the team limit, one of the Sinners already on the team can be replaced.

※ When replacing an Identity, the new Identity uses the Uptie Tier shown on its reward, and some of that Sinner's Slivers are refunded.

※ Unowned Identities and E.G.O can also appear. The following rules apply when using them.
- Identities can only be upgraded to Uptie Tier III within Schemacraft.
- E.G.O is equipped at Threadspin Tier I.

※ Unobtained COLLABORATION - LIMITED E.G.O will not appear.

- Relevant E.G.O → Command : Meltdown Faust, To Remain Oneself [TEE] Hong Lu, Tidal Elegy Ishmael, Unbrilliant Glory Gregor

▶ Elite Encounter Representation
- Winning an Elite Encounter grants an Elite Reward.
- Replace Identity: Replace a specified Sinner's Identity with another Identity.
- Recruit Identity: Add a specified Identity to the team.
- Upgrade: Obtain 3 Slivers for a Sinner currently on the team, which may increase their Uptie Tier.

※ When choosing Replace Identity or Recruit Identity, if that Sinner already has an Identity in the team, Slivers are granted based on the previous Identity's Uptie Tier.

▶ Boss Encounter Representation
- After winning a Boss Encounter, choose between 2 reward options, which may offer Reshuffles, Identity Uptie Tier increases, or Universal Slivers.

- Each reward option also includes an additional choice of a Sinner Pack or a Random Pack.

※ The available Boss Encounter reward options may vary by Schema.

- Obtain specific Identities and E.G.O at the cost of 0~2 remaining dice rolls, depending on the option selected.

- If there are not enough dice rolls remaining, the "Deformation" effect is applied instead.

▶ Event Representation
- Choose beneficial effects to aid in battle, along with drawbacks that strengthen enemies.

- After clearing Schemacraft through the boss of Schema 5 once, additional Event Representations with special choices will become available.

※ If too many drawbacks have accumulated or none of the benefits are worthwhile, passing without making a selection can be a sound strategy.

▶ Respite Representation
- Choose 1 of 3 options at a Respite Representation. Universal Slivers obtained from Reward Packs can also be spent here.
- Reshuffles + → Gain additional reshuffles for Reward Packs, Identities, and E.G.O.
- Uptie Tier + for 1 Identity → Increase the Uptie Tier of 1 Identity in the team.
- Bonus Rolls + → Gain additional dice rolls for movement. This bonus carries over when advancing to the next Schema.
- Spend Universal Slivers to increase the Uptie Tier of an Identity of choice.

※ When Uptying, Identities not obtained by your account may only be Uptied to Tier 3.

※ Uptie Tiers available at each Respite Representation in each Schema are as follows (same applies when Uptying via Universal Slivers):

- Schema 1 ~ Schema 3: Up to Uptie 3
- Schema 4 ~ Schema 5: Up to Uptie 4

Final Results & Schemacraft Pass

▶ Final Battle Results
- There are 3 ways to end a Schemacraft Run and view your Final Battle Results: 1) Clear the final boss on "Schema 5";
2) Lose an Encounter with 0 Retries remaining;
3) Selecting "Forfeit Schemacraft Run" in the options menu.

- On this screen, you may review various details of your progress, such as Identities used, damage tally, Schemacraft EXP gained, and more.

▶ Schemacraft Pass
- Gain Schemacraft Pass EXP based on the Encounter outcomes.

- Higher Schemacraft zones and difficult Encounters will yield more Schemacraft Pass EXP.

- Beginning with a Randomized Team will yield a +40% bonus on Schemacraft Pass EXP.

※ Up to 1,000 Schemacraft Pass EXP may be gained per week. This cap will be reset every Thursday, 06:00 (KST).

※ Schemacraft Pass EXP cannot be gained past the currently unlocked Pass level.

※ Obtain "Nominable Announcer Ticket [Yisang Exchange] (Schemacraft Beta)" as a special reward upon reaching Schemacraft Pass Level 10.
- Use this item to select and obtain an Announcer from a limited list of Announcers that can be acquired through Yisang Exchange on the Extraction menu. (※ Only Announcers released up to the Season 8's end date can be obtained through this item. Does not include Walpurgis Night Announcers.)
