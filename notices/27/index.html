Notice of Upcoming Adjustments to Passive Activation

March 24, 2023

Greetings, Dear Manager.

For the April 6th update, the activation conditions for Passive Abilities will be adjusted to be easier.
Below are the planned changes and their intended purposes.

Aims

- To allow Passive Abilities to activate more frequently and emphasize a choice between E.G.O skills and Passive Abilities, we intend to adjust their requirements for activation.

Planned Changes

- The activation requirements of certain Combat and Support Passives will be adjusted.
- Certain Passives will change to activate based on the number of E.G.O resources in possession.
- Certain Passives may receive little to no change in activation requirements.
