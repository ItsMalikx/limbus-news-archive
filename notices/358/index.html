2025, Mar. 13th Scheduled Update Notice

2025-03-10

---------------------------------------

Greetings, Dear Manager.

The server will enter scheduled maintenance from 10:00 [AM] through 12:00 [PM] on Mar. 13th, 2025 (KST).

Please make note of the maintenance schedule; we recommend that you refrain from entering combat or making in-app purchases and close the application before maintenance begins so that no data is lost.

Please refer below for details about the update.

New Content
-----------

1. Mirror of the Dreaming Achievements - Part 3

Combat category will be added to the list of Achievements; Achievement Reward Levels will be expanded to Level 50.

Bug Fixes and Improvements
--------------------------

► Fixed an issue where Liu South Section 4 Director Rodion's 4th Coin of Skill 3 effect at Uptie 3 that inflicts additional Wrath damage works in an unintended manner
※ Relevant effect: Inflict Wrath Affinity damage equal to half of the Burn Potency on target (max 20)
- Before: was inflicting Wrath Affinity damage equal to the Burn Potency on target (max 20)
- After: adjusted to match the description - now it inflicts Wrath Affinity damage equal to half of the Burn Potency on target (max 20)

► Improved the post-Uptie 3 animated CG art for Firefist Office Survivor Gregor

► Fixed an issue where certain vfx that should appear when using Firefist Office Survivor Gregor's Skill 3 was missing

► (EN Only) Fixed a typo in Firefist Office Survivor Gregor's Identity Voicelines

► (JP Only) Fixed a typo and translation error in Firefist Office Survivor Gregor's Passive
- Before: 自分がこのターンで消耗した12区産燃料、過熱燃料1につきダメージ量+0.2% (最大40%)
- After: 自分がこの戦闘にて消耗した12区産燃料、過熱燃料1につきダメージ量+0.2% (最大40%)

► (JP Only) Fixed an issue where the subtitles did not match the characters in Stage 4-1

► Clarified the description for certain Achievement conditions in Mirror of the Dreaming
※ Relevant Achievements
(1) For 1 ally, replace all of their Skill 1 copies with Skill 3 in the Shop; then, clear Mirror Dungeon Floor 5
(2) Enhance 1 Tier 3 or higher E.G.O Gift to Enhancement stage ++; then, clear Mirror Dungeon Floor 5
- Before: Clear Mirror Dungeon Floor 5
- After: Clear Mirror Dungeon Floor 5 or higher
※ This is a text-only change; it does not affect the actual conditions for this achievement.

► Fixed an issue where certain effects that trigger based on 'Focused Attack' and 'Nerve Strike' didn't activate for E.G.O Gift "Plume of Proof"

► Fixed an issue where, in certain circumstances, the player's list of E.G.O Gifts could not be viewed while progressing through the Main Story Dungeons

► Fixed an issue where story choice events were occurring in the Mirror Dungeon encounter against the Abnormality 'Golden Apple'

► Addressed the issue where certain texts could appear covered by the end of the Mirror Dungeon Rest Bonus Retention Unit text box

► Addressed the issue where dummy resources lingered on certain Mounting Trials icons & made adjustments to certain icons

► Addressed the issue where the in-game text may load incorrectly for certain content

► (EN Only) Fixed an issue where the E.G.O Gift 'Dueling Manual Book 3' had an unnecessary highlight around the 'Focus Attack' text

Maintenance & Application Update Compensation
---------------------------------------------

- Content : Lunacy x300
- Recipients: all users whose game accounts were created before 10:00 Mar. 20th, 2025 (KST)
- When: After Mar. 13th (Thu) 2025, 12:00 (KST)

Notes
-----

- Please update your game client to the newest version on the respective store (Google Play/App Store/Steam).
- Forcibly shutting down the game while it is downloading resources may cause the download to fail, so please be careful not to close the application during the download.
- You will not be able to play <Limbus Company> during maintenance. Please understand.
- The maintenance schedule may suddenly change depending on the status of maintenance. If such a change occurs, we will let you know in a follow-up notice.
- A resource download may be prompted after the build update. If you're using a mobile device, it is recommended to proceed with a Wi-Fi connection.
- If you're playing with a guest account, we recommend that you link your account with other platforms to prevent possible account loss.
- The maintenance compensation will be provided once per account. Please note that the distribution may be slow due to network delays or other causes.
- The update gifts are valid to claim for 7 days. Please redeem them within this timeframe.
