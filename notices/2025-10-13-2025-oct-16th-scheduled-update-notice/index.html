2025, Oct. 16th Scheduled Update Notice

October 13, 2025
Balance

Greetings, Dear Manager.

The server will enter scheduled maintenance from
10:00 [AM] through 12:00 [PM] on Oct. 16th, 2025 (KST).

Please make note of the maintenance schedule; we recommend that you refrain from entering combat or making in-app purchases and close the application before maintenance begins so that no data is lost.

Please refer below for details about the update.

Major Changes

1. Adjusted the difficulty balance of certain enemies in Stage 6-41. In order to improve plot presentation, Announcer voices will be fixed.

※ We are aware that due to the growing performance gap between Identities from the 6-41 main story update to the present, it has become difficult for Managers to experience the dramatic presentation we originally intended to showcase during the initial planning. To address this, we have adjusted the difficulty balance and plot presentation so that Managers may enjoy the intended immersive effect regardless of the performance gap caused by successive updates.

2. Added 3 new theme packs to the Mirror Dungeon Parallel Superposition EXTREME Mode:
- A Beautiful Voice, The Dawn of Green, Into a Certain Library's Book

Bug Fixes & Improvements

▶ Fixed an issue where, the effect below from Heishou Pack
- Wu Branch Adept Yisang's combat passive "The Steed's Houndlike Loyalty [拘馬之心]" did not trigger
※ Effect : After this unit uses the Skill "Obsidian Stormrider's Crescent Decapitator", Family Hierarch Candidate Ishmael uses "Crimson Spring" as Assist Attack (once per turn)
※ This issue was identified following the Assist Attack system update on October 2nd.

▶ (JP Only) Fixed translation errors and unclear descriptions of Shi Assoc. East Section 3 Faust's skill effects
1. Revised the skill effect translation of Skill 3: Glimmering Bow [弓閃] at Uptie IV
- Before : 自分の呼吸と対象の出血の合計4につき、マッチ威力 +1 (最大2)
- After : 自分の呼吸と対象の出血の合計4につき、コイン威力 +1 (最大2)

2. Revised the unclear parts in defense skill description
- Before : [戦闘開始時]「閃弓」として使用するなら「回避」に変更
- After : [戦闘開始時]「閃弓」を使用するなら「回避」に変更
※ No changes to actual performance; only the Japanese text is revised.

▶ Improved the final rendering of character illustration unlocked at N Corp. E.G.O::Contempt, Awe Ryōshū's Uptie III

▶ Fixed an issue where, when Heishou Pack - You Branch Sinclair's basic (pre-Uptie III) illustration is applied, the cutscene triggered upon using Skill 3 becomes misplaced on-screen

▶ Fixed an issue where W Corp. L2 Cleanup Agent Faust's basic (pre-Uptie III) illustration applied onto the Identity Card does not match the identity's current illustration

▶ Fixed an issue where, in Mirror Dungeon's combat screen, keyword tooltips did not appear in the "E.G.O Gifts Owned" popup

Maintenance & Application Update Compensation

- Content: Lunacy x300
- Recipients: All users whose game accounts were created before 06:00 Oct. 23th, 2025 (KST)
- When: After Oct. 16th, 2025 (Thu) 12:00 (KST)

Maintenance Compensation can be claimed until Oct. 23th, (Thu) 2025, 06:00 (KST) via the in-game Mailbox. (Mail available for 7 days)

Notes

- Please update your game client to the newest version on the respective store (Google Play/App Store/Steam).
- Forcibly shutting down the game while it is downloading resources may cause the download to fail, so please be careful not to close the application during the download.
- You will not be able to play <Limbus Company> during maintenance. Please understand.
- The maintenance schedule may suddenly change depending on the status of maintenance. If such a change occurs, we will let you know in a follow-up notice.
- A resource download may be prompted after the build update. If you're using a mobile device, it is recommended to proceed with a Wi-Fi connection.
- If you're playing with a guest account, we recommend that you link your account with other platforms to prevent possible account loss.
- The maintenance compensation will be provided once per account. Please note that the distribution may be slow due to network delays or other causes.
- The update gifts are valid to claim for 7 days. Please redeem them within this timeframe.

Thank you.
