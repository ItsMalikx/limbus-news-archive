Update

New Content - “Reflectrial][lairtcelteR - Chachihu”

August 17, 2026

Greetings, Dear Manager.

With the Aug. 20th, 2026 (KST) Scheduled Update, new content [”Reflectrial][lairtcelteR - Chachihu”] will be added to the game.

► Requirements

Clear all Stages of Canto 8

Description

- When the event starts, “Reflectrial][lairtcelteR - Chachihu” will be added to the game.

- This content allows you to challenge a boss unit from Canto 8 on a higher difficulty.

※ Once “Reflectrial][lairtcelteR - Chachihu” is added with the Scheduled Update, it will become available indefinitely.

※ Clear "Reflectrial][lairtcelteR - Chachihu” to earn the mode-exclusive currency “Burnt-in Lens.” Clear the battle under a certain number of turns to earn more “Burnt-in Lens.”

※ Obtained “Burnt-in Lens” can be used to exchange for other in-game resources in the “Reward Exchange” screen in the Reflectrial][lairtcelteR menu.

※ Clear “Reflectrial][lairtcelteR - Chachihu” to obtain Ticket Deco items and a special banner.

※ Clear “Reflectrial][lairtcelteR - Chachihu” on Normal difficulty before Sep. 17th, 2026, 06:00 (KST) to obtain the limited reward “Nominable Announcer Ticket [Yisang Exchange]”.

Event Gameplay

► ”Reflectrial][lairtcelteR - Chachihu”

Upon clearing all Stages of Canto 8 “The Surrendered Witnessing”, “Reflectrial][lairtcelteR - Chachihu” will become accessible from the “Reflectrial][lairtcelteR” menu on the “Drive” tab. This content features a stronger version of a Boss unit that appears in “The Surrendered Witnessing”.

Clearing the boss battle yields a per-difficulty clear reward. Clear the boss fight under certain turn counts to obtain extra rewards.

Content Progression

► Event Main Page

On the Event Main Page, you can view details such as enemy information, special battle effects, and the general progression method.

You can access this Reflectrial][lairtcelteR game mode freely without spending any currencies.

Reflectrial][lairtcelteR has 2 difficulty levels: Normal and Hard. Hard Difficulty is unlocked upon clearing the content on Normal Difficulty.

※ "Reflectrial][lairtcelteR" is a high-difficulty challenge. Level up and Uptie/Threadspin your Identities and E.G.O to meet the recommended specs before entering the battle.

<Recommended Specs>

- Identities : Uptie Tier 4, Level 60
- E.G.O : Threadspinning Tier 4

► Unique Effects

- “Reflectrial][lairtcelteR” features unique effects (“Shade of Reflection” and “Tribulation”) based on your current battle and phase.

- “Effect: Shade of Reflection” applies across all difficulties. This effect is beneficial to allied units.

- “Effect: Tribulation” applies only on Hard Difficulty. This effect strengthens enemy units.

- “Effect: Shade of Reflection” differs based on combat phase. It is recommended to plan out your team by taking this into account.

► Team Loadout

- “Reflectrial][lairtcelteR” is a “Chain Battle”. In a Chain Battle, units that have died will be replaced by Backup units according to their Deployment Order.

- You can enter the battle only after selecting 7 Deployed units and 5 Backup units.

- Entry SP Buff is applied on Backup units upon Substituting in. When an ally that utilizes SP-based Coin types Substitutes in, Entry SP Buff will be granted with a positive or negative value, depending on the ally’s current SP-based Coin type. (e.g. Lobotomy E.G.O::In the Name of Love and Hate Don Quixote, Lobotomy E.G.O::The Sword Sharpened with Tears Rodion)

- Upon Substituting in, Identities that use Minus Coins will receive an Entry SP Buff with a negative value. This Entry SP Buff will not drop the Identity’s SP below -40. (e.g. Lobotomy E.G.O In the Name of Love and Hate Don Quixote, Lobotomy E.G.O::Sunshower Heathcliff, Lobotomy E.G.O::The Sword Sharpened with Tears Rodion, The One Who Shall Grip Sinclair)

- Reflectrial][lairtcelteR allows a maximum of 3 Team Loadouts to be used in order in a battle. However, Identities assigned to a previous Loadout cannot be reused in subsequent Loadouts.

► Progression

- A 12-turn limit is applied to each Team Loadout.

- Exceeding 12 turns or selecting “Quit Battle and Save Progress” will save the battle progression state. You may continue progressing through the battle with the next Loadout.

Progress saved : Boss Unit's Combat Phase, HP, and SP, E.G.O Resources owned, Certain unique effects applied on enemies
Progress not saved : Boss Unit’s Pattern, HP and SP of enemies, excluding the Boss Unit, Certain effects applied on enemies

► Saving Your Progress

- After saving your progress, you can view the boss unit’s remaining HP and the battle progress of the previous Loadout.

- Should you wish to reattempt the battle from the beginning, you may reset the battle via the “Reset Progress” button.

► After the First Battle

- Starting with the second battle, your team's Entry SP increases based on the starting cumulative battle Turn Count of the previous Loadout. Minus Coin Identities start with negative SP numbers instead.

※ Identities assigned to previous Team Loadouts cannot be reused in subsequent Loadouts of the same run.

Battle Progression and Outcome

► Battle Options

- You may choose to Retry, Forfeit, or Quit Battle and Save Progress. Please note that selecting Retry or Forfeit will reset your battle progress back to the last save state.

※ Selecting “Quit Battle and Save Progress” will save the Boss Unit’s HP, SP, as well as your Turn Count. You will not be able to use the Identities assigned to the current Loadout in the battle again.

► Battle Outcome

- The Battle Outcome screen will appear once the Boss Unit’s HP reaches 0.

- On the Battle Outcome screen, you can view the Identities used in your Team Loadouts, along with other battle-related details.

※ Reflectrial][lairtcelteR does not save Total Turn Count Records for previous runs. Please note that the Main Screen of Reflectrial][lairtcelteR will not show the Total Turn Count Record of your previous runs.

Reward Claim

- Defeating the boss unit of “Reflectrial][lairtcelteR” will allow you to claim rewards based on cumulative Turns taken.

- Normal Difficulty and Hard Difficulty each offer a different set of rewards. Claiming your rewards does not cost any currencies.

Major Changes

Clearing “Reflectrial][lairtcelteR - Chachihu” yields ticket deco items and a special banner.

The quantity of items that can be received in exchange for “Burnt-in Lens” in the Standard Rewards exchange shop will be increased.

※ This does not include the “Identity Training Ticket III x1” reward.

Notice

※ Clear “Reflectrial][lairtcelteR - Chachihu” on Normal difficulty before Sep. 17th, 2026, 06:00 (KST) to obtain the limited reward “Nominable Announcer Ticket [Yisang Exchange]”. Please note that this item has to be claimed by clicking on the relevant icon in the “Reflectrial][lairtcelteR” menu.
