(3) [NEW] Ideality Exchange for Extractions
With the Season 2 update, the Ideality Exchange system will be added for target extractions.
During an extraction banner, you can gain Yisang [Ideality] [理想以上] based on the number of extractions you’ve performed.
After collecting a certain amount of Ideality, you can exchange it for a 3★ Identity or E.G.O represented in the target extraction.

Ideality is not shared between target extraction banners; Ideality in each target extraction is considered as its own currency.
After the target extraction is over, the remaining Ideality is converted to Thread.

(6) Adjusted Increase and Decrease Rates of Sanity

Aims: We felt that current rates of changes to Sanity were influenced too dramatically by results of clashes and unit deaths, so the rates of change from these factors have been adjusted to reduce the degree of impact.

<Details>

[Factors Increasing Sanity]

- After winning a Clash, increases based on the Clash count
(Old: Base Value is 10, raised by 25% per Clash count after 1)
(New: Base Value is 5, raised by 20% per Clash count after 1)

- If the level of the defeated enemy was higher than the unit’s, increase based on the level difference
(Old: Base Value is 10, raised by 10 per level)
(New: Base Value is 5, raised by 5 per level)

- If the level of the enemy defeated by an ally was higher than the unit’s, increase based on the level difference
(Old: Base Value is 5, raised by 5 per level)
(New: Base Value is 2, raised by 2 per level)

[Factors Decreasing Sanity]

- After losing a Clash, decreases based on the Clash count
(Old: Base Value is 10, raised by 25% per Clash count after 1)
(New: Base Value is 5, raised by 10% per Clash count after 1)

- If the level of the defeated ally was higher than the unit’s, increase based on the level difference
(Old: Base Value is 10, raised by 10 per level)
(New: Base Value is 5, raised by 5 per level)

4. Information on End of Season 1

Please refer to the link below for further details on changes and additions coming with Season 2.
https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1973530/view/3685678897893653591

(1) End of Season 1 Limbus Pass - Orientation
Unclaimed pass rewards from the previous season will be automatically claimed when you first login to the game after the season change update.
The carryover rates for unconverted Egoshard-related items from multiple seasons past will be halved for each following Season.
Egoshard-related items that remain after the carryover are converted into equal amounts of Thread-related items.

(2) Carryover of Seasonal Currencies
Half of the currencies listed below in your possession will be converted to Season 2’s.
- Season 1 [Sinner]’s Egoshard
- Season 1 Egoshard Crate
- Season 1 Nominable Egoshard Crate

The carryover of currencies to Season 2 will automatically occur the first time you log into the game during Season 2.
A popup will show how much Season 1 currency you owned, and how they have been converted.

Season 1 currencies still in the mailbox will only be converted after they are claimed.
If you receive Season 1 currencies through the mailbox during Season 2, they will be automatically converted upon redemption into Season 2 currency.
