2026, Oct. 15th Scheduled Update Notice

October 14, 2026
Identities & E.G.OMain StoryMirror DungeonNew Content

Greetings, Dear Manager.

The server will enter scheduled maintenance from 06:00 AM through 12:00 PM on Oct. 15th, 2026 (KST).

Please make note of the maintenance schedule; we recommend that you refrain from entering combat or making in-app purchases and close the application before maintenance begins so that no data is lost.

Please refer below for details about the update.

※ Maintenance for the Oct. 15th Scheduled Update will take place from 06:00 through 12:00 (KST).

When the Oct. 15th update maintenance concludes at 12:00, the number of Mirror Dungeon Weekly Bonuses and any ongoing Mirror Dungeon run records will be reset.

Any Mirror Dungeon run records remaining from before Oct. 15th, 06:00 will be reset, and their rewards will become unobtainable. Projection Level progression and reward claims for Mirror Dungeon 7, "Mirror of Names and Spiders", will also become unavailable.

Please keep this in mind and ensure that you claim all Projection Rewards before the Oct. 15th Scheduled Update maintenance.

Bug Fixes & Improvements

▶ Revised the text of a certain effect belonging to a certain unit in Main Story Chapter 10 - Part 3, as the effect functioned differently from its description
→ Relevant effect: Gain +1 Pulsation Count every time Bleed is activated in an Encounter
- Before: The said effect would activate only when "Bleed damage" was triggered
- After: Text is changed to "Gain +1 Pulsation Count every time Bleed damage is activated in an Encounter"
※ As the effect was found to activate only upon taking "Bleed damage", only the relevant text will be revised to preserve Managers' gameplay experience.

▶ Adjusted the difficulty of the Floor 5 boss in the Shadowed from Main Story Chapter 10 - Part 3
- Before: Attack End: If the main target has 5+ L'Abject, use a powerful Skill against the main target this unit attacked
- After: Attack End: If the main target has 5+ L'Abject, use a powerful Skill against the main target this unit attacked (once per turn)

▶ (EN Only) Revised the translation of the Panic Type belonging to a certain boss unit in Main Story Chapter 10 - Part 3
- Before: Die Hämmer
- After: Les Marteaux
※ The actual effects of the Panic Type will remain unchanged; only the translated Panic Type name will be revised.

▶ Fixed an issue in a certain boss battle in Main Story Chapter 10 - Part 3, where Meursault could not generate a certain E.G.O again after generating it and transitioning to the next phase without using the said E.G.O

▶ Fixed an issue where certain effects would not be displayed under certain conditions in Main Story Stages 7-36 and 9-42
※ This issue will also be fixed in Mirror Dungeon encounters featuring the relevant unit.

▶ Fixed certain unnatural elements in the story cinematic of a certain boss unit in Main Story Chapter 10 - Part 3

▶ Certain Sapling of Light Ability can no longer be used during a certain boss Encounter in Main Story Chapter 10 - Part 3
