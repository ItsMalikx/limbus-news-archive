2025, Apr. 10th Scheduled Update Notice

April 7, 2025
BalanceMain Story

Greetings, Dear Manager.

The server will enter scheduled maintenance from 10:00 [AM] through 12:00 [PM] on Apr. 10th, 2025 (KST).

Please make note of the maintenance schedule; we recommend that you refrain from entering combat or making in-app purchases and close the application before maintenance begins so that no data is lost.

Please refer below for details about the update.

Bug Fixes & Improvements

▶ Fixed an issue where certain triggers would linger after activation and would repeatedly activate when using Defensive Skills
※ Relevant Effects
- Dieci South Section 4 Hong Lu's 'Support Passive'
- Cinq West Section 3 Meursault's 'Combat Passive' (Uptie 4 only)
- Intervallo V - LCB Regular Check-up Enemy Passive 'Censored Courage'
- E.G.O Gift 'Wobbling Keg'
- E.G.O Gift 'Omnivibro-octovecti-bell'

▶ Fixed an issue where certain effects of the E.G.O Gift 'Contempt of the Gaze of Contempt' did not work properly
1. Fixed an issue where, at Enhancement Tier ++, the effect triggers for all Identities present in the Stage, then applies to both Identities that the Gift actually affects
2. Fixed an issue where the effect 'Combat Start: when targeted by 2+ Attack Skills, deal (# of Skills targeting this unit) fixed HP & SP damage against all enemies targeting this unit' did not work properly

▶ Fixed an issue where certain effects of the E.G.O Gift 'Epicenter' E.G.O Gift does not work properly
※ Relevant Effect: When using a Skill that consumes Tremor Count on self: if the target is defeated or Staggered, gain +(Tremor Count consumed / 2) Tremor Count (once per Skill)

▶ Fixed an issue where, in Focused Encounters, certain enemy information could not be unlocked

▶ Fixed an issue where the UI element denoting the Parts of the Abnormality 'Dream-devouring Siltcurrent' would not load properly

▶ Added an advantage modifier for the 'Envy' Sin Affinity in The Middle Choice Event featured in the Mirror Dungeon Theme Pack 'Nocturnal Sweeping'

▶ (AOS, iOS) Fixed an issue where the game may not work properly when the player returns to the main title screen due to additional downloads occurring during gameplay

▶ (Steam) Fixed an issue where, while in combat, if the player's mouse cursor is hovering above the Win Rate or Damage buttons, the relevant shortcut keys didn't work properly

Bug Fixes & Improvements

▶ (Custom TL Only) Fixed an issue where Fonts could not be changed for English alphabets and Numbers

▶ (Custom TL Only) Fixed an issue where Custom Translations could not be applied to certain Battle Pass related UI elements

▶ Fixed an issue where, under certain circumstances, continuing a paused Dungeon Stage causes the Tutorial SKIP button to appear

▶ (KR Only) Fixed an issue where certain Keyword highlights did not work properly for the E.G.O Gift 'Bloodied Mask of a Devotee'

▶ (EN Only) Standardization work to address inconsistent wordings and word clutters in Skill descriptions in progress
- Before : Deal +X% more damage /// Deal -X% less damage
- After : Deal +X% damage /// Deal -X% damage

▶ (EN Only) Fixed an issue where 'Sinclair E.G.O - Lantern' had an unnecessary Keyword highlight

▶ (EN, JP Only) Adjusted translation regarding mentions of 흑수(Heishou Pack) in Main Story Canto 7

Maintenance & Application Update Compensation

- Content: Lunacy x300
- Recipients: all users whose game accounts were created before 10:00 Apr. 17th, 2025 (KST)
- When: After Apr. 10th (Thu) 2025, 12:00 (KST)
- Can be claimed once per account

Maintenance Compensation can be claimed until Apr. 17th (Thu) 2025, 10:00 (KST) via the in-game Mailbox. (Mail available for 7 days)

Notes

- Please update your game client to the newest version on the respective store (Google Play/App Store/Steam).
- Forcibly shutting down the game while it is downloading resources may cause the download to fail, so please be careful not to close the application during the download.
- You will not be able to play <Limbus Company> during maintenance. Please understand.
- The maintenance schedule may suddenly change depending on the status of maintenance. If such a change occurs, we will let you know in a follow-up notice.
- A resource download may be prompted after the build update. If you're using a mobile device, it is recommended to proceed with a Wi-Fi connection.
- If you're playing with a guest account, we recommend that you link your account with other platforms to prevent possible account loss.
- The maintenance compensation will be provided once per account. Please note that the distribution may be slow due to network delays or other causes.
- The update gifts are valid to claim for 7 days. Please redeem them within this timeframe.

Thank you.
