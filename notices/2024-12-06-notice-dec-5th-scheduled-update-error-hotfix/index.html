    [Notice: Dec. 5th Scheduled Update Error Hotfix]

    December 6, 2024
    Main Story

    Greetings, dear Manager.

    After the Dec. 5th Scheduled Update, we identified an issue where story CG images will not load; we intend to deploy a hotfix to correct this issue.
    Hotfixes do not require a separate client update, and can be applied upon restarting the game. Please note that additional downloads may occur.

    Issue
    ▶ An issue where certain story CGs in Chapter 5.5(Yield My Flesh to Claim Their Bones), Canto 6, and Canto 7 appear as white images

    Deployment ETA
    ▶ 12.6(FRI) 17:00(KST) - There may be a 10 minute margin of error.

    Other known issues will be applied via a future Client Update, and we will inform you of those issues in the Dec. 9th (MON) Scheduled Update Notice.
    We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience.
