    Notice Regarding a Game-stopping Error and Update Plans

    March 24, 2023

    Greetings, Dear Manager.

    We have identified an error where the game freezes when a unit dies from a skill's use effect.
    A build resolving the issue will be pushed for the Steam version before the end of the day.
    As for the Android and iOS versions, the fix will come with the March 30th update as an immediate hotfix is impossible to publish due to the individual platforms' screening procedures.

    We hope you understand and appreciate your patience.

    Again, we apologize for the inconvenience.

    Compensation for the error will be provided after the March 30th update.
    Please refer below for more details.

    ※ The patch has been released for Steam at 15:35, Mar 24 (KST).
