June 1, 2026

Hello, this is Project Moon.

The AOS market review has been completed for the fix of "AOS Inaccessibility Issue" that occurred after the May 28th, 2026 Scheduled Update.

- Jun. 1st (Mon), 22:00 (KST)

Should there be any further information to share regarding this matter, we will notify you via a separate announcement.
