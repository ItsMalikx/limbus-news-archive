Notice: Nov. 16th STEAM Error Fix

November 16, 2023

Greetings, this is Project Moon.

The following issue occurred on the November 16th Scheduled Update; the fix for this issue was deployed at 15:30 today.

[Fixed Issue]

(STEAM only) Fixed an issue where a character could not be selected for the Main Story 0-4, making it impossible to progress past that stage.

For Managers experiencing this issue: please close the game, update the game client, and restart in order to progress.
We apologize for causing a disturbance to your enjoyment of our game.
