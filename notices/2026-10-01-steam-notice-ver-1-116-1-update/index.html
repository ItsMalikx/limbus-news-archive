    (Steam) Notice: Ver. 1.116.1 Update

    October 1, 2026

    Hello, this is Project Moon.

    Ver. 1.116.1, which fixes the known issues identified in Ver. 1.116.0, updated Oct. 1st, will be deployed soon.

    This update will be deployed to Steam first.

    As mobile platforms (AOS, iOS) require the update to go through each market's review, the same update cannot be deployed simultaneously across both PC and mobile environments.

    Until the update is applied to the AOS and iOS platforms, we would like to ask our mobile users to access the game on Steam if possible.

    We will inform you via a separate notice as soon as updates become available on each AOS and iOS platform.

    Please refer to the information below for more information.

    [ Fixes Deployed in this Version ]

    ▶ Fixed an issue where, in Main Story Chapter 10, Meursault could die when using "The Prince of La Manchaland" Identity against a certain boss unit.

    ▶ (The Shadowed) Fixed an issue where restarting the stage under certain conditions could cause the game to become unresponsive

    ▶ Fixed an issue where, when Excision Coins or Erasure Coins deleted Unbreakable Coins, the game would become unresponsive

    [ Version Update ETA ]

    - After 10.1 (Thu) 21:30 (KST)
    - There may be a delay of approximately 10 minutes.

    ※ Once the update has been applied successfully, the version number displayed at the bottom right of the title screen will show 1.116.1.

    We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience caused to gameplay and story immersion.
