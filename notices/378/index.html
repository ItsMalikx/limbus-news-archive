Hello, this is Project Moon.

※ Notice: Regarding Service Unavailability in Vietnam

3. Detailed Info : Limbus Company's Google Play Store Service has become unavailable due to Vietnamese Local Regulations (New Downloads, Updates, and Paid Transactions Unavailable)

It is with great regret that we share this unfortunate news with our managers in Vietnam. We will update you with additional information if there are any changes to the availability of our service in Vietnam.

Thank you.
