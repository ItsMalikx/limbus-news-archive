March 7th Scheduled Update Notice

March 4, 2024
Identities & E.G.ONew ContentBalanceMain Story

Greetings, Dear Manager.

The server will enter scheduled maintenance from 10:00 [AM] through 12:00 [PM] on Mar. 7th, 2024 (KST).
Please make note of the maintenance schedule; we recommend that you refrain from entering combat or making in-app purchases and close the application before maintenance begins so that no data is lost.
Please refer below for details about the update.

New Content

1. New Target Extraction - [New Target Extraction: Liu South Section 4 Director Rodion & Section 4 Ryōshū] (Mar. 7th 12:00 ~ Mar. 21st 10:00 (KST))
Extraction rate increases for the following identities.
- 3★ : Liu South Section 4 Director Rodion
- 2★ : Liu South Section 4 Ryōshū

2. [Target Extraction: Ishmael] (Mar. 7th 12:00 ~ Mar. 21st 10:00 (KST))
The following Identities and E.G.O will have increased odds of extraction.
- E.G.O [HE] : Ardor Blossom Star Ishmael
- 3★ : The Pequod Captain Ishmael, Liu South Section 4 Ishmael, R Corp. 4th Pack Reindeer Ishmael
- 2★ : Lobotomy E.G.O::Sloshing Ishmael, LCCB Assistant Manager Ishmael, Shi South Section 5 Ishmael
- 1★ : LCB Sinner Ishmael

3. New Identities Added
The following are added to the list of extractable Identities.
- 3★ : Liu South Section 4 Director Rodion
- 2★ : Liu South Section 4 Ryōshū

Major Changes

1. Refraction Railway Line 3 clear record recovery for erroneously marked records (marked as 0 turns)

The data error [Issue where the Refraction Railway clear record is displayed as 0 Turns], as informed with the Feb. 2nd Notice, will be corrected.

- Changed Content: Affected Managers' records displayed on their respective Banners & logs will now reflect the correct value. We will withdraw the relevant reward from Managers who have unlocked the sub-100 turns reward due to this error.

We will notify affected Managers(who will have their records changed or will have their sub-100 turns reward withdrawn) separately via the in-game inbox.

▶ Fixed an issue where Uptying/Threadspinning Cutscenes don't play the chain-breaking animation properly

▶ (iOS) Fixed an issue where certain vfx for 'E.G.O Bg - Yield My Flesh to Claim Their Bones - Special Ticket' does not display properly

▶ (iOS) Fixed an issue where, in Focused Encounters, the chaining arrow does not display properly

▶ (iOS) Fixed an issue where the cutscene texts are missing after certain story Encounters

▶ Fixed an issue where an acquired Announcer does not immediately appear on the relevant loadout menus

▶ Fixed an issue where R Corp. 4th Pack Rhino Meursault's Skill 3 caused enemies to fly up to an abnormal coordinate

▶ (EN Only) Fixed typos in Canto IV Main Story dungeon stage descriptions

▶ (EN Only) Fixed typos for certain character names in Canto V credits

Maintenance & Application Update Compensation

- Content: Lunacy x300
- Recipients: All users whose game accounts were created before 10:00 Mar. 14th, 2024 (KST)
- When: After Mar. 7th, 2024 (Thu) 12:00 (KST)
- Can be claimed once per account

Maintenance Compensation can be claimed until Mar. 14th (Thu) 2024, 10:00 (KST) via the in-game Mailbox. (Mail available for 7 days)

Notes

- Please update your game client to the newest version on the respective store (Google Play/App Store/Steam).
- Forcibly shutting down the game while it is downloading resources may cause the download to fail, so please be careful not to close the application during the download.
- You will not be able to play <Limbus Company> during maintenance. Please understand.
- The maintenance schedule may suddenly change depending on the status of maintenance. If such a change occurs, we will let you know in a follow-up notice.
- A resource download may be prompted after the build update. If you're using a mobile device, it is recommended to proceed with a Wi-Fi connection.
- If you're playing with a guest account, we recommend that you link your account with other platforms to prevent possible account loss.
- The maintenance compensation will be provided once per account. Please note that the distribution may be slow due to network delays or other causes.
- The update gifts are valid to claim for 7 days. Please redeem them within this timeframe.

Thank you.
