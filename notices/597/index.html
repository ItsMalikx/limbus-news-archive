Notice: Korean App Store Package Price Error and Temporary Sales Suspension

May 28, 2026

Hello. This is Project Moon.

As of May 28th, 2026, 00:00 (KST), the prices of packages sold on the Korean App Store have increased.
In order to adjust the prices of the affected packages, sales of certain packages will be temporarily suspended.

At present, we are using the US Dollar (USD) as the base currency for in-app purchases.
In accordance with Apple's policy, package prices for Korean Won (KRW) payments were automatically increased on May 28th due to price equalization with the base currency.

For the affected packages, we plan to resume sales after adjusting the KRW prices on the App Store to match the package prices on Steam and Android.

We will proceed with this correction as quickly as possible.
Once the correction is complete, we will provide another notice outlining the follow-up measures.

Please refer below for more information.
