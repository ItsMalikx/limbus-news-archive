2024, Dec. 19th Scheduled Update Notice

2024-12-16

---------------------------------------

Greetings, Dear Manager.

The server will enter scheduled maintenance from 10:00 [AM] through 12:00 [PM] on Dec. 19th, 2024 (KST).

Please make note of the maintenance schedule; we recommend that you refrain from entering combat or making in-app purchases and close the application before maintenance begins so that no data is lost.

Please refer below for details about the update.

Major Changes
-------------

1. The color for equipped Wrath E.G.Os in the Dashboard UI has been changed.

- The equipped E.G.O indicator surrounding the Sinner portrait on the dashboard
- The Sin Affinity color display around the Passive text when an E.G.O Passive activates

2. Buffs or Debuffs with overly long texts under combat sprites have been adjusted to be cut off in the middle with ellipses when hovering over those effect icons

Bug Fixes & Improvements
------------------------

► Fixed an issue where the Sin Affinity Resistance to Sloth appears incorrectly when a unit is "immune" to it

► Fixed an issue where, if multiple effects were selected in the Mirror Dungeon - Grace of the Dreaming Star, the relevant SFX would play multiple times overlayed over one another

► Fixed an issue where, when selecting Grace of Stars in the Mirror Dungeon, the page turn dot indicator may appear incorrectly

► Fixed an issue where, when selecting Grace of Stars in the Mirror Dungeon, the forced activation of the auto-scroll function made it difficult for players to read the effect texts

► Fixed an issue where, in the Mirror Dungeon Theme Pack "The Unchanging", the image resources for certain Abnormality Battle Encounters were incorrect

► Fixed a soft-locking issue in Canto 7 related Theme Packs in the Mirror Dungeon

► Fixed 2 issues with a boss enemy appearing in Mirror Dungeon Theme Packs related to [Canto 4 - Part 3]

- An issue where the boss summons an incorrect number of summoned units
- An issue where the boss' max HP does not match the boss phase they're in (Their "current HP", however, matches the phase they're in)

► Fixed an issue where, in Mirror Dungeon Theme Pack "Line 1", dying to Bleed damage while the Abnormality kqe-1j-23 is using the Skill "Processing Transfer Reg" causes relevant resources in the forced Stagger effect to remain

► Fixed an issue where The Manager of La Manchaland Don Quixote's Buff 'Responsibility' was missing its relevant icon resource

► Fixed a typo in The Manager of La Manchaland Don Quixote's empowered Skill 1

- Before: Variant Sancho Hardblood Arts 9 - Whip
- After: Variant Sancho Hardblood Arts 6 - Whip

Bug Fixes & Improvements
------------------------

► Fixed an issue where the relevant effect text does not appear when using a Skill with both normal Coins and Unbreakable Coins : attacking with an Unbreakable Coin after Clash lose does not show the relevant effect texts applied with the Unbreakable Coins

► Fixed an issue where the eye animation for The Manager of La Manchaland Don Quixote's CG art was awkward

► Addressed the issue where the "Resolution adjustment in combat gameplay" applied with the 2024.12.12 Scheduled Update does not work as intended

► Fixed an issue where attempting to Enhance an E.G.O Gift or Refresh in a Store in Story Mirror Dungeons (e.g. Stage 3.5-9) causes an error message to appear

Known Issues
------------

► An issue where certain resources linger when the target is defeated when clashing against The Manager of La Manchaland Don Quixote's Empowered Skills

► An issue where, in the Identity Story, certain Identities' CG art has misaligned outlines

Maintenance & Application Update Compensation
----------------------------------------------

- Content : Lunacy x300

- Recipients : All users whose game accounts were created before 10:00 Dec. 26th, 2024 (KST)

- When : After Dec. 19th, 2024 (Thu) 12:00 (KST)

- Can be claimed once per account

Maintenance Compensation can be claimed until Dec. 26th (Thu) 2024, 10:00 (KST) via the in-game Mailbox. (Mail available for 7 days)

Notes
-----

- Please update your game client to the newest version on the respective store (Google Play/App Store/Steam).

- Forcibly shutting down the game while it is downloading resources may cause the download to fail, so please be careful not to close the application during the download.

- You will not be able to play <Limbus Company> during maintenance. Please understand.

- The maintenance schedule may suddenly change depending on the status of maintenance. If such a change occurs, we will let you know in a follow-up notice.

- A resource download may be prompted after the build update. If you're using a mobile device, it is recommended to proceed with a Wi-Fi connection.

- If you're playing with a guest account, we recommend that you link your account with other platforms to prevent possible account loss.

- The maintenance compensation will be provided once per account. Please note that the distribution may be slow due to network delays or other causes.

- The update gifts are valid to claim for 7 days. Please redeem them within this timeframe.

Thank you.
