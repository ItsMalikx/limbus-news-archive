    Regarding Schemacraft and E.G.O Synergies Going Forward.

    November 27, 2026
    Identities & E.G.O

    Hello.
    This is Jihoon Kim, CEO of Project Moon.

    First, I would like to begin by apologizing for the anxiety and confusion I have caused with the sudden synergy update direction announcement.

    The ultimate purpose of Schemacraft was to be endgame content where the player is able to enjoy deckbuilding after obtaining and upgrading the full roster of Identities and E.G.O. With that purpose in mind, we wanted to add a universally applicable synergy to each E.G.O's Threadspinning Tier 5, so that it is usable in content not limited to Schemacraft.

    However, I did not deeply consider the fact that the current version of Schemacraft is far from reaching that potential in both content and structure; I also did not consider how unreasonable it may feel to Threadspin E.G.O to Tier 5 just to enjoy something new in a beta access version of gameplay content.

    After an internal discussion, we have decided to cancel the changes shared with the Nov. 26th update, "[Notice: Upcoming Updates to Identity Trait Keywords and E.G.O Synergies]", and instead apply the contents of this notice.

    Also, any information regarding E.G.O Threadspinning Tier 5 that was set to be shared with the Nov. 30th update notice, as stated in the Nov. 26th notice, will be shared in this notice instead.

    The following is our direction going forward regarding Schemacraft, E.G.O Threadspinning Tier 5, and synergies.

    ※ There are two types of "Synergies" to be discussed in this notice.
    (1) Schemacraft-exclusive Synergy: Its effects activate exclusively within Schemacraft.
    (2) E.G.O Synergy: Its effects are added to Threadspinning Tiers 4 & 5, and are separate from the effects of (1).

    [1. We will design "Schemacraft-exclusive synergies".]

    Schemacraft-exclusive synergies will have a two-pronged approach: stats and visuals. They will empower team compositions with specific themes or give them a more unique characteristic.
    - Stat improvement effects for certain Team compositions (e.g.: empowering Liu Assoc. teams, Kurokumo Clan teams)
    - Improved visual effects for certain Team compositions (e.g.: 9:2 E.G.O will empower N Corp. Teams. In addition, having a certain number of N Corp. Identities will have bonus visual flair such as firing nails during or after the E.G.O animation)

    [2. Schemacraft-exclusive synergy effects can be activated by either equipping E.G.O (of Threadspin Tier 3 or higher) or using them.]
    - Schemacraft-exclusive synergy effects will be added to E.G.O Threadspinning Tier 3.
    - Schemacraft-exclusive synergy effects will activate only within the Schemacraft gameplay mode.

    [3. Players can now temporarily Threadspin E.G.O within a Schemacraft run.]

    - Just as Identities can be Uptied as you progress through Schemacraft, E.G.O can now be Threadspun up to Tier 5 throughout a Schemacraft run. Upon acquisition within Schemacraft, your current E.G.O Threadspin tier will be applied as-is, just as it is now.
    - E.g.: During Schemacraft, if you were to equip an E.G.O obtained by your account that is at Tier 1, you may Threadspin it. Threadspinning it to Tier 3 within Schemacraft will enable you to activate the Schemacraft-exclusive synergy.
    - Even if you were to obtain, in a run of Schemacraft, an unowned E.G.O, it can be Threadspun throughout your progress in that run and have its "Schemacraft-exclusive synergy" activated.
    - We will design the activation condition for each synergy effect to be diverse and appropriate for the concept of each E.G.O.

    4. Threadspin Tier 5 for E.G.O that were early-releases will enhance their unique traits or empower them. The considerations for these changes will be separate from Schemacraft.]
    - The aforementioned "Schemacraft-exclusive synergy" and "E.G.O synergy" will be separate mechanics.
    - Though we wanted the role of E.G.O to be an "ultimate move" to turn the tide of battle, their current design is generally weaker than empowered Skill 3s of the newest Identities, and is not as appealing to use. As we upgrade each E.G.O's versatility and power, we will try our best to minimize any possible discomfort coming from their direction.
    - We will not be changing the recently released 4th Match Flame synergy. Our intent was to make each E.G.O more meaningful than they are now, and to have them play a more prominent role in teambuilding discussions. (we are currently looking at the "Shadow-vested Bladesinger" E.G.O as an ideal form of that role)
    - Activating the effects of the "4th Match Flame E.G.O synergy" requires that the user meet the team composition requirements, consume a large amount of E.G.O resources, and SP. As such, we decided that its current damage output is appropriate.
    - We are planning to design future E.G.O Threadspinning Tier 5 with conditions, power-ups, and cost-to-payoff ratio comparable to the current version of the 4th Match Flame E.G.O.

    [5. The diversification and subdivision of trait keywords, as mentioned in the livestream and our notices, will not be applied with this update.]
    - The initial intent was to use the subdivision of trait keywords to diversify the types of teams. However, I now realize that the decision was made too hastily, and that now is too premature a time to start considering such mechanics. It was something we should have approached after reinforcing the foundations of the gameplay mode.
    - Even if we were to implement these changes later, we will first have a more thorough internal design discussion before deciding how or if it would be applied to our game

    [6. In addition to the "Schemacraft-exclusive synergies", we will implement structural and QoL changes to Schemacraft to provide means of acquiring E.G.O or Identities you want.]

    <Example>

    - Using a Randomized Team gives extra Cost; you may use that extra Cost to equip E.G.O you would like to add to your team before entering Schemacraft. (E.G.O Cost will be determined by their Risk Levels and release Seasons)
    - Spend Schemacraft-specific currencies such as "Universal Slivers", to narrow the pool of possible Identities or E.G.O to an increasingly specific category of your choice. (Spending more currency will narrow the pool further, increasing the chance of obtaining the result of your choice)

    Based on the results of the survey scheduled to begin Dec. 3rd, we will inform you again of the hows and whats of our improvement plans as soon as we are ready.

    As many of our players have suggested, we will design "Schemacraft-exclusive synergy" to more aggressively empower older Identities in the gameplay mode and make them more appealing and exciting to use.

    We will make sure that the rollout of "Schemacraft-exclusive synergy", even if it may initially be slow and smaller in scope, improves the fun factor of our Schemacraft gameplay mode and does not affect the general schedule of our roadmap.

    Going forward, I will more carefully review our direction before sharing it publicly, and try my best to look at it from the players' point of view.

    Once again, I sincerely apologize for the concerns and confusion I have caused.
