    Notice: Identities with Uncraftable Façade

    March 10, 2026
    Identities & E.G.O

    Hello, this is Project Moon.

    We have previously announced that the "Façade Projection" feature will be added with the Mar. 12th Scheduled Update.

    However, since we did not include the full list of Identities whose Façade is not craftable, we will provide the additional information in this notice.
    Please refer below for the list of Identities whose Façade is not craftable.

    [ List of Identities Whose Façade is Not Craftable (Total: 5 Identities) ]

    1★: LCB Sinner Ryōshū, LCB Sinner Heathcliff
    3★: The House of Spiders: The Index Nursefather Yi Sang, Lobotomy E.G.O::Faint Aroma & Solitude Ryōshū, Lobotomy E.G.O::Hornet 【Alteration】 Meursault

    ※ The above are Identities whose "Weave Projection" feature is not available.
    ※ Please note that it is still possible to apply another Identity's Façade onto these Identities when they are in the team loadout.
    ※ We will work to make Façade crafting available for these Identities in a future update.

    Thank you.
