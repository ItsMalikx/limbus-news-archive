New Content - Mirror of Immortality Parallel Superposition Mode & Achievements

August 4, 2025
Mirror DungeonNew Content

Greetings, dear Manager.

New Content <Mirror of Immortality Parallel Superposition Mode & Achievements>
will be added to the game with the Aug. 7th, 2025 (KST) Scheduled Update.

※ For the Mirror of Immortality Parallel Superposition mode and Achievements implementation, Mirror Dungeon entry will
be blocked for the following period: 2025.08.07 06:00 ~ 12:00
Please note this information for your gameplay plans.

<Description>

- Engage the “Mirror of the Dreaming - Parallel Superposition Mode” to expand the dungeon to 10 Floors.

- Mirror Dungeon Achievements have been added to the game.
Complete Achievements to advance on the Achievements Progress bar and receive respective rewards based on your progress.

Parallel Superposition Mode

- Just like the Mirror Dungeon Hard Difficulty, Parallel Superposition Mode is unlocked upon clearing Canto 7.

- Engage the Parallel Superposition Mode to expand the Mirror Dungeon to 10 Floors.

- Parallel Superposition mode presents a difficult level of challenge and exists only for challenge runs or completing Achievements.

- Clearing higher floors in the Parallel Superposition mode awards more Starlight rewards.

- Please note that the Pass EXP or Lunacy rewards for clearing Floors above Floor 6 or higher are no different from clearing Floor 5.

- You may engage the Parallel Superposition Mode by toggling on the Infinity Mirror button at the entrance of the Mirror Dungeon or by using the toggle button on the right side of the Dungeon Progress(Settings) pop-up window.

- In order to enter the Parallel Superposition Mode after clearing Floor 5, you had to have finished every Floor Theme Pack up to Floor 5 of that run in Hard difficulty.

- Please note that Mirror of Immortality runs that were started using Rental Teams will only have 5 floors available.

- Once you clear Floor 5 while the Parallel Superposition Mode is engaged, the game will ask if you would like to enter the Parallel Superposition mode and ascend to Floor 6.
Confirm to enter the Parallel Superposition mode and ascend to Floor 6. Cancel to end your run at 'Floor 5 Cleared' state.
Please note that, once you've cleared Floor 5 and moved on to higher floors, failing or giving up midway through the run will still yield the same rewards as clearing Floor 5.

Mounting Trials

- The trials will get increasingly stronger the higher you rise.

- Parallel Superposition Mode is a challenging content.
Enemies may become incredibly powerful or sturdy once they’ve gained just the right combination of Trials.

- If continuing appears to be difficult, it may be a good idea to forfeit the dungeon for now and try again with a different combination of Mounting Trials and E.G.O Gifts, Grace of the Immortal Star, or use the E.G.O Gift Search function.

Achievements

- You can gain Achievements Progress by clearing Mirror Dungeon achievements.

- Mirror Dungeon floors are counted as 'cleared' only when the player consumed Enkephalin Modules in the Reward Claim window.
Please note this information before attempting to complete an Achievement tied to clearing the Mirror Dungeon.

- Please note that future updates will bring even more Achievements to the game.

- Please note that Mirror of Immortality Achievements & Achievement Reward claims are available only until the Mirror Dungeon #7 Update.

- As long as you meet the conditions, you can gain Mirror Dungeon Achievements progress or complete Achievements even when clearing the Mirror Dungeon with Rental Teams.

- Obtain various rewards based on your Achievements Progress.

- Achievements added in the future will bring additional rewards as well.
