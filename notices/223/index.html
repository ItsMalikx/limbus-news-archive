May 2nd Scheduled Update Notice

April 29, 2024
Identities & E.G.OEventsMirror DungeonNew Content

Greetings Dear Manager.

The server will enter scheduled maintenance from 10:00 [AM] through 12:00 [PM] on May 2nd, 2024 (KST). Please make note of the maintenance schedule; we recommend that you refrain from entering combat or making in-app purchases and close the application before maintenance begins so that no data is lost. Please refer below for details about the update.

※ Note
Major changes to Mirror Dungeon systems will be deployed after the Scheduled Update. Therefore, with the end of Scheduled Maintenance at 12:00, your Mirror Dungeon Weekly Bonus Count and your current Mirror Dungeon run progress will be reset. Rewards for Mirror Dungeon runs that remain incomplete before May 2nd, 10:00 will become inaccessible with the progress reset, so please claim your rewards BEFORE the Scheduled Maintenance begins.
