    Notice: Known Issues After the May 28th, 2026 Scheduled Update

    May 27, 2026

    Hello, this is Project Moon.

    We would like to inform you about the known issues identified after the May 28th, 2026 Scheduled Update.

    The following issues are currently being investigated, and we will share further information, such as causes, fixes, or Hotfix ETA, in a future notice.

    Please continue reading for more information.

    [ Known Issues ]

    ▶ (AOS) An issue where Limbus Company's App icon appears differently
    ※ This issue was caused by AOS icon settings missing in the Unity engine update process.

    ▶ (Steam) An issue where the game's resolution cannot be freely adjusted

    ▶ An issue where "Sinnerlings' Chicken-scratch Farewells" item does not show up in the players' inventory
    ※ Please note that the item is not lost and is still in your accounts, only hidden from the inventory display.

    ▶ An issue where certain interactions such as viewing Stage info, accessing Mirror Dungeon Shops, or entering Choice Events could cause the game to lag briefly

    ▶ (AOS) An issue where a black screen appears upon launching Limbus Company, blocking game launch
    ※ For Managers experiencing this issue, please submit an inquiry to the Customer Support email address below along with your device information. We will collect the information and investigate the issue as quickly as possible.

    limbussupport@projmoon.com
