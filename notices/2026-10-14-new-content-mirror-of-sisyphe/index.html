Oct. 15th New Content - Mirror of Sisyphe

October 14, 2026
Identities & E.G.OMirror DungeonEventsNew Content

Greetings, Dear Manager.

The new content Mirror Dungeon 8 「Mirror of Sisyphe」 will be added with the Oct. 15th, 2026 (KST) Scheduled Update.

▶ Date : After 26/10/15 (KST) Scheduled Update

▶ Requirements Normal Difficulty: Clear Main Story Chapter 2 Hard Difficulty: Clear Main Story Chapter 9

▶ How to Activate Hard Mode: From the Theme Pack selection menu, select the "Difficulty Toggle" button to switch between Hard and Normal difficulties

▶ How to enter Parallel Superposition Mode:
Clear 1F ~ 5F with the Parallel Superposition Mode toggled ON

▶ How to enter EXTREME Mode: Clear up to 10F with the Parallel Superposition Mode toggled ON

Notes

1. With the introduction of Mirror Dungeon #8 「Mirror of Sisyphe」, Mirror Dungeon #7 [Mirror of Names and Spiders] will be closed.
※ Theme Pack Compendium recorded from Mirror Dungeon #7 will not be reset.

2. With the Oct. 15th Scheduled Update, Normal, Hard, Parallel Superposition, and EXTREME Modes will be updated simultaneously for 「Mirror of Sisyphe」.

3. 4 new Theme Packs, 8 new E.G.O Gifts, and 6 new Choice Events will be added.

4. Once Mirror of Sisyphe updates, any Starlight acquired in Mirror of Names and Spiders will be removed and will not carry over. A certain amount of starting Starlight will be given by default so that all Managers can start using the Starlight system immediately.

5.26/10/15 (KST) Scheduled Update Maintenance will last from 06:00 ~ 12:00.
Major changes to Mirror Dungeon systems will be deployed after the Scheduled Update. Therefore, with the end of Scheduled Maintenance at 12:00, your Mirror Dungeon Weekly Bonus eligibility and your current Mirror Dungeon run progress will be reset. Rewards for Mirror Dungeon runs that remain incomplete before Oct 15th, 06:00 will become inaccessible with the progress reset, so please claim your rewards BEFORE the Scheduled Update Maintenance begins.

6. 「Mirror of Names and Spiders」 Achievements progress and rewards will become unavailable when 「Mirror of Sisyphe」 is updated.
Please take note of this information to claim your rewards BEFORE the Oct. 15th Scheduled Maintenance.
※ Please note that Mirror Dungeon #8 「Mirror of Sisyphe」 Mirror Projection / Achievements will be updated at a later date.

7. Hidden Bosses featured in the EXTREME Mode will be updated at a later date.
※ Hidden Boss E.G.O Gifts obtainable from Mirror Dungeon #7 EXTREME will have their specs partially adjusted when the Hidden Boss roster is changed at a later date.

Progression

Entering the dungeon, as before, does not cost any resources. However, you can spend Enkephalin modules at the end of your exploration to receive rewards.

<Major Changes>

1. Enemy levels will be raised to Level 65.

2. 4 new Theme Packs and 8 new E.G.O Gifts will be added.
※ This update features 1 new Theme Pack for the Normal
difficulty, 3 new Theme Packs exclusive to the Hard difficulty, and 8 new E.G.O Gifts.

Creating your TEAM

In Normal Difficulty, your Identity Levels will be fixed at
65. In Hard Difficulty, your actual Identity Levels will be reflected.

※ Your current Uptie & E.G.O Threadspinning Tiers will be reflected in the Mirror Dungeon regardless of difficulty, just as they were in the previous Mirror Dungeons.

Grace of the Sisyphean Star

The 「Grace of the Star of Names and Spiders」 will be replaced with 「Grace of the Sisyphean Star」. From 「Grace of the Sisyphean Star」, you can obtain Grace of Stars with special effects by spending Starlight.

Select the "Select All Graces" button to select all graces at once. Select the "+" or "++" buttons below the "Enhance All" button to enhance all graces at once.
※ Please note that choosing either of the "Enhance All" buttons does not select the said graces; either select the "Select All Graces" button or manually select Graces of your choice.

※ If you do not purchase the Grace of Stars, you can obtain the remaining sum of Starlight, either from the Starlight Bonus of your Team or the base sum, as a reward upon clearing the Mirror Dungeon run.

※ Selecting Grace of Stars consumes your starting Starlight & Starlight Bonuses first, and will tap into your Starlight reserve only when you have exceeded that amount.

※ You may not be able to select Grace of Stars if you have insufficient Starlight & Starlight Bonuses.

Starting E.G.O Gifts

Upon entering the Mirror Dungeon, players will be presented with E.G.O Gift Keyword categories to choose from. Starting E.G.O Gifts are fixed for each Keyword, and you can select as many E.G.O Gifts as you are allowed to select within each category. Also, the E.G.O Gift of the Keyword category you select here will appear more frequently during the Mirror Dungeon run.

E.G.O Gift Search

After selecting your Starting E.G.O Gifts, you will be brought to the "E.G.O Gift Search" screen, where you can obtain up to 3 additional E.G.O Gifts (Tier 3 or below) of your choice. Each E.G.O Gift selection will increase the price of the next choice.

※ E.G.O Gift Search becomes available only when the "Activate Gift Search" option is toggled ON in your Starting E.G.O Gift selection menu.

Selecting Theme Packs

Select a Dungeon Theme to explore upon entering a new floor. Choose a Theme Pack by selecting the pack of your choice and dragging it downward.

Select the difficulty switch button at the top-right part of the screen to toggle between Normal and Hard difficulties. Certain Theme Packs featured in the Hard difficulty pose a significantly higher difficulty compared to those found in the Normal difficulty. Hard Difficulty shows a total of 4 E.G.O Gift Clear Bonuses.
※ Once Hard difficulty has been toggled on, you cannot switch back to Normal difficulty.

<Major Changes>

1. 4 new Theme Packs and 8 new E.G.O Gifts will be added.
※ This update features 1 new Theme Pack for the Normal difficulty, 3 new Theme Packs exclusive to the Hard difficulty, and 8 new E.G.O Gifts.

2. The new Theme Packs will add a total of 6 new Choice Events.
New E.G.O Gifts can be obtained from the said Choice Events.

Theme Pack Search

This is the "Theme Pack Search" function, accessible by pressing the "Search" button. Using this function, you can spend Starlight to search for and select a Theme Pack of your choice. However, please note that the selectable Theme Pack pool is limited to what is available from your current Floor and Difficulty.

※ Theme Pack Observation fee rises with every use.

※ Searching for and selecting a new, previously unaccessed Theme Pack costs 50% more Starlight.

Encounters

After selecting the Theme Pack of your choice, select a Stage node to begin Combat Encounters or Choice Events.

In the Hard difficulty, only up to 2 nodes from the current position can be viewed.

Shops

You can spend your Cost at the Shops to purchase E.G.O Gifts or use one of its many useful services for progressing through the Mirror Dungeon.

There is a chance for Super Shops to appear in Hard Difficulty Theme Packs. The more Cost you spend in a Shop, the greater the chance for a Super Shop to spawn on the next Floor.

The following are the benefits provided by Super Shops.
- E.G.O Gift Shop Selection +3
- Skill Replacement Opportunity +1 (cannot replace the Skill of the same Identity again)
- Access to "ID Skill Search" option, which lets you choose which Identity's Skills you want to replace
- Can fuse up to 5 E.G.O Gifts at once in Fusion
- Greater chance for higher Tier E.G.O Gifts to appear when Fusing (Fusion Point Modifier)

Replacing Skills

You can spend a certain amount of Cost to replace the Skills of Identities in your loadout. However, if you have completely removed a Skill in favor of another in previous Shops, that Skill can no longer be replaced.

※ In "Super Shops" that may spawn in Hard Difficulty, Skill Replacement for 2 Identities will now be shown at once instead of appearing one after another. Also, "Identity Skill Search," a new function to pick and choose which Identity's Skill you would like to replace, will become available.

3★ Skill Replacement can only replace Skills within the same Identity, even when using the ID Skill Search function.

※ Using ID Skill Search to replace Skills is more expensive than standard Skill Replacement.

※ ID Skill Search cost is fixed, and is not affected by modifiers from Grace of Stars or E.G.O Gifts.

Wishmaking

Through "Wishmaking," you can spend Starlight to improve your Fusion chances.

※ Each instance of Wishmaking uses the same amount of Starlight regardless of how many times it has been activated.

※ Depending on the number of E.G.O Gifts used, the forecast maxes out at 99.99%.

Encounter Reward Cards

Each Encounter has a certain chance to provide you with a selection of "Encounter Reward Cards". Higher-difficulty Mirror Dungeons, higher Floors, and higher-difficulty Encounters will yield higher-value rewards.

Trials upon each Floor Completion

Clear the Floor to gain an E.G.O Gift of your choice. Selecting an E.G.O Gift will give you its linked "Mounting Trials" and Enemy level-up effects.

You can select 2 Reward E.G.O Gifts upon clearing a Hard Difficulty Theme Pack. However, please note that you will gain 2 Mounting Trials linked to each E.G.O Gift as well.

※ Should you refuse to select any E.G.O Gifts by selecting the "Refuse Gift" button, a number of the given Mounting Trials will be randomly selected.

Parallel Superposition & EXTREME Modes

Clearing IF ~ 5F of the Mirror Dungeon on Hard difficulty unlocks access to Mirror Dungeon Parallel Superposition & EXTREME Modes, which present an even higher level of challenge. In Mirror of Sisyphe, Parallel Superposition lasts from 6F ~ 10F, and EXTREME from 11F ~ 15F, presenting even further empowered enemies.

The combination of Mounting Trials and Adversities in Mirror of Sisyphe EXTREME mode will greatly empower the enemies; the higher your Floor, the more Starlight and Projection you will obtain.

※ Please note that Parallel Superposition and EXTREME modes present a high level of difficulty and exist for purposes of challenge runs or for clearing Achievements, which will be added with a future update.

EXTREME Mode - Adversities

- For Parallel Superposition EXTREME, in addition to Mounting Trials applicable when entering Floor 11, you will have an option to set "Adversities" for yourself.

- Starting from Floor 11, up to 6 Adversities can be selected on each Floor. Just like Mounting Trials, Adversities will continue to compound instead of going away.

- Selecting more Adversities will net more Adversity rewards; you will gain additional Starlight based on your cumulative Adversity Score.

※ Adversities aren't mandatory; you can reach Floor 15 without setting any Adversities, which may still be a good strategy to experience the dungeon.

※ Mirror of Sisyphe Projection Levels can be updated only after the Mirror Projection / Achievement update, which will arrive at a later date.

Exploration Complete and Starlight Reward

Upon completion, you can view your statistics and progress of the relevant Mirror Dungeon run.

※ Starlight Rewards The following are ways to increase the amount of Starlight gained upon clearing a Mirror Dungeon run.

- Clearing at higher Floors gives more Starlight.
- Clearing in Hard difficulty gives more Starlight.
- Clearing Refraction Railway Theme Pack gives more Starlight.
- Select Starlight Encounter Reward Cards to gain additional Starlight.
- Higher Starlight Bonus in the Team loadout window gives a better Starlight multiplier.

<Major Changes>

1. Any Starlight acquired in Mirror Dungeon #7 will be removed and will not carry over.
A certain amount of starting Starlight will be given by default so that all Managers can start using the Starlight system immediately.

[Other Sources of Starlight]

Starlight, which is a Mirror Dungeon resource, can be obtained via Extractions and Yisang Exchanges as well.

※ Other sources of Starlight (There is a single, first-time opportunity to gain Starlight from one of the following conditions):

- Obtain a newly released 3★ Identity or E.G.O from New Target Extractions.
- Within a certain period of time, obtain a newly released 3★ Identity or E.G.O from Extractions.
- Within a certain period of time, obtain a newly released 3★ Identity or E.G.O from Yisang Exchange.
- Within a certain period of time, obtain a newly released 3★ Identity or E.G.O from Extraction or Yisang Exchange after obtaining it from the Dispenser.

※ What are not Starlight acquisition conditions:
- Obtaining a newly released Identity or E.G.O from the Dispenser.
- Extracting the same newly released Identity or E.G.O for the second time or obtaining it from Yisang Exchange for the second time or more.
- Obtaining the said Identity or E.G.O outside the certain period of time.

[Weekly Bonuses]

Mirror Dungeon Hard Difficulty with Weekly Bonuses yields more Battle Pass EXP compared to Mirror Dungeon Normal. Your reward will be based on the number of Floors you cleared and the Difficulties you selected for each Floor.

※ However, without Weekly Bonuses, the clear reward between Hard and Normal is identical, just as it had been in the previous Mirror Dungeons.

If you have selected the Hard Difficulty for every floor, you can use two or more Weekly Bonuses at once. The maximum obtainable Reward per week is identical to the amount obtained from clearing Mirror Dungeon #7 (Mirror of Names and Spiders) Hard Difficulty Floor 5.

- Using 3 Weekly Bonuses all at once: Lunacy x750, Battle Pass EXP x225
- Using 3 Weekly Bonuses 3 times, one at a time: Lunacy x750, Battle Pass EXP 250

Upon clearing the Mirror Dungeon, based on the number of Theme Packs cleared in the Hard Difficulty, your Starlight reward will be greatly increased. Also, clearing Refraction Railway Theme Packs, which are exclusive to Hard Difficulty runs, will each yield bonus Starlight as well.

※ While Weekly Bonuses can be used for runs cleared before reaching 5F, please note that your Weekly Bonus will be reduced based on the number of floors you did not clear.

E.G.O Gifts

<Major Changes>

1. 8 new E.G.O Gifts will be added.

※ 2 new E.G.O Gifts are obtainable from both Normal and Hard, and 6 new E.G.O Gifts are exclusive to the Hard Difficulty.

2. Keywords of certain E.G.O Gifts will be adjusted.
→ Relevant E.G.O Gifts: Universal Instinct, Universal Skeleton
- Before: They were categorized as Keywordless Gifts
- After: They are now Bleed Gifts

3. Theme Pack availability for a certain E.G.O Gift will be adjusted.
→ Relevant E.G.O Gift: WB Flask
- Before: Featured in all Theme Packs
- After: La Manchaland Re-opening, The Infinite Procession, The Dream Ending, Who Couldn't be Bloodfiends, Blessed Carnival, and The Master of La Manchaland

4. The E.G.O Gift "Blue Starshard" has been identified as having unintentional effects, so its description will be adjusted.
→ Relevant Effect: - When dealing damage against the affected enemies with Skills that apply Sinking, Sinking Count, or Unique Sinking, inflict X Defense Level Down (Y times per turn)
- Before: This effect inflicts Defense Level Down next turn.
- Updated the description with "inflict X Defense Level Down next turn" to match its behavior

5. The E.G.O Gift "Searing Brass" description will be updated.
- Before: When an enemy takes Burn damage, deal Wrath damage equal to Burn Potency
- After: When an enemy takes Burn damage due to Burn activation, deal Wrath damage equal to Burn Potency

<Major Changes>

6. Certain E.G.O Gift effects not working as intended will be fixed.

(1) Dimensional Perception Modifier
- Before: The "Discard" effect trigger checked for Discards activated by E.G.O Gifts as well
- After: Fixed the E.G.O Gift behavior so that it does not trigger for Discards activated by E.G.O Gifts

(2) Purloined Flame
→ Relevant Effects On Hit with a Skill(including E.G.O Skills): if the target has 3+ Burn Count, activate Burn once on the target. (target loses 1 Burn Count; activates once per Skill) Turn End: targets with 3+ Burn Count activate Burn one more time (target loses 1 Burn Count)
- Before: Instead of activating Burn, it was "dealing damage equal to Burn Potency."
- After: This effect now activates Burn, as described

(3) Charge-type Gloves Keyword "Near-light Superbattery"
→ Relevant Effect: When this unit consumes Charge Count with its Skills, consume the same amount of Charge Count 1 more time (when there isn't enough Charge Count, consume as much as available)
- Before: This effect consumed more Charge Count than the actual number of Charge Count available on the affected unit
- After: This effect now consumes as much Charge Count as available, as described

(4) Torn Bandolier
→ Relevant Effect 1: Pierce Skills gain Clash Power +1
- Before: This effect activated only on Pierce Base Attack Skills
- After: Now, this effect activates for all Pierce Attack Skills, as described
→ Skills that consume Ammo deal +(X/# of Coins)% damage
- Before: When an Identity that does not have Ammo used an E.G.O Skill that spends Ammo, this E.G.O Gift effect did not trigger
- After: This E.G.O Gift effect now triggers for E.G.O Skills that gain/spend Ammo

※ More E.G.O Gift adjustments are planned for the future.

※ Future E.G.O Gift adjustment details will be shared in notices for each respective adjustment update.
