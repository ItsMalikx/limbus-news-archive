    The direction of our E.G.O Threadspinning Tier 5 design will be as follows.

    November 26, 2026
    Identities & E.G.O

    This is Jihoon Kim, CEO of Project Moon.

    I sincerely apologize for the anxiety, concerns, and disappointment we have caused you due to today's Schemacraft update and its direction going forward.

    I promise to keep both my ears open to all of your voices and feedback regarding not only the Schemacraft experience and its future direction, but also the effects that E.G.O Threadspinning Tier 5 synergies could have.

    I also promise to make Schemacraft as enjoyable as I am able to make it while ensuring it has as little effect as possible on our story content development process.

    Please, I would greatly appreciate your trust this one time.

    As the director, I believe the vision I have for Schemacraft as long-term content is something our game needs.

    Though the development time for the current version of Schemacraft has been brief, the picture of the gameplay experience I have painted in my heart is something I have cherished for a long, long time.

    I am sorry for my repeated failure to properly communicate my intentions and deliver updates.

    I want to emphasize that I am not trying to ignore our players' voices of concern.

    Still, if you could place your trust in me this once, I will do everything I can to make it as enjoyable an experience as it can be over the course of this Season.

    While there is indeed a vision of the gameplay experience I would like to pursue, I will remain receptive to feedback from our players and work toward finding the appropriate middle ground as we continue to develop the gameplay mode.

    In that process, I will continue to polish and update the gameplay mode to make it more enjoyable, so that our players' concerns do not come to pass.

    But if, even after my vision for Schemacraft has been realized, many of our players express dislike for the gameplay mode through the surveys, and if we have internally determined that the gameplay mode is at a dead end with no prospect for further improvements, I will not force it to carry over into the next Season.

    Of course, we will be working hard to make it as enjoyable as possible throughout this Season so that it does not come to that.

    Regarding E.G.O Threadspinning Tier 5, about which many of you have raised questions and expressed concerns, we will proceed with its design and implementation as follows.

    - We will revisit and revise the planned list of E.G.O for Threadspinning Tier 5 that we announced earlier. In this updated list, we will focus on E.G.O that have seen less use due to their weaker performance and are well suited to receiving both power-ups and synergy effects through Threadspinning Tier 5.

    - We will also make sure not to overlook the role of E.G.O as equipment that complements the performance and uniqueness of the Identities they are paired with. (e.g. Threadspinning Tier 5 update for Heathcliff's Binds E.G.O to complement the Wild Hunt Heathcliff Identity)

    - We are preparing synergy effects to visually highlight the unique characteristics and concepts of each E.G.O while providing meaningful upgrades. We will design them to provide benefits that are worth the resources required to upgrade them.

    - We will design E.G.O Threadspinning Tier 5 synergies so that they do not significantly disrupt the balance of existing content, while ensuring that, as more synergy effects are added with future updates, they become one of the enjoyable axes by which you may shape the direction of your deckbuilding plans within Schemacraft.

    - Through synergies, we wanted to enhance not only existing E.G.O, but also the enjoyment of using E.G.O as a whole by further developing their power, concepts, and visual presentation. We hope to gradually demonstrate this direction through future updates.

    - We would greatly appreciate your understanding that the E.G.O synergies we have announced are not intended to balance E.G.O solely around Schemacraft, but to enhance the enjoyment of the game as a whole in the long term by bringing out the unique characteristics of E.G.O and their respective Abnormalities while addressing their performance shortcomings.

    - We will properly consolidate our direction going forward and share it with you once again in the Nov. 30th (Mon) Scheduled Update.

    I sincerely apologize for the disappointment and anxiety I have caused everyone.
