2025, Oct. 23rd Scheduled Update Notice

2025-10-20

---------------------------------------

Greetings, Dear Manager.

The server will enter scheduled maintenance from 06:00 [AM] through 12:00 [PM] on Oct. 23rd, 2025 (KST).

Please make note of the maintenance schedule; we recommend that you refrain from entering combat or making in-app purchases and close the application before maintenance begins so that no data is lost.

Please refer below for details about the update.

※ Please note that this maintenance period will last from 10/23 06:00 ~ 12:00 (KST); please note this for your gameplay plans.

The Time-Limited Content [Pilgrimage of Compassion] Closure Early Notice
------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. The Time-Limited Content [Pilgrimage of Compassion] will be closed after the Oct. 23rd Scheduled Maintenance.
Please note that any Chapter Progression and Extra Conditions Fulfilled rewards cannot be claimed afterward; make sure to claim them before the Scheduled Maintenance begins.
※ While Normal difficulty Extra Conditions Fulfilled rewards are automatically claimed as well after clearing the Hard difficulty Extra Conditions, claiming Hard difficulty Chapter Progression rewards do not automatically claim Normal difficulty Chapter Progression rewards. Please ensure that you did not leave any Chapter Progression rewards unobtained.

2. [Pilgrimage of Compassion] content will be removed from the Drive & Theater menus.

3. After the Scheduled Maintenance, the Mirror Dungeon Theme Pack "Pilgrimage of Compassion" and its featured E.G.O Gifts will be removed.

4. [Sea Terror Sample] will no longer be obtainableafter this Scheduled Maintenance.
Please note that any remaining [Sea Terror Sample] in your inventory can still be used in the [Pilgrimage of Compassion] Reward Exchange menu until Oct. 30th Scheduled Update.

5. The New E.G.O Extraction [Pilgrimage of Compassion] will be closed after this Scheduled Maintenance.
Any un-exchanged Yisang[理想] will be converted to Threads once the New E.G.O Extraction [Pilgrimage of Compassion] closes. Please note that Unbrilliant Glory Gregor, Command : Meltdown Faust, and To Remain Oneself Hong Lu cannot be obtained from other Extractions once this Scheduled Maintenance ends, and will not be featured in the Dispenser either.

6. Sales of Amiya Announcer Pack will end after this Scheduled Maintenance.
