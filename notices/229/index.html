Notice: Mirror Dungeon Improvements

2024-05-02

-----------------------------------

※ The above improvements will gradually be applied with future updates. We will inform you via a notice as soon as each of them is updated.

Our goal in this round of Mirror Dungeon improvements was to increase the chances of obtaining E.G.O Gifts that you want.

The changes to fix the Starting E.G.O Gifts have limited the possibility of obtaining the E.G.O Gifts you want from the get-go. However, we realized that the only way to find the E.G.O. Gifts you want in the current system is to rely on the randomness of the Floor Theme Card Packs, which may have caused the players to feel that their Mirror Dungeon runs were aimless.

To address this issue, we seek to adjust the system so that the E.G.O Gift Keyword Category you picked at the start will affect the kind of Gifts you'll get during the Mirror Dungeon Run. We also plan to add a menu in the E.G.O Gift Fusion and the refresh option in the Shops so that you can choose which Keyword to use for your particular Mirror Dungeon run. With the above improvements, we seek to make it easier to control which Keyword you will obtain during the Mirror Dungeon runs.

Please refer to the information below for more details.

1. Mirror of the Wuthering - Simulation Difficulty is reverted back to 4 floors.

2. Now, after clearing the Floor Theme Boss, at least 1 E.G.O Gift of the Keyword Category you selected at the start of each Mirror Dungeon run will appear.
- However, if you have already obtained all E.G.O Gift Keywords for that category, the resulting E.G.O Gift Keyword may not be from that category.

3. Now, you can choose which Keyword you'd like to fuse your E.G.O Gifts into.
- The more E.G.O. Gifts you use for the Fusion, the greater your chance of obtaining the E.G.O. Gift of the Keyword you selected.

4. A new 'Refresh' button with higher chances to reroll for certain Keywords will be added to the shops.
- You can choose which category of Keyword you would like to refresh into, and those Keywords will have a higher chance of appearing upon refresh.

5. Encounter Reward Cards: the cards that have a chance to give you an E.G.O Gift will now give you only the E.G.O Gifts from the category you selected upon entering the Mirror Dungeon.
- However, if you have already obtained all E.G.O Gift Keywords for that category, the resulting E.G.O Gift Keyword may not be from that category.

6. Encounter Reward Cards:
You can now obtain more Cost from the cards that give you Cost.
- The range of Cost that can be obtained from those cards has been widened; with luck, you should be able to gain much more Cost than before.

7. Encounter Reward Cards: Starlight Reward Cards will now have a lower chance of appearing. However, they will also give out much more Starlight.
- Before, the Starlight Encounter Reward Cards were designed to take up about 15% of the final Starlight result. However, dividing the 15% into several Reward Cards made it difficult to make them feel like meaningful gains. Therefore, we intend to lower the appearance rate but raise the amount of Starlight rewarded by those Cards.

8. Encounter Reward Card appearance chance will now be visible via an in-game tooltip.

The above improvements will gradually be applied with future updates. We will inform you via a notice as soon as each of them is updated.

Thank You.
