Text Revision for Lobotomy E.G.O::Hornet 【Alteration】 Meursault's Support Passive "Worker Bee"

The text for Lobotomy E.G.O::Hornet 【Alteration】 Meursault's Support Passive, "Worker Bee", will be revised to match its current in-game behavior.

[Text Change]

- Before:
Ally Pierce Base Attack Skill End: The said ally heals HP equal to 10% of HP damage dealt using the attack (2 times per Encounter)
- For this turn, if all Base Attack Skills equipped on allied Skill Slots are Pierce Skills, all allies heal 10 SP at Turn End

- After:
Apply the following effects for this turn (2 times per Encounter)
- Attack End of Ally Pierce Base Attack Skill: The said ally heals HP equal to 10% of HP damage dealt using the attack
- If all Base Attack Skills equipped on allied Skill Slots are Pierce Skills, all allies heal 10 SP at Turn End

[Details on the Change]

1) Added a conditional phrase "for this turn" to clarify the turn during which the Passive is triggered
2) Revised the placement of the SP healing effect text so that it does not appear to be a sub-effect of the HP healing effect
3) Revised the activation timing phrasing from "Skill End" to "Attack End"

- This Passive was designed and implemented as an effect that continuously applies during the turn in which it activates, however, the previous text did not clearly explain the active duration of the effect.
- Additionally, the HP and SP healing effects were structured in a way that their activation conditions are checked separately, however, there was a possibility that the previous text would appear as though the SP healing effect was dependent on the HP healing effect.
- Lastly, this Passive was implemented to activate based on "Attack End" so that the healing effect could be applied before being hit by a Counter Skill; however, the previous text described the timing as "Skill End".

- Adjusting the behavior to match the text would result in a risk of the Identity underperforming compared to its current behavior:
1) If the effect were changed from remaining active for the turn to activating only at Skill End, the total active duration of the Passive would be reduced.
2) If SP healing effect became dependent on HP healing effect, the Passive would become harder to activate.
3) If activation timing is set at Skill End, the Passive would activate after being hit by a Counter Skill.

- Generally, when text and behavior do not match, we prioritize adjusting the behavior to match the textual description. However, as the Identity concerned is a Walpurgis Night Identity, we have judged that adjusting its behavior would result in a noticeable change in its performance in-game.
- As such, for this issue, we have decided to revise the text to match the Identity's current behavior so that its current use and performance may be preserved in the status quo.

We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience.

Bugs & Improvements

▶ Fixed various issues related to "Blade of the House of Spiders Ryōshū" Passive "Saikai"
※ Issue 1 : Fixed an issue where, when this unit gains Skill Slots while under E.G.O Corrosion, it can access its Base Attack Skills instead of Corrosion Skills
※ Issue 2 : Fixed an issue where newly-gained Skill Slots show 0 Aggro value
※ Issue 3 : Fixed an issue where this unit fails to gain Skill Slots in non-Chain Battle Encounters
- Because allies' Slots technically don't disappear upon death in non-Chain Battle Encounters, Skill Slots not increasing upon ally defeat is normal. However, when it comes to Blade of the House of Spiders Ryōshū, this means that players are forced to choose one extra Backup unit to activate this unit's Passive, which we decided is an unnecessary gameplay step. Moreover, as this particular scenario was not specified in this Identity's Passive description, we will update the behavior to match the unit's Passive description for convenience and to alleviate potential confusion in the future.
※ Issue 4 : Disabled the "If any of The House of Spiders allied Identities are on the field, transfer HP damage taken to The House of Spiders Identity that has the lowest current HP percentage" effect in story Encounters featuring Fixed Units that have the invincibility effect
- The invincibility effect in such Encounters was employed to provide a more tactile experience for players, so that flow of battle may spotlight specific Sinners. However, if Ryōshū's damage transference effect applies to invincible units as well, it risks hurting the intended battle difficulty and experience. Therefore, we will be disabling the damage transference effect in Encounters featuring Fixed Units that have the invincibility effect.
