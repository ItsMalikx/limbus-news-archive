    Early Closure of 5F Boss Difficulty Survey for Chapter 10 - Part 3

    October 6, 2026
    BalanceMain Story

    Hello, this is Project Moon.

    On Oct. 2nd, we launched a survey regarding difficulty adjustments for a certain boss. A total of 49,678 Managers have participated so far.

    As we have collected sufficient meaningful data regarding the boss difficulty adjustments, the survey will close earlier than originally scheduled.

    - Before: Responses accepted until Oct. 8th (Thu), 10:00 (KST)
    - After: Responses accepted until Oct. 7th (Wed), 00:00 (KST)

    We will provide further details regarding the specific adjustments and survey results through a separate notice before the update.

    We sincerely thank all Managers who participated in the survey and shared their feedback regarding the difficulty adjustments.

    Thank you.
