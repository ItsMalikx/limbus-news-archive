[iOS) App Store Update Delay]

September 12, 2024

※ It was an urgent matter, so We proceeded with machine translation, and we will replace it as soon as the translation is completed.

Hello, This is Project Moon.

After the September 12th 12:00(KST) Scheduled Update, version 1.56.1 of the app was successfully released. However, we have identified that in some cases, the app update is not proceeding on the iOS App Store.

If you are experiencing issues with the app update, please try the following steps:

1. Open the App Store.
2. Select your account profile icon in the top right corner.
3. Swipe down screen and check for the loading indicator at the top of the screen.
4. After loading is complete, check if the Limbus Company app shows an "Update" option.
5. If the issue persists even after following the steps above, please try again after some time.

We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your understanding.

Thank you.
