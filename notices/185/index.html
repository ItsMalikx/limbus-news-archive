February 1st Scheduled Update Notice

Greetings, Dear Manager.

The server will enter scheduled maintenance from 10:00 [AM] through 12:00 [PM] on Feb 1st, 2024 (KST). Please make note of the maintenance schedule; we recommend that you refrain from entering combat or making in-app purchases and close the application before maintenance begins so that no data is lost. Please refer below for details about the update.

-----------

1. New Content - Refraction Railway Line 3

- Unlock Conditions : Clear Main Story 5-36

- Unlock Conditions : Clear Main Story 5.5(Intervallo III-1)-12

1. Refraction Railway Line 2 Closure

Bug Fixes & Improvements
------------------------

► Fixed an issue where Bayonet Artierlly Crab's 'Shell Tackle!!!' Skill did not activate 'On Hit, inflict 3 Pierce Fragility next turn', but instead activated 'On Hit, inflict 3 Pierce Fragility this turn'

► Fixed an issue where, in Focused Encounters, achieving Resonance or Absolute Resonance with Defensive Skills did not show Resonance details UI

► Fixed an issue where certain Voicelines played at a lower volume than the average volume

► Fixed an issue where 'Summoned Unit - Flotsam' passive "Harden" did not activate

► Adjusted the camera focus timing when enemies are killed at Turn End by effects such as Burn

► Fixed an issue where, if all enemies are killed at Turn Start, clearing the Stage caused the Combat UI to linger

► Fixed an issue where an unvoiced Announcer's subtitles appeared only for a brief second

► E.G.O Gift - Fixed an issue where 'Black Sheet Music' effect 'When hitting an enemy with a Skill, inflict 5 Sinking Potency' did not activate properly
- Before : Activated only when the unit dealt HP damage On Hit
- After : Activates On Hit

► Adjusted Announcer voicelines in response to certain boss patterns in a certain encounter of Main Story 5-36

► Fixed an issue where the focused camera did not act properly at Turn Start & Turn End

► Fixed issues in Drive - Inferno - Mini Stories - The 2nd Walpurgis Night numbering
(1) Changed 'MINI-1' to 'MINI-2'
(2) Added the missing 'District 21' text on the map

► Adjusted placement of certain portrait resources
(1) Dieci Association South Section 4 Hong Lu
(2) Dieci Association South Section 4 Yi Sang
