2026, Oct. 22nd Scheduled Update Notice

October 21, 2026
Main StoryBalanceMirror Dungeon

Greetings, Dear Manager.

The server will enter scheduled maintenance from 10:00 AM through 12:00 PM on Oct. 22nd, 2026 (KST).

Please make note of the maintenance schedule; we recommend that you refrain from entering combat or making in-app purchases and close the application before maintenance begins so that no data is lost.

Please refer below for details about the update.

Bug Fixes & Improvements

▶ Fixed an issue where gaining a Keyword through the effects of certain Identities or E.G.O would count toward its per-turn limit even when the Keyword was already at its maximum cap and was not actually gained
→ Relevant Identities and E.G.O:
(1) "Haute Couture::Le Noir Brand Manager Don Quixote" Passive: Keyword "Blessing of the Obsidian God"
(2) "R Corp. 4th Pack Rhino Meursault" Defense Skill: Keyword "Charge"
(3) "Lobotomy E.G.O::Sunshower Heathcliff" Passive: Keyword "Protection"
(4) "Heishou Pack - You Branch Adept Heathcliff" Passive: Keyword "Battleblood Instinct"
(5) "AEDD Heathcliff" E.G.O Passive: Keyword "Charge"

▶ Fixed an issue where the Support Passive of ""Fanghunt Office Fixer Hong Lu" would apply its effect in reverse deployment order when two or more Identities were tied for the sum total of Bleed Potency and Bleed Count

▶ Fixed an issue where hit effects would not be displayed when a Skill was used while the Façade of "Haute Couture::The Crimson God of Le Rouge Sinclair" was projected onto another Sinclair Identity

▶ Fixed an issue where certain effects would remain on screen after a unit died while in E.G.O Corrosion state with certain E.G.O
→ Relevant E.G.O: 4th Match Flame Yi Sang, Dimension Shredder Yi Sang

▶ Fixed an issue where a unique combat speech for "The Pequod Captain Ishmael" would play when the said Identity's attack triggered "Mercé" for "Night Awls Capitano Gregor"

▶ Fixed an issue where part of the animation would play unnaturally when a Coin was reused by the Awakening Skill of the "Hollow Faust" E.G.O

▶ Fixed an issue where an attack made with a Cracked Unbreakable Coin while out of ammo would appear as though using a regular Coin
※ The attack was correctly performed with a Cracked Unbreakable Coin. This fix only addresses its visuals.

▶ Fixed an issue where certain Identities would play an unintended animation when their appearance changed from Staggered to normal
→ Relevant Identities: The Index Proxy - Effloresced E.G.O::Procuration Don Quixote, Wild Hunt Heathcliff, Dawn Office Fixer Sinclair

▶ Fixed an issue where part of the Support Passive text for "The Pequod Harpooneer Heathcliff" would incorrectly be tagged as a Keyword

▶ (EN Only) Updated the activation condition text of the Awakening Skill for the "Unbrilliant Glory Gregor" E.G.O to match the game text phrasing renewal
- Before: When used for the first time in "battle..."
- After: When used for the first time in "an Encounter..."

Notes

- Please update your game client to the newest version on the respective store (Google Play/App Store/Steam).
- Forcibly shutting down the game while it is downloading resources may cause the download to fail, so please be careful not to close the application during the download.
- You will not be able to play <Limbus Company> during maintenance. Please understand.
- The maintenance schedule may suddenly change depending on the status of maintenance. If such a change occurs, we will let you know in a follow-up notice.
- A resource download may be prompted after the build update. If you're using a mobile device, it is recommended to proceed with a Wi-Fi connection.
- If you're playing with a guest account, we recommend that you link your account with other platforms to prevent possible account loss.
- The maintenance compensation will be provided once per account. Please note that the distribution may be slow due to network delays or other causes.
- The update gifts are valid to claim for 7 days. Please redeem them within this timeframe.
