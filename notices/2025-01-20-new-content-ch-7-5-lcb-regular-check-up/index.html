New Content - Ch.7.5: LCB Regular Check-up

January 20, 2025
EventsMain StoryIdentities & E.G.ONew Content

Greetings, Dear Manager.

With the Jan 23rd, 2025 (KST) Update, new content [Ch.7.5: LCB Regular Check-up] will be added to the game.

▶ Duration
2025/1/23 12:00 (KST) ~ Before 2/20 (KST) Scheduled Update
▶ Event Rewards Exchange
2025/1/23 12:00 (KST) ~ Before 2/27 (KST) (KST) Scheduled Update
▶ Requirements
- Event Unlock Conditions: Clear Main Story 0-4
- Event Stage Unlock Conditions: Clear Main Story 7-36

Event Description

- When the event starts, the [Ch. 7.5 - LCB Regular Check-up] Event Page will open.

- During the event period, you can earn [Amassed Data] by clearing the LCB Regular Check-up Event Stages & the Mirror Dungeons.

- You can redeem various rewards from the [Reward Exchange] by spending the [Amassed Data] that you have collected.

Event Gameplay

► Combat Encounters of ‘LCB Regular Check-up’
You can obtain [Amassed Data] every time you complete a combat Encounter of ‘LCB Regular Check-up’. Each Encounter has a first-time reward, and with it, you can obtain even more [Amassed Data].

The final combat Stage of ‘LCB Regular Check-up’ costs Enkephalin Modules. That final Stage will have a higher Enkephalin-to-[Amassed Data] ratio, so it will be much more effective as a means of collecting [Amassed Data].

Event Gameplay

► Mirror Dungeons
During the Event Period, ‘LCB Regular Check-up’ Floor Theme Pack will have a fixed appearance on the final floor of both Mirror Dungeon Normal & Hard. The Theme Pack features exclusive choice events and E.G.O Gifts.

※ Once the event is over, your LCB Regular Check-up Theme Pack will no longer be a fixed appearance, and LCB Regular Check-up Theme Pack spawn location may be adjusted.

Clearing a Mirror Dungeon run will net you [Amassed Data]. Selecting the LCB Regular Check-up Theme Pack as the final floor theme will net you even more [Amassed Data].

Mirror Dungeon Hard Difficulty rewards contain much more [Amassed Data] than Mirror Dungeon Normal.
※ However, please note that using the Weekly Bonuses does not increase the event currency yield.
※ Please note that, if you have started a Mirror Dungeon run before the event started, you will not gain event currency from that run.

► Main Story
If you have not fulfilled the event unlock requirements, you can obtain [Amassed Data] as a first-time reward from Main Story Combat Encounters. Upon first-time-completion of an Encounter, you can obtain [Amassed Data] proportional to the amount of Enkephalin Spent regardless of whether you’ve met the EX Clear Conditions or not.

※ You cannot obtain event currencies from stages that have already been completed.
※ You cannot obtain [Amassed Data] from Refraction Railway or Luxcavation.

Event Currency

If you have cleared the ‘LCB Regular Check-up’ Stages, Mirror Dungeons, and the Main Story, you have a small chance to obtain [Classified Data]

[Classified Data] has the highest chance of dropping from the final Encounter of the event. However, the chance for it to drop is the same across all other Encounters.

[Classified Data] is automatically converted to 20 [Amassed Data] in the Results or the Rewards screen upon acquisition.

Event Bonus Identities & E.G.O

Obtain more [Amassed Data] by completing Event Encounters or Mirror Dungeons while equipped with Event Bonus Identities or E.G.O.

<Event Bonus Identities & E.G.O>

► 40% Bonus
- E.G.O [HE] : Thoracalgia Ryōshū
- 3★ : LCE E.G.O::Ardor Blossom Moth Faust
- 2★ : LCE E.G.O::Lantern Yi Sang

※ The Event Currency Bonus from each Identity & E.G.O is additive. (Rounded up)
※ Event Currency Bonus maxes out at 120% Bonus.
※ Event Bonus Identities don’t actually have to participate in a battle; you can earn the bonus currency simply by having them in your Team loadout.
※ Event Currency Bonus applies to first-time clear rewards and Mirror Dungeon clear rewards as well.

Event Rewards

At the [Rewards Exchange], you can spend [Amassed Data] you’ve collected to purchase various rewards.

※ You can no longer obtain [Amassed Data] after you obtain enough [Amassed Data] to purchase every item in the [Reward Exchange].

※ [Amassed Data] will no longer be obtainable once the Event closes after the 2025/02/20 (KST) Scheduled Update.

※ [Reward Exchange] will be open for 5 weeks, from 2025/1/23 12:00 (KST) ~ Before 2025/2/27 (KST) Scheduled Update.

※ Even after the event fully ends, you will be able to experience the LCB Regular Check-up combat Encounters and view its story anytime at the Drive tab and Theater - [Detour Tales].
