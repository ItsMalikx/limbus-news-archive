September 7th Scheduled Update Notice

September 4, 2023
EventsIdentities & E.G.ONew ContentBalance

Greetings, Dear Manager.

The server will enter maintenance from 10:00 [AM] through 12:00 [PM] on September 7th, 2023 (KST) for system optimizations and content updates. Please make note of the maintenance schedule; we recommend that you refrain from entering combat or making in-app purchases and close the application before maintenance begins so that no data is lost. Please refer below for details about the update.

Update Contents

- 3★ : Seven South Section 4 Faust
- 2★ : Seven South Section 4 Heathcliff

- 3★ : Cinq South Section 5 Director Don Quixote, W Corp. L3 Cleanup Agent Don Quixote
- 2★ : N Corp. Mittelhammer Don Quixote, Shi South Section 5 Director Don Quixote

(2) JPY - 6 package prices adjusted

Clash Arrow colors for Clashes and Unopposed attacks will be changed.
- Unopposed attack by allies: Blue hued
- Unopposed attack by enemies: Red hued

Bug Fixes and Improvements

- Fixed an issue where displaying the E.G.O Skill selection menu for the first time in combat would cause the game to hitch
- (Steam) Fixed an issue where restarting the game after setting the FPS setting to AUTO would revert the setting back to 60 FPS
- Fixed an issue where W Corp. L3 Cleanup Agent Ryōshū’s Uptie 4 「Charged Evade」 skill did not activate upon activating the skill a second time
- Fixed an issue where an “UNKNOWN” value was displayed in the Keywords menu of Uptie 4 N Corp. Mittelhammer Rodion’s Skill 3 “Ironclad Retribution”
- Fixed an issue where players sometimes could not revert their 3★ Identity’s Uptie 3 art to pre-Uptie 3 art when changing them in the Lobby
- Fixed an issue where certain effects in 3-22 of Canto III did not activate properly

Maintenance & Application Update Compensation

- Content: 300 Lunacy
- Recipients: All users whose game accounts were created before 09:59 Sep. 14th, 2023 (KST)

Notes

- Please update your game client to the newest version on the respective store (Google Play/App Store/Steam).
- Forcibly shutting down the game while it is downloading resources may cause the download to fail, so please be careful not to close the application during the download.
- You will not be able to play <Limbus Company> during maintenance. Please understand.
- The maintenance schedule may suddenly change depending on the status of maintenance. If such a change occurs, we will let you know in a follow-up notice.
- A resource download may be prompted after the build update. If you're using a mobile device, it is recommended to proceed with a Wi-Fi connection.
- If you're playing with a guest account, we recommend that you link your account with other platforms to prevent possible account loss.
- The maintenance compensation will be provided once per account. Please note that the distribution may be slow due to network delays or other causes.
- The update gifts are valid to claim for 7 days. Please redeem them within this timeframe.
