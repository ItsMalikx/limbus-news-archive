Bug Fixes & Improvements

▶ (Steam) Fixed an issue where, in Mirror Dungeons, if The Middle Big Brother Heathcliff is included in the Team and present on the battlefield, the battle may become unresponsive after an effect of the E.G.O Gift "Sorrowful Exhale" activates
※ Relevant Identity Passive Effect : If an enemy attacked any of The Middle-faction allies including this unit, use "Kicking" on the said enemy as an Unopposed Attack at Attack End of the said enemy's Skill (once per turn)
※ Relevant E.G.O Gift Effect : deal +80% damage on Critical Hit (once per Encounter, except E.G.O Skills)

[ Cause ]

- "The Middle Big Brother Heathcliff" Combat Passive effect activating at Turn Start in the same turn as the second effect of the E.G.O Gift "Sorrowful Exhale" caused a critical system error, causing the game to become unresponsive.

[ Issue Fix ]

- We have applied a logic fix to the game client to resolve this issue.

※ As mobile platforms require each client update to undergo a market review, it is difficult for us to apply this fix with the May 7th Scheduled Update.

※ Please note that this issue will be resolved on May 14th for mobile platforms. We would like to ask the Managers playing on mobile platforms to refrain from using the affected Identity and E.G.O Gift in the meantime.

We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience.

▶ Fixed an issue where battle against the Abnormality Doomsday Calendar could fail to proceed to the next phase after a specific part was "Severed"

[ Issue Cause ]

- During the implementation of the final battle of Chapter 7, the modified code recognized the severed Parts as unbroken Parts.
- Doomsday Calendar transitions to the next phase when it has no unbroken Parts, or when its HP falls below a certain percentage.
- If a Part was destroyed before the HP condition was met, the next phase would not start, causing turns to pass indefinitely.

[ Issue Fix ]

- Adjusted the code that identifies broken Parts as unbroken Parts
