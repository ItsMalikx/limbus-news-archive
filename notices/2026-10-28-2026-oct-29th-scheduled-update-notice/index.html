6. Mirror of Sisyphe - Mirror Projection / Achievements Added

Mirror Projection / Achievements - Part 1 will be added to the game with the 2026. 10. 29 (KST) Scheduled Update.

※ Projection Rewards from Achievements can be earned only up to Projection Level 60. They do not count toward the weekly Projection limit. Up to 1,000 Projection Rate can be earned each week by completing Mirror Dungeon runs.

※ Achievements that require a clear will count as completed only if Enkephalin Modules are spent on the reward claim screen. Please keep this in mind when attempting these Achievements.

※ More Achievements are planned for future updates.

※ Mirror of Sisyphe Achievements can be completed, and their rewards claimed, only until before the next Mirror Dungeon update.

※ Clearing a Mirror Dungeon with a Rental Team can still complete Achievements and earn Projection Rate if conditions are met. However, please note that loadout-related Achievements added in the future cannot be completed with a Rental Team.
