New Content - Refraction Railway Line 6

2026-05-27

---------------------------------------

Greetings, Dear Manager.

New Content <Refraction Railway Line 6> will be added to the game with the May 28th, 2026 (KST) Scheduled Update.

<Unlock Conditions>
Clear Main Story Chapter 9

<Event Description>
- Refraction Railway Line 6 is composed of 5 total Sections, and you will face highly difficult encounters in all of them. More rewards can be claimed the further you progress into the Refraction Railway.
- If you have achieved certain conditions before clearing the final Section, you will be rewarded with a Special Decorative Banner.
- Refraction Railway Line 6 will end with the start of Refraction Railway Line 7.

<About Refraction Railway Line 5 Rewards>
Any rewards you have not claimed from Line 5 Lost & Found will be lost with the start of Refraction Railway Line 6 (unclaimed rewards will not be delivered to your inbox either).
If you have not claimed your Line 5 rewards yet, please claim them before the May 28th (KST) Scheduled Update.

Content Details
---------------

※ The above image is for demonstration purposes only and is subject to change before the official release.

- <Refraction Railway Line 6> will open after completing Main Story Chapter 9, and can be accessed via the "Drive" menu.
- As long as Line 6 is active, it can be accessed anytime without spending any resources. Line 6 will close upon the opening of <Refraction Railway Line 7>.
- You can spend a certain number of Modules(only for the first time) at the Lost & Found to claim your Refraction Railway rewards.

Recommended Upgrade Levels
--------------------------

※ The above image is for demonstration purposes only and is subject to change before the official release.

- The recommended upgrade levels for Refraction Railway Line 6 are: Identity Uptie Tier 4, Level 60, and E.G.O Threadspin Tier 4. The Refraction Railway presents a very difficult series of battles; we strongly recommend that you enter the Railway after meeting the above conditions.
- Any Level Ups, Upties, or Threadspinnings performed after entry will be reflected in your current loadout within the Refraction Railway as well.

About Line 6
------------

※ The above image is for demonstration purposes only and is subject to change before the official release.

- Clearing the Section will record your Clear Turn Count. With it, the Total Turn Count will also be saved. The Total Turn Count is the sum of all Clear Turn Counts from each Section, from Section 1 to your current Section.
- Below each Section, you can view the rewards for clearing that Section. Claim them at the Lost & Found.
- Any remaining E.G.O Resource after the Section can be used in the subsequent Section.
- Save for Section 5, entering a new Section revives all defeated allies, fully heals all wounded Identities, and resets SP to 0.
- During an expedition, you can choose to start over from previously cleared Stages in order to reduce your Total Turn Count. When you do so, the Stage will start with the Sinners' statuses and E.G.O resources reverted to that point, and any progress you have made since that Section will be reset.

※ The above image is for demonstration purposes only and is subject to change before the official release.

- Line 6 is comprised of 5 different "Sections", and you may freely adjust your loadout before entering each Section. If you are having difficulties progressing, perhaps changing up your Team Loadout may be an effective option.
- In Line 6, the Team's HP percentage and SP at the point of clearing Section 1 will be carried over to Section 5.
- In Sections 3 and 4, you will be unable to use the Identities assigned in the Team Loadout used in the immediately preceding Sections.
e.g. If LCB Sinner Yi Sang was assigned in the Team Loadout for Section 2,
- LCB Sinner Yi Sang cannot be assigned to Section 3's Team Loadout
- LCB Sinner Yi Sang can be assigned to Section 4's Team Loadout

Loadouts
--------

※ The above image is for demonstration purposes only and is subject to change before the official release.

- Refraction Railway Line 6 uses the 'Chain Battle' system. In Chain Battles, when a Deployed unit is defeated, the Backup units will fill their spots in their own Deployment orders.
- You cannot enter the Encounter until all 7 Deployment units and 5 Backup units have been selected.
- Backup Units receive a bonus Starting SP. Minus Coin Identities (Identities whose Base Attack Skills are all Minus Coins) gain negative Starting SP buffs.
※ Relevant Identities : Lobotomy E.G.O::Sunshower Heathcliff, The One Who Shall Grip Sinclair
- For Identities whose Skill Coin types change depending on their SP, the bonus Starting SP will be applied based on their usable Skills at the point of Substitution.
※ Relevant Identities : Lobotomy E.G.O::In the Name of Love and Hate Don Quixote, Lobotomy E.G.O::The Sword Sharpened with Tears Rodion
- If Starting SP is applied in negative numbers, SP will not fall below -40.

► Buffs

※ The above image is for demonstration purposes only and is subject to change before the official release.

- When an allied Identity is killed, you will obtain E.G.O resources corresponding to the ranks and Affinities of their Base Attack Skills.
- In Refraction Railway Line 6, some Encounters feature unique effects to be applied on allies. You can view the buffs by hovering over the buff icons located below each unit sprite.

Focused Encounters
------------------

※ The above image is for demonstration purposes only and is subject to change before the official release.

- Every battle in the Refraction Railway will allow you to directly match ally skills against enemy skills, just like in Focused Encounters.

► Chain Battles

※ The above image is for demonstration purposes only and is subject to change before the official release.

- When a Deployed unit is defeated, the Backup unit will fill their spot in the Deployment order.
- When a Backup unit is Deployed into battle, its Combat Passive activates instead of its Support Passive. When a Deployed unit is defeated, its Support Passive will activate instead of its Combat Passive.

Combat Progression
------------------

※ The above image is for demonstration purposes only and is subject to change before the official release.

- You can choose to forfeit or retry a Stage while progressing through it. Please note that choosing to forfeit or retry the Stage will reset the entire Stage and that you will have to start over from the very first wave.

※ The above image is for demonstration purposes only and is subject to change before the official release.

- If all of your allies are defeated, you can choose between returning to the Team Loadout menu to change your Deployment orders or immediately retrying the battle from Wave 1.

Claiming Rewards
----------------

※ The above image is for demonstration purposes only and is subject to change before the official release.

- After clearing a Stage for the first time, rewards from the said Stage will be sent to the "Lost & Found".
- To access the "Lost & Found" for the first time, a certain number of Enkephalin Modules will be required. Rewards that can be claimed afterward are free of charge.
- You can earn various Level-up and Extraction currencies here. Special rewards include 「Level Boost Ticket VI」, 「Season 7 - 3★ Guarantee Decaextraction Ticket」, 「Refraction Railway Line 6 Themed Ticket Deco」.

Special Decorative Banners
--------------------------

※ The above image is for demonstration purposes only and is subject to change before the official release.

- Here, you can view the acquisition conditions for Refraction Railway Line 6 Banners. The harder the conditions, the more elaborate your Banner will be.
- Once you have obtained a Banner, the Turn Count of your best record for each Section and Total Turns will be displayed on it. If the Total Turns of two records are the same, the newest record will be prioritized.
- The highest Turn Count that can be recorded in each Section is 100 Turns. If your Turn Count goes beyond 100 Turns in a single Stage, it will be considered a defeat.

Clear Info
----------

※ The above image is for demonstration purposes only and is subject to change before the official release.

- After clearing each Section, you can view statistics such as the obtained Active Effects and participating Identities in the Clear Info menu.

Records
-------

※ The above image is for demonstration purposes only and is subject to change before the official release.

- Your stats will be recorded upon clearing Section 5, which can be viewed from your Travel Collection during the Refraction Railway's runtime.
- Up to 20 records can be saved. If you have more than 20 records, records with the highest Total Turns will be deleted first.
- The date of completion, Total Turns taken, used Identities, and damage output statistics will be displayed in your logs (Travel Collection).
- You can view only your own records in the Travel Collection.
