1. Season 6 Egoshard Carryover

Guide on Season Changes - Egoshards

- When a new Season begins, half of the previous Season’s
Egoshards will be carried over (rounded up).
- The carryover rates for unconverted Egoshards from multiple seasons past will be halved for each following Season.
- The remaining Egoshards are converted into Thread.
- Egoshard Crates (both random and nominable)
will be carried over in the same way.

As per the information above, half of the currencies listed below will be converted to Season 7’s.

- Season 6 [Sinner]’s Egoshards
- Season 6 Uptie & Threadspinning only Shards (Universal)
- Season 6 Egoshard Crate
- Season 6 Nominable Egoshard Crate

The carryover of currencies to Season 7 will automatically occur the first time you log into the game during Season 7.
A popup will show how much previous Season’s Egoshard relevant currency you owned, and how they have been converted.

※ Previous Season’s Identity Egoshard currencies that are still in the Mailbox or in the Lost & Found will be carried over when they are claimed. Carryover to Season 7 currency will occur as soon as they are claimed, and currencies that were not carried over will be converted to Thread-related currencies.

※ If the Season 5 currency obtainable from Refraction Railway Line 5 is claimed in Season 7, 1/4 of that currency will be carried over to Season 7. (rounded up) Currencies that were not carried over will be converted to Thread-related currencies.

2. Gradual Main Story & Battle Pass Updates

Chapter 9 of the main story and the Season 7 Pass will be updated in 3 parts.

▶ 12/31
- Main Story Ch. 9 《Part 1》
- Season 7 Pass Rewards 《Lv. 1 ~ Lv. 20》

▶ 1/8
- Main Story Ch. 9 《Part 2》
- Season 7 Pass Rewards 《Lv. 21 ~ Lv. 40》

▶ 1/15
- Main Story Ch. 9 《Part 3》
- Season 7 Pass Rewards 《Lv. 41 ~ Lv. 120 (Max Pass Level)》
- Season 7 Pass Max Pass Level+ Rewards

Accumulated Pass Levels are continuously tracked regardless of the gradual updates.
When the subsequent updates unlock the rewards for higher
Pass Levels, you can immediately claim them up to your accumulated Pass Level.

Max Pass Level rewards earned for leveling up the Pass past the max Pass Level are also kept track of even when they aren’t unlocked, and you can claim the accumulated Season 7 Nominable
Egoshard Crates immediately once they are made available.

Chapter 9 stage progression rewards and Max Pass Level rewards will become available to claim with the Jan. 15th update, when 《Part 3》 of this chapter is added.
