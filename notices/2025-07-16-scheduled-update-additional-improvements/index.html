    2025.07.17(KST) Scheduled Update Additional Improvements

    July 16, 2025
    Balance

    Hello, this is Project Moon.

    We would like to inform you about additional improvements that have been made to be deployed with the July 17th Scheduled Update.

    Please continue reading for more information.

    [ List ]

    ▶ Further changes to E.G.O Gift 'T-1 Perpetual Motion Machine' effects
    Before: Each Identity gains 1 Charge every time they consume 10 Charge Count. (5 times per Identity per Encounter; does not interact with Unique Charge)
    After: Each Identity gains 1 Charge every time they consume 10 Charge Count. (5 times per Identity per Encounter; does not interact with Unique Charge with fixed Potency)

    ※ After the July 17th Scheduled Update, this effect will now apply to 'Unique Charge' that gains Potency as well.
