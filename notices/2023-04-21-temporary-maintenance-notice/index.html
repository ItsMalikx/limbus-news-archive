Temporary Maintenance Notice

April 21, 2023

Greetings, dear manager.
To resolve the issues listed below, the game will temporarily enter maintenance.

[Maintenance Time]

- 2023/04/21 17:30 ~ 18:30 (KST)
The time of completion may vary depending on the situation.

[Update Contents]

- Addressed omissions and typos from the translation
- Fixed an issue where the image for R.B. Sous-chef Gregor was shown as a white blank when selecting support Identities
- Fixed an issue where interacting with the UI on the lower side slowed the game down to an abnormal degree
- Fixed an issue where events that give the E.G.O Gift already owned from the start could appear in Hell's Chicken dungeons

In addition, we would like to provide an additional compensation for the delay in the roadmap. Please refer to below for more details.

※ As Season 2 has been postponed to 2023/06/01 (KST), the duration of Season 1 has been extended by two weeks, its end date moving from May 18th to June 1st. This extension will apply to the game with the next scheduled update.

We apologize for the inconvenience.
