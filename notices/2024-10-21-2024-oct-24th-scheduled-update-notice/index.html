Bug Fixes & Improvements

▶ Fixed an issue where a certain part of Zwei West Section 3 Ishmael's 'Clashable Guard' was not working as intended
※ Relevant Effect: On Clash Lose: does not gain Final Power from the following sources: Resonance and the Level Disparity against the Attack Skill

▶ Fixed an issue where the 'Wild Hunt' effect activates against entities that weren't supposed to be targetable by this effect

▶ Fixed the description error for an enemy Passive in Stage 7-6
※ Relevant Passive: Leisurely Breathing
- Before: Turn End: Gain Pierce DMG Up equal to (Poise on self/4) next turn (max 3)
- After: Turn End: Gain Haste equal to (Poise on self/4) next turn (max 3)

▶ Improved the animation and voicelines clarity of certain Skills for the boss in Stage 7-16

▶ Improved the Stage 7-35 story cutscene

▶ (EN Only) Fixed the capitalization issue of a certain Skill for the boss in Stage 7-34

▶ Fixed an issue where, under certain circumstances, the number of Skill Slots could exceed the limit in Focused Chain Battles

▶ Fixed an issue where, in combat, an incorrect animation would load for the target before attack

▶ (EN Only) Updated the translation for the E.G.O Gift '가시 올가미(currently Barbed Snare)'
- Before: Barbed Snare
- After: Barbed Lasso

▶ Adjusted the font colors for the following in the Choice Event UI: Success, Prediction, and Result values
※ Relevant Sin Affinities: Wrath, Gluttony, Gloom

▶ Fixed an issue where the game softlocks when a server communication attempt occurs when attempting to make purchases past the number of limited stock for items with limited stock

▶ (AOS) Fixed an issue where certain areas of the screen in the Dispenser UI would have issues registering touches

▶ (iOS) Adjusted the application settings to prevent unintentional home bar interactions

▶ Adjusted the timing for when the 'Checkpoints' tutorials appear during the Story Dungeon

▶ Updated the pop-up window UI resource for the Battle Pass EXP Gain scene post-Mirror Dungeon completion to match the current Season

▶ Adjusted the camera settings so that, if the popup UI is open in a Story Dungeon, the dungeon map camera remains static

Maintenance & Application Update Compensation

- Content: Lunacy x300
- Recipients: All users whose game accounts were created before 10:00 Oct. 31st, 2024 (KST)
- When: After Oct. 24th, 2024 (Thu) 12:00 (KST)
- Can be claimed once per account

Maintenance Compensation can be claimed until Oct. 31st (Thu) 2024, 10:00 (KST) via the in-game Mailbox. (Mail available for 7 days)

Notes

- Please update your game client to the newest version on the respective store (Google Play/App Store/Steam).
- Forcibly shutting down the game while it is downloading resources may cause the download to fail, so please be careful not to close the application during the download.
- You will not be able to play <Limbus Company> during maintenance. Please understand.
- The maintenance schedule may suddenly change depending on the status of maintenance. If such a change occurs, we will let you know in a follow-up notice.
- A resource download may be prompted after the build update. If you're using a mobile device, it is recommended to proceed with a Wi-Fi connection.
- If you're playing with a guest account, we recommend that you link your account with other platforms to prevent possible account loss.
- The maintenance compensation will be provided once per account. Please note that the distribution may be slow due to network delays or other causes.
- The update gifts are valid to claim for 7 days. Please redeem them within this timeframe.

Thank you.
