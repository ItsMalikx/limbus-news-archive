Notice: Don Quixote Employee Card Resource to be Changed

April 25, 2025

Hello, this is Project Moon.

We have identified an issue in the updated mugshot resources, updated Apr. 24th, 2025, where a certain part of the image does not match the established lore.

※ Mugshot replacement Sinner: Don Quixote
※ Mugshot resource update ETA: After Apr. 30th Scheduled Update

We realized that, upon comparing Don Quixote's height in the updated mugshot to story sprites, the mugshot height does not match the established height for her character.

Therefore, we will be making certain adjustments to Don Quixote's mugshot to be more in line with the preexisting resources.

Also, the story illustrations used in the LCB Regular Check-up and its Banners will be replaced to reflect this as well.

We sincerely apologize for the confusion caused by these additional illustration replacements. Going forward, we will take better care to prevent similar mistakes from occurring again.
