2026, Jan. 29th Scheduled Update Notice

January 28, 2026
Main StoryBalance

Greetings, Dear Manager.

The server will enter scheduled maintenance from
10:00 [AM] through 12:00 [PM] on Jan. 29th, 2026 (KST).

Please make note of the maintenance schedule; we recommend that you refrain from entering combat or making in-app purchases and close the application before maintenance begins so that no data is lost.

Please refer below for details about the update.

Major Changes

1. Main Story Chapter Progression Bar UI will be adjusted.

- Before : When a Main Story (Part 3) unlocked, the full number of Stages was made visible as well.
- After : The full number of Stages will not be visible until
Main Story (Part 3) final Stage is cleared.

※ As the immediate reveal of the full length of the Main Story Stages could harm the Managers' engagement and immersion with the story by prematurely revealing the story and its progression, we have adjusted the in-game UI so that the full scope of the Main Story will be revealed only upon clearing the final stage.

Bug Fixes & Improvements

▶ Fixed an issue where The House of Spiders: The Middle Apprentice Ishmael was incorrectly treated as a valid target for the E.G.O Gift 'Bloodflame Sword'

▶ Fixed an issue where The House of Spiders: The Middle Apprentice
Ishmael was incorrectly classified as a 'Sinking'-type Identity

▶ Fixed an issue where all of the effects of the E.G.O Gift
'Little and To-be-Naughty Plushie' failed to function
※ This issue has been confirmed to have occurred with the Mirror of the Lake
Update on Dec. 14th, 2023. Although this issue was resolved with the Jan. 22nd,
2026 update, the details of the bug fix were omitted from the notice.
We apologize for the inconvenience.

▶ Fixed an issue where the E.G.O Gift 'Little and To-be-Naughty Plushie'
did not activate against targets with 'Unique Bleed'.

▶ Added an additional explanatory text for Stage 9-37 on the unavailability of Retreat abilities
※ Added text: The Sinners & Assist Units' Retreat-related abilities do not activate
※ Retreat was intentionally disabled as part of the game stage design.
For clarification, this information has been added to the description text of the enemy's effect 'Viewing the Tableau'.

▶ Fixed an issue where using the E.G.O Corrosion Skill of 'Contempt,
Awe Ryōshū' in the final battle of Main Story Chapter 9 could block game progression under certain conditions

▶ Fixed an issue in the final battle of Stage 4-54 where allies would get
Staggered repeatedly under certain conditions, blocking game progression

▶ Removed incorrect skill text from enemies in Main Story Chapter 9
※ Removed text: If target's base Slash Resist. is lower than 1, treat it as "Normal" (1.0)
※ We have identified that this effect failed to apply in-game. As such, the text was removed to maintain consistent gameplay experience.

▶ Fixed issues related to battle voiceline speech bubbles appearing above characters during battles
※ Issue 1: The next speech bubble disappeared immediately when multiple voicelines play consecutively
※ Issue 2: Speech bubbles remained on screen when a separate external cutscene takes place while the speech bubble has not finished displaying

▶ Fixed an issue where announcer lines played incorrectly during the turn where certain enemies use powerful Skills
- Before: Announcer voicelines for "Danger" would play
- After: Announcer voicelines corresponding to "Powerful Enemy Skill Warning" will play
※ Previously, we had identified this issue occurring in Stage 9-18 on Jan. 15th and deployed a fix. Please note that this is an additional improvement applied as we have confirmed that the issue persisted with certain other enemies.

▶ (AOS, iOS) Fixed an issue where excessive camera zoom-in and zoom-out could occur in certain battle Stages

▶ Improved visual effect visibility of 'The Prescript's Target' and 'Mark of the Prescript'

▶ Improved camera angle for certain enemy Skill animations in Main Story Chapter 9

▶ Improved visibility where effects with darker shade are displayed over dark backgrounds in Main Story Chapter 9

▶ Improved the standing illustration displayed after clearing the final stage of Main Story Chapter 9

▶ Fixed an issue in Stage 6-41 where voicelines from other story chapters could play during certain sequences

▶ Fixed an issue in a certain Chapter 9 battle where Reused Coins could trigger attack
Skills equal to the number of Coins Reused when the enemy successfully evaded using its 'Precognition' effect

▶ Fixed an issue in Main Story Chapter 9 where some texts would overlap during special battle voiceline played at Turn Start when the enemy has 'Eye of Precognition - Overheat'

▶ Improved manual story playback in Main Story Chapter 9 so that voicelines no longer skip automatically in certain sections

▶ Fixed an issue where enemy stats were displayed when viewing Sinner information after checking enemy details

▶ Fixed an issue where unintended colors appeared when sepia-tone image effects were applied in certain story scenes

▶ Added explanatory text for certain server errors that may occur under specific conditions

Maintenance & Application Update Compensation

- Content: Lunacy x300
- Recipients: All users whose game accounts were created before 10:00 Feb. 5th, 2026 (KST)
- When: After Jan. 29th, 2026 (Thu) 12:00 (KST)
- Can be claimed once per account

Maintenance Compensation can be claimed until Feb. 5th (Thu) 2026,
10:00 (KST) via the in-game Mailbox. (Mail available for 7 days)

Notes

- Please update your game client to the newest version on the respective store (Google Play/App Store/Steam).
- Forcibly shutting down the game while it is downloading resources may cause the download to fail, so please be careful not to close the application during the download.
- You will not be able to play <Limbus Company> during maintenance. Please understand.
- The maintenance schedule may suddenly change depending on the status of maintenance. If such a change occurs, we will let you know in a follow-up notice.
- A resource download may be prompted after the build update. If you're using a mobile device, it is recommended to proceed with a Wi-Fi connection.
- If you're playing with a guest account, we recommend that you link your account with other platforms to prevent possible account loss.
- The maintenance compensation will be provided once per account. Please note that the distribution may be slow due to network delays or other causes.
- The update gifts are valid to claim for 7 days. Please redeem them within this timeframe.

Thank you.
