Notice: Ver. 1.110.0 Known Issues and Hotfix ETA

July 23, 2026

Hello, this is Project Moon.

We would like to inform you of the known issues identified after the Scheduled Update on Jul. 23rd, 2026, along with the hotfix schedule and detailed information.

Please refer below for more information.

< To-be Hotfixed >

▶ (JP Only) Fixed an issue where, in Refraction Railway Line 2 BokGak, a certain enemy unit was given an incorrect name

< Hotfix ETA >

- Jul. 23rd (Thu) 19:00 (KST)
- There may be a delay of approximately 10 minutes.

※ The Hotfix will be deployed upon restarting the game, and does not require a separate client update. However, please note that additional in-game downloads may occur.

< Issues to be resolved with the next Scheduled Update >

▶ An issue where, in one of the Refraction Railway Line 2 BokGak battles, the Choice Event for a specific enemy occurs even when the said enemy is defeated

▶ An issue where a certain spine animation resource does not function as intended in the Victory UI

▶ An issue where, in the Refraction Railway Line 2 BokGak boarding UI, the size of the "CLEAR" stamp (which appears over a reward when it is claimed) is too small, making it difficult to see whether a reward has been claimed or not

We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience.
