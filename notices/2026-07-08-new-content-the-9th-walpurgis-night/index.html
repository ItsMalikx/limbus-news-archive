※ Notice: Extraction Rate Changes during the Event Period
During the Walpurgis Night Event, Extractable Announcers will be added to the game.

Due to this addition, the Extraction rates for certain Extractions will be changed while the Walpurgis Night event is active.

In compliance with the 「Voluntary Restraint Guidelines for Probabilistic Items」 of Korea Association of Game Industry, the exact Extraction rates are disclosed in the in-game menu "Detailed Rates."

Please note this information as you play the game.

7/9 Linked Content - Enkephalin Spending Event

Linked content 「Enkephalin Spending Event - The Fixer at Dawnbreak」 will be introduced with the start of the Event.

- Event Period2026/7/9 12:00 ~ 7/23 10:00 (KST)
- Event Reward Exchange Period2026/7/9 12:00 ~ 7/30 10:00 (KST)
- Unlock ConditionsClear Stage 0-4

<Description>

- With the start of the Event, the 「Enkephalin Spending Event - The Fixer at Dawnbreak」 Event page will be added to the game.
- Every Enkephalin spent during the Event period (save for those spent to assemble Enkephalin Modules) will be recorded, and you can earn various rewards based on how much Enkephalin was spent.

<Event Progression>

- 「Enkephalin Spending Event - The Fixer at Dawnbreak」 Event is unlocked upon clearing Stage 0-4, regardless of your Main Story progress.
- The rewards include Threads, EXP Tickets, and Extraction Tickets, and various Ticket Deco/Cosmetic Banners related to 「The 9th Walpurgis Night」
- The actual amount of Enkephalin spent during the event period will be recorded.
- Once the event closes after the 2026/7/23 (KST) Scheduled Update, Enkephalin spent after that point will not be recorded.
- [Rewards Claim] will remain open for 3 weeks from 2026/7/9 12:00 (KST) ~ 2026/7/30 (KST) (KST) Scheduled Update.

※ Spending Enkephalin to assemble Enkephalin Modules does not count toward your spent Enkephalin count.

※ All Enkephalin and Enkephalin Modules spent in Story Stages, Luxcavations, Mirror Dungeons, and Refraction Railway will count toward the Enkephalin Spending Event.

※ Each Enkephalin Module spent counts as 20 Enkephalin spent.

※ Even if you’ve started a Story Mirror Dungeon prior to the beginning of the Event, if you’ve consumed the Enkephalin Modules after the Scheduled Maintenance, each Module spent will still count. (20 Enkephalin per Module spent)

※ Even if you’ve completed the Refraction Railway prior to the beginning of the event, if you’ve not yet claimed your rewards from the Lost & Found, spending Enkephalin Modules to claim them will still count. (20 Enkephalin per Module spent)
