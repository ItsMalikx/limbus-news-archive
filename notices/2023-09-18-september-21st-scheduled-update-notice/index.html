September 21st Scheduled Update Notice

September 18, 2023
Identities & E.G.ORefraction RailwayEventsNew Content

Greetings, Dear Manager.

The server will enter maintenance from 10:00 [AM] through 12:00 [PM] on September 21st, 2023 (KST) for system optimizations and content updates. Please make note of the maintenance schedule; we recommend that you refrain from entering combat or making in-app purchases and close the application before maintenance begins so that no data is lost. Please refer below for details about the update.

Major Changes

1. Texts Added to Refraction Railway Line 2 Progression Arrows

2. In-combat E.G.O Selection UI Changes & Improvements - 2
When hovering over, or after selecting an E.G.O to be equipped, the changing resistance values upon using the E.G.O will be displayed. The E.G.O equipping method will be changed accordingly.

- Before : E.G.O Skills were equipped immediately after selection
- After : E.G.O Skills will be highlighted upon selection and will be equipped after selecting it again after the highlight

※ Intent : To make the changing resistance information upon E.G.O use more easily accessible

Future Updates

1. Special Attendance Check Event - Major Reward Identities Notice

「Molar Office Fixer Outis」 and 「Molar Office Fixer Yi Sang」 can be obtained from the ongoing Special Attendance Check Event.

The event runs from Sep. 7th 12:00 ~ Oct. 12th 05:59 (KST), and once the Event is over, the two Event Identities will be added to the Dispense menu with the Oct. 12th (KST) Update.

「Molar Office Fixer Outis」 and 「Molar Office Fixer Yi Sang」 are Season 2 Event Identities.

Therefore, they cannot be crafted in the Dispense menu during Season 3, and will be added again once Season 4 begins.

Managers who have not obtained the Event Identities during the Special Attendance Check Event period, please note the above information and craft them in the Dispense menu before the end of Season 2.

2. Refraction Railway Line 2 Additional Rewards
New rewards will be added to the Refraction Railway Line 2. The following content will be added mid-October, and we will post a reminder with a future Scheduled Update notice.

The added rewards and unlock conditions are as follows.

- New Banner

Unlock Conditions : Clear Refraction Railway Line 2 with 5 or more Cycle Counts and under 150 Total Turns before the end of Season 2: Reminiscence. (Only records made AFTER Sep. 15th, 2023 20:15 (KST) will count toward unlocking this reward)

- New Business Card Deco

Unlock Conditions : Clear Refraction Railway Line 2 with 5 or more Cycle Counts and under 150 Total Turns before the end of Season 2: Reminiscence. (Only records made AFTER Sep. 15th, 2023 20:15 (KST) will count toward unlocking this reward)
