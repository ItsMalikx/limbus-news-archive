2026, Jul. 23rd Scheduled Update Notice

July 22, 2026

Hello,
This is Kim Jihoon, Director at Project Moon.

We would like to provide you with an update regarding our plans and release schedule for Chapter 10.

Although we had initially scheduled the Chapter 10 update for release by August, it has been delayed to September 17th.

Additional time provided by the delay will be used to add voice acting to Chapter 10 and refine its content.

During the 3-week delay, Reflectrial content will be added to the game to provide additional combat content and resource-gathering opportunities.

In addition to the above, as per our usual content release schedule, there will be a release of Identities or 1 E.G.O and 1 Announcer every other Thursday.

While Project Moon will participate in TGS as planned, our development personnel will not attend the event and will instead focus on developing Chapter 10 to meet our updated release schedule.

HamHamPangPang's employees will be managing the Limbus Company booth at TGS. Merchandise will be distributed at the booth.

We will strive to make up for this delay by providing a more engaging experience for our users.

Once again, we sincerely apologize for the delay.

Bug Fixes & Improvements

► Fixed an issue where "4th Match Flame Rodion" E.G.O Passive would not work as intended

→ Relevant effect : After defeating an enemy that has Burn, heal (Wrath A-Reson.)% HP

- Before :
1. This effect would not trigger when the user defeated an enemy that had Burn by attacking the said enemy's Part that did not have Burn
2. This effect would trigger when an ally that has Burn was defeated

- After :
1. This effect will now trigger even when the user defeats an enemy by attacking the said enemy's Part that does not have Burn, as long as the enemy's other Part has Burn
2. This effect will now only trigger when an enemy that has Burn is defeated

※ This effect was designed to trigger when the user defeats an enemy by attacking the enemy's Part that does not have Burn, as long as its other Parts have Burn. However, in a review of the effect's function, we have identified an issue where the logic would fail to distinguish enemies from allies, and an issue where the effect would not trigger when a non-Burn Part is attacked. As such, we will adjust the logic of this effect to match its textual description and intended design. We sincerely apologize for the confusion caused by this issue.
