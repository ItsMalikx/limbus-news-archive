Notice: Ver. 1.111.0 Known Issues and Hotfix ETA

August 6, 2026

Hello, this is Project Moon.

We would like to inform you of the known issues identified following the Aug. 6th, 2026 Scheduled Update, along with the hotfix schedule and further details.

Please refer below for more information.

< To-be Hotfixed >

▶ Fixed an issue where the Awakening Skill "Unbrilliant Glory - Flowing" of the "Unbrilliant Glory Gregor" E.G.O could Clash against enemy Skills despite being [Unclashable]

▶ (EN Only) Fixed a typo in the Coin Effect text of the Threadspin Tier 5 Awakening Skill for the "Lifetime Stew Sinclair" E.G.O
- Before: Gain 1 E.G.O Resource Amp and 2 Haste next turn
- After: Apply 1 E.G.O Resource Amp and 2 Haste next turn
※ The effect itself is functioning normally; this fix only corrects a typo in the English text.

▶ Fixed an issue where certain Coin Effects of Skills used by the Abnormality "Refracted Steam Transport Machine" in Refraction Railway Line 2 BokGak functioned differently than intended
→ Relevant Skills: "5384" - Coin 1 Effect, "5475384" - Coin 1 Effect
- Before: [On Hit without Cracking]
- After: [On Hit]
※ To preserve Managers' gameplay experience, the Coin 1 Effect texts will be corrected to match the effect's current behavior.

< Hotfix ETA >

- Aug. 6th (Thu) 19:30 (KST)
- There may be a delay of approximately 10 minutes.

※ The Hotfix will be deployed upon restarting the game and does not require a separate client update. However, please note that additional in-game downloads may occur.

We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience.
