Bug Fixes & Improvements

▶ Adjusted the Starting SP effect in Chain Battles so that allied Sinners with Coin types that change depending on SP will have their Bonus SP applied based on the currently usable Skill Coin type at the point of Substitution
※ Relevant Identities: Lobotomy E.G.O::In the Name of Love and Hate Don Quixote, Lobotomy E.G.O::The Sword Sharpened with Tears Rodion
※ If SP at the point of Substitution is 0 or higher, the Identity will gain SP. If SP at the point of Substitution is below 0, the Identity will lose SP.
※ Please note that negative SP modifiers will not reduce the relevant Identities' SP below -40.

▶ Fixed the trigger conditions for the activation of the "Assist Defense" Skill
- Before: Assist Defense trigger would be determined even against Unclashable Skills and consume 1 Stack even when it would not actually activate
- After: Assist Defense trigger will no longer be determined against Unclashable Skills

▶ Fixed an issue where the applier of certain Keywords inflicted on enemies through Skills and Coin effects during combat behaved differently than intended
※ Relevant Keywords: Butterfly, Corpus Theater
※ "Butterfly": No external effects can raise the amount of Butterfly inflicted. "Inflict additional Sinking" effect inflict additional Sinking instead of additional Butterfly.
- Before: When inflicting Sinking in place of the additional Butterfly through the "Inflict additional Sinking" effect, it was treated as if the target "gained" Sinking
- After: Standardized Sinking infliction of this effect so that Sinking inflicted due to the "Inflict additional Sinking" effect is now "inflicted" by the applier of the Keyword
※ "Corpus Theater": When this unit gains Bleed from enemy Attack Skills, randomly gain 1 of the following effects next turn and lose 1 Stack of this effect (once per Skill)
- Before: Corpus Theater's effect would activate regardless of whether the Bleed effect was "inflicted" by an attacker or "gained" by the attacker's own effects, whether by the unit itself or its allies
- After: Corpus Theater's effect will now be activated when the opponent's (attacker) Attack Skills "inflict" Bleed on this unit (attacked), matching its textual description

▶ Fixed certain Skills used by a certain Assist Unit appearing in Main Story Chapter 9 that were functioning differently from their text
※ Relevant Effect 1: [On Use] If target has 5+ Tremor, inflict +1 Tremor Count on Coin Hit
- Before: The effect would activate by checking the target's Tremor Potency in real time
- After: The effect will now check the condition On Use, matching the text
※ Relevant Effect 2: [On Use] If this unit does not have Charge Count, inflict +1 additional Tremor Count on target on Skill Hit
- Before: The effect activated when this unit had neither Charge Potency nor Charge Count
- After: The effect will now activate only when this unit has no Charge Count, matching the text

▶ Removed a non-functioning effect description from Skills of a certain enemy unit in Main Story Chapter 9
※ Relevant Skills: Phantom Step, Dismay
※ Relevant Effect: [Clash Lose] Lose 5 SP
※ To preserve our Managers' gameplay experience, the text of this non-functioning effect will be removed.

▶ Fixed certain Passives used by a certain enemy appearing in Main Story Chapter 9 that were functioning differently from the intent
※ Relevant Passive : All of My Corpus has been Given to Tibia
※ Relevant Effect : Turn Start: If this unit is Staggered, recover from Stagger. Afterward, gain 2 Wrath Fragility and becomes unable to use 'Installation Art no. 3: Improvised Ribcage' until next turn (once per Encounter)
- Before : This unit was still able to use "Installation Art no. 3: Improvised Ribcage" even after this Passive activated
- After : As described, this unit will no longer use "Installation Art no. 3: Improvised Ribcage" after this Passive activates

▶ Fixed an issue where some effects of the E.G.O Gift "The End of all Evil" would not activate if "The Pequod" characters are included among Backup units
※ Relevant Effect
: If one of The Pequod-faction Identities attacks and hits an enemy, heal 7 SP for The Pequod-faction Identity with the earliest Deployment order (once per Skill)
: When an ally kills an enemy, heal 15 SP for The Pequod-faction Identity with the earliest Deployment order if said Identity lost SP
※ This issue has occurred since Feb. 26th, 2026, and will be fixed so that the effects activate even when a "The Pequod" character is included among Backup units.

▶ (EN Only) Fixed typos in the English skill texts of certain Identities
※ Relevant Identity 1: Dieci South Section 4 Yi Sang (Uptie 4 - Skill 3)
- Before: [On Use] At 50%- HP, Coin Power +1
- After: [On Use] "At less than 50% HP", Coin Power +1
※ Relevant Identity 2: Heishou Pack - Mao Branch Adept Faust (Uptie 3 - Skill 3-1)
- Before: [Clash Win] Inflict +2 Rupture
- After: [Clash Win] Inflict +2 Rupture "Count"
※ Relevant Identity 3: LCE E.G.O::Ardor Blossom Star Faust (Uptie 4 - Skill 3-1)
- Before: [On Use] If this unit is missing 30%+ HP, Coin Power +1 (max 2)
- After: [On Use] "For every 30% missing HP on this unit", Coin Power +1 (max 2)

▶ Added "Threadspinning Tier 5" item to the sorting filters on the E.G.O selection screen

▶ Fixed an issue where, in the Mirror Dungeon Hidden Boss battle, the battle would be treated as a defeat if all allied units on the field are killed, even when there are Backup units available

▶ Fixed an issue where, when using "Dark Mode" UI, the grace period pop-up text displayed following account closure request would not appear on the main screen upon logging in again

▶ Fixed an issue where a certain visual effect of an Assist Unit in the 9.5-1 "Twining Threads" battle was displayed incorrectly

▶ Fixed an issue where the damage typography displayed when the "fixed HP damage" effect activated would not reflect the increase/decrease values applied by effects on the damage-taking target
※ Application of actual damage increase/decrease values is functioning correctly; only the damage typography display issue will be fixed.

▶ Updated the display image for the Mirror Dungeon Theme Pack "Nocturnal Sweeping BokGak"

▶ Fixed an issue where, in certain situations, E.G.O images would not appear when checking equipped E.G.O from the Identity details screen

▶ Fixed an issue where, after setting "4th Match Flame Ryōshū" E.G.O as the Manager Ticket image and enhancing it to Threadspinning Tier 5, the illustration animation would not play

▶ Optimized the performance of afterimage effects displayed behind character SDs during combat
