    Additional Bug Fixes included in the May 9th Scheduled Update

    May 8, 2024

    Greetings, dear manager.

    The following are additional issues that have been addressed since the May 9th Scheduled Update Notice.

    ▶ Additional Issues Addressed

    ▶ Fixed Simulation & Ritornello Enemy Buff(Mounting Trials) text to match the actual values

    ▶ Upon clearing Simulation F1
    (1) Take Less Damage
    - Text Value: Damage Taken -4%
    - Actual Value: Damage Taken -5%

    ▶ Upon clearing Simulation F3 (Reworked Simulation)

    (2) Body Growth
    - Text Value: Offense Level +4, Defense Level +4, Max HP +10%
    - Actual Value: Offense Level +3, Defense Level +2, Max HP +10%

    (3) Body Fortification
    - Text Value: Offense Level +6, Max HP +12.5%
    - Actual Value: Offense Level +5, Max HP +7.5%

    (4) Base Combat Gear
    - Text Value: Base Power +1, Defense Level +5, Max HP +12.5%
    - Actual Value: Base Power +1, Defense Level +3, Max HP +5%

    (5) Final Combat Gear
    - Text Value: Final Power +2, Defense Level -2, Max HP +15%
    - Actual Value: Final Power +1, Defense Level -2, Max HP +17.5%

    (6) Defense Gear
    - Text Value: Defense Level +3, Max HP +15%
    - Actual Value: Defense Level +3, Max HP +22.5%

    (7) Coin Attack Boost
    - Text Value: Plus Coin Power +1, Minus Coin Power -1, Defense Level +6
    - Actual Value: Plus Coin Power +1, Minus Coin Power -1, Defense Level +2

    ▶ Upon Clearing Ritornello F1

    (8) Damage Taken Down
    - Text Value: Damage Taken -5%
    - Actual Value: Damage Taken -7.5%

    ▶ Fixed an issue where certain E.G.O Gifts had incorrect descriptions

    ▶ Fixed an issue where certain stage maps were incorrectly connected

    We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience.
