May 2nd New Content - The 3rd Walpurgis Night

2024-04-29

---------------------------------------------

Greetings, Dear Manager.

On May 2nd, 2024 (KST), new content 「The 3rd Walpurgis Night」 will be added to the game.

- Event Period: 5/2 12:00 ~ 5/16 10:00 (KST)

New Content
-----------

- Announcers : Yesod Announcer, Molar Office Announcer
- E.G.O [WAW] : Sanguine Desire Rodion
- E.G.O [HE] : Wingbeat Ishmael
- 3★ : Dawn Office Fixer Sinclair

※ Notice: Extraction Rate Changes during the Event Period
During the Walpurgis Night Event, Extractable Announcers will be added to the game.

Due to this addition, the Extraction rates for certain Extractions will be changed while the Walpurgis Night event is active.

In compliance with the 「Voluntary Restraint Guidelines for Probabilistic Items」 of Korea Association of Game Industry, we would like to inform you about the changing Extraction rates via this notice.

(This information can also be viewed via [The 2nd Walpurgis Night Event] Notice from the Jan. 11th, 2024 Steam Announcements Page)

Please note this information as you play the game.

Extractions
-----------

「The 3rd Walpurgis Night」 Target Extraction will be featured over the Event period.

「The 3rd Walpurgis Night」 Target Extraction features increased Extraction rates for the following Identities, E.G.O, and Announcers.

► Extractable

Announcers : Yesod Announcer, Molar Office Announcers
E.G.O [WAW] : Sanguine Desire Rodion
E.G.O [HE] : Wingbeat Ishmael
3★ Identity : Dawn Office Fixer Sinclair

► Identities & E.G.O
During the Event period, all current and previous 「Walpurgis Night」 event Identities and E.G.O will be Extractable from the following Extractions.

The previous 「Walpurgis Night」 Identities and E.G.O do not receive increased Extraction rates.

「The 3rd Walpurgis Night」 Identities receive increased Extraction rates only for the ‘The 3rd Walpurgis Night Target Extraction’, and not other Extractions.

► Extractions
- The 3rd Walpurgis Night Target Extraction
- Target Extraction : Ryōshū
- New Manager’s Welcome Extraction
- Assured Extraction: 3★ Identity Extraction (※ Does not include E.G.Os)
- Standard Extraction

► Featured Identities & E.G.O

E.G.O [WAW] : Sanguine Desire Rodion
E.G.O [HE] : Wingbeat Ishmael
E.G.O [TETH] : Regret Meursault
3★ : Dawn Office Fixer Sinclair, Lobotomy E.G.O::Magic Bullet Outis, Lobotomy E.G.O::Regret Faust
2★ : Lobotomy E.G.O::Lantern Don Quixote, Hook Office Fixer Hong Lu

► Announcers
During the Event period, all current and previous 「Walpurgis Night」 event Announcers will be Extractable from the following Extractions.

The Announcers’ Extraction rate does not affect the Extraction rates of 3★ Identities. However, 2★ and 1★ units have lower Extraction rates to make room for the Announcers’ Extraction rates.

Please note that Announcers can be Extracted only from the following list of Extractions.

The ongoing Walpurgis Night Event featured Announcers' Extraction rates will go up for [The 3rd Walpurgis Night Target Extraction]. However, previous [Walpurgis Night] Announcers will not have higher Extraction rates.

A duplicate of an already-owned Announcer will be automatically converted to 50 Thread Crates.

► Extractions
- The 3rd Walpurgis Night Target Extraction

► Featured Announcers

Announcers : Yesod Announcer, Molar Office Announcer, Malkuth Announcer, Jack/Pierre Announcer, Angela Announcer

※ Angela Announcer, who was available as a free 1st Anniversary Event reward, has been added to the Walpurgis Night Extraction List.

Extractions - Yisang Exchange
-----------------------------

Yisang Exchange popup will now be separated into two categories: Identities/E.G.O and Announcers.

The following Identities & E.G.O will be available from 「The 3rd Walpurgis Night」 Extraction Yisang Exchange.

E.G.O [WAW] : Sanguine Desire Rodion
E.G.O [HE] : Wingbeat Ishmael
3★ : Dawn Office Fixer Sinclair

Announcers from the previous Walpurgis Nights will be added to the Yisang Exchange list.
The following Announcers will be available from 「The 3rd Walpurgis Night」 Extraction Yisang Exchange.

Announcers : Yesod Announcer, Molar Office Announcer, Malkuth Announcer, Jack/Pierre Announcer, Angela Announcer

Please note that any exchanged duplicates of Identities & Announcers that have already been obtained will be immediately be converted to 50 Thread Crates.

Dispenser
---------

「The 3rd Walpurgis Night」 Event period, you can purchase all Identities and E.G.Os featured in the previous 「Walpurgis Night」 Events.

However, Identities featured in the currently ongoing 「The 3rd Walpurgis Night」 event cannot be purchased from the Dispenser.

Please note that 「Walpurgis Night」 Event Announcers will not be featured in the Dispenser.

► Available from the Dispenser

E.G.O [TETH] : Regret Meursault
3★ : Lobotomy E.G.O::Magic Bullet Outis, Lobotomy E.G.O::Regret Faust
2★ : Lobotomy E.G.O::Lantern Don Quixote, Hook Office Fixer Hong Lu

► Unavailable from the Dispenser

E.G.O [WAW] : Sanguine Desire Rodion
E.G.O [HE] : Wingbeat Ishmael
3★ : Dawn Office Fixer Sinclair

5/2 Linked Content - Mini Episode
---------------------------------

With the start of the Event, a new Mini Episode 「The 3rd Walpurgis Night」 will be introduced to the game.

- Unlock Conditions : Clear 6-48

※ Upon first entry, Managers who have not completed Stage 6-48 will be asked to confirm if they want to view the story before proceeding to the “Event Encounter - Into a Certain Library’s Book”

5/2 Linked Content - Into a Certain Library’s Book
--------------------------------------------------

During the Event period, linked content 「Into a Certain Library’s Book」 will be introduced to the game.

- Event Period : 5/2 12:00 ~ 5/16 10:00 (KST)
- Event Reward Exchange Period : 5/2 12:00 ~ 5/23 10:00 (KST)
- Unlock Conditions : None

<Description>
- With the start of the Event, the 「Into a Certain Library’s Book」 Event page will be added to the game.
- Complete special Missions(challenges) in the Easy and Hard difficulty Encounters to receive Mission Rewards.

<Event Progression>
- 「The 3rd Walpurgis Night - Into a Certain Library’s Book」 Event can be accessed regardless of the Main Story progress.

- There are two difficulties to the ‘Into a Certain Library’s Book’ Encounter: Easy and Hard.

- Completing the ‘Into a Certain Library’s Book’ Encounters will consume an Enkephalin Module. Clear the Easy difficulty to unlock the Hard difficulty.

- In ‘Easy’ difficulty, if your Identity’s Level is below 45, it will be raised to 45. Also, if your Identity’s Uptie Level is below Tier 3, it will be raised to Tier 3. However, your current E.G.O Threadspinning Tier will be reflected.

- During the Event Period, fulfill Missions(challenges) in each of the Easy and Hard difficulties to obtain their Rewards from the ‘Missions’ menu.

<Missions>
- View Missions(challenges) to complete in the [Missions] menu. Every mission provides a reward upon completion.

- There are Easy and Hard Missions, which are Missions that can be completed in Easy and Hard difficulties, respectively.

- Once the Event closes on May 16th 2024, 10:00 (KST), you can no longer complete your Missions.

- [Rewards Claim] will remain open for 3 weeks from May. 2nd 2024, 12:00 ~ May 23rd 2024, 10:00 (KST).

- Even after the event fully ends, you will be able to experience the 'The 3rd Walpurgis Night - Into a Certain Library’s Book' combat Encounters and view its story anytime at the Driverside and Theater - [Detour Tales].

- From May 16th, 2024, 12:00 ~ May 23rd 2024, 10:00, you can only revisit the story and not the Encounter. You can revisit the Combat Encounter after May 23rd 2024, 10:00 (KST) via the Driverside menu.

<Event Team Loadout>
- The 1st Sinner to be deployed in the Teams menu will be imbued with the 'Librarian of History' effect, which will vary depending on which difficulty Encounter you are running. The Sinner with the 'Librarian of History' effect will gain more Skill Slots and gain other beneficial effects.

<Event Combat Encounter>
- ‘Into a Certain Library’s Book’ Encounter is a Suppression of a ‘Fairy Festival’ Abnormality.

- Suppress the Abnormality, complete various missions, and earn various Event Rewards from them.

- However, going past 99 Turns will result in defeat.
