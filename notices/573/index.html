2026, Apr. 9th Scheduled Update Notice

2026-04-08

-------------------------------------

Greetings, Dear Manager.

The server will enter scheduled maintenance from 10:00 [AM] through 12:00 [PM] on Apr. 9th, 2026 (KST).

Please make note of the maintenance schedule; we recommend that you refrain from entering combat or making in-app purchases and close the application before maintenance begins so that no data is lost.

Please refer below for details about the update.

New Update to the Mirror of Names and Spiders
--------------------------------------------

With the Apr. 9th Scheduled Update, additional updates will be deployed to the Mirror of Names and Spiders EXTREME Mode.

※ Due to the update maintenance, entry to the Mirror Dungeon will be unavailable from 06:00 to 12:00 (KST) on Apr. 9th, 2026. Please note the above for your gameplay schedule.

► Replacement of Hidden Bosses in EXTREME Mode
- The four existing Hidden Bosses will be replaced with new "Mirror of Names and Spiders EXTREME Mode" Hidden Bosses.
- As before, Hidden Bosses will appear with a low probability from Floor 11 onward, and defeating them will yield exclusive E.G.O Gifts.
※ Replaced Hidden Bosses will be updated as bosses for EXTREME Mode-exclusive Theme Packs at a later date. The specifications of these existing bosses and their E.G.O Gifts may be adjusted upon the said update.

► New Selectable Adversities in EXTREME Mode
- Each floor will now feature a new selectable Adversity.
- Selecting all Adversities up to Floor 15 will yield a total Adversity Score of 60.
- Adversities exist solely for high-difficulty challenges or achievement completion in EXTREME Mode.

► Mirror of Names and Spiders – Mirror Projection / Achievement Part IV - Hidden
※ Hidden Achievements do not contribute to overall Achievement progress.

► Mirror of Names and Spiders – Mirror Projection Rate can now be purchased with Paid Lunacy.
※ Mirror Projection Rate can now be purchased to reach the maximum level even when the last digit of the current progress is not 0.

Bug Fixes & Improvements
------------------------

► Fixed an issue where LCE E.G.O::AEDD Gregor does not count for certain Achievements and E.G.O Gift Activation conditionals.
- Relevant Achievement: Clear Mirror Dungeon at Floor 5 or higher with 5 or more Rupture Keyword Identities in your Loadout at the time of completion.
- Relevant E.G.O Gift Effect: Turn Start: This Gift activates for the whole Encounter when 5 or more Identities have Attack Skills that apply Rupture Potency or Count.

► Fixed an issue where Lobotomy E.G.O::Lamp Gregor's "Guardians of the Forest" effect "Minus Coin Skills lose 8 SP (this effect cannot reduce the unit's SP below -40)" can reduce the unit's SP below -40.

► Fixed an issue where the E.G.O Gift "Hemorrhagic Shock" effect "Enemies become Staggerable by Bleed Damage" bypasses the "Cannot be Staggered before using this Skill" condition and Staggers the enemy anyway.

► Fixed an issue where, when the E.G.O Gift "Perpetual Generator Servos" effect "In this Encounter, when an ally takes damage that would kill them, consume all Charge Count and 'Unique Charge' (fixed Potency) on self, then, immediately heal (10 + sum of Charge Count and 'Unique Charge' (fixed Potency) consumed)% HP" activates, the effect also consumes Potency stacks of 'Unique Charge' with unfixed Potency despite the description saying otherwise.

► Fixed an issue where, under certain conditions, the effects of the E.G.O Gift "Sharp Needle & Thread" would not activate properly.

► Fixed an issue where the game becomes unresponsive when an Identity uses a Skill while projected with the Facade of "The Princess of La Manchaland Rodion".

► Fixed an issue where visual effects from "The Ring Pointillist Student Outis" attacks continue to linger on screen.

► Fixed a story progression blocking issue where the game becomes unresponsive if "The House of Spiders: The Ring Nursefather Hong Lu" is participating in Stage 5-36.

► Fixed an issue where the game becomes unresponsive if, in Stage 7-36, the user wins the Encounter and enters a cutscene while the enemy unit has the "Faint Aroma" effect.

► Fixed an issue where certain enemy units' Skill, Panic, and Passive descriptions did not match their behavior (updated description to match behavior).
(1) Main Story 5-36 - Starting Hunt, Harpooning
- Before : [On Hit] Inflict 2 Bleed
- After : [On Hit] Inflict 4 Bleed

(2) Main Story 9-37, 9-43 - Panic Type "Outmoded Art" (both Low Morale and Panic)
- Before : Turn Start: Lose X HP and gain Y Offense Level Up
- After : Turn Start: Gain X Offense Level Up

(3) Refraction Railway Line 1 BokGak - Passive "HP Ampule Injection"
- Before : At the start of the turn, if this unit's HP is not at full, heal 25% of Max HP (Up to 3 times)
- After : Turn Start: If this unit's HP is not at full, heal 25% HP (max 3 times; recover from Stagger if this unit is Staggered)

※ To maintain the current gameplay experience, the descriptions will be updated to match their current behaviors.

► Fixed an issue where the Mirror Dungeon boss enemy "Portrait of a Certain Day" Encounter did not give additional Skill Slots when playing with fewer Sinners than the Participant Limit.

► Fixed an issue where the unique visual effect that appears after The House of Spiders: The Ring Nursefather Hong Lu uses the Skill "Closing Time - Installation Art no. 1: Your Flesh and Bones as the Gallery's Seats" appears in story cutscenes as well.

► Fixed an issue where, if The House of Spiders: The Index Nursefather Yi Sang dies after meeting the conditions for his Empowered Skill 3, the relevant visual effects continue to linger.

► Clarified a vague Achievement description for one of the Mirror Projection / Achievement challenges.
- Before : Defeat the "Abnormal Seismic Zone" Theme Pack boss while carrying Hardwood Liquor Cup and 5 or more liquor-related E.G.O Gifts: "Green Spirit", "Blood, Sweat, and Tears", "Wobbling Keg", "Reverberation", "Chalice of Trickle-down", "Downpour", "Devil's Share", "Angel's Cut", "Trash Crab Brain Wine", "Shaoxing Wine".
- After : Defeat the "Abnormal Seismic Zone" Theme Pack boss while carrying 5 or more liquor-related E.G.O Gifts: "Hardwood Liquor Cup", "Green Spirit", "Blood, Sweat, and Tears", "Wobbling Keg", "Reverberation", "Chalice of Trickle-down", "Downpour", "Devil's Share", "Angel's Cut", "Trash Crab Brain Wine", "Shaoxing Wine".

※ The achievement's current conditions reflect the "After" description. We are updating the description to clarify sections that could be misinterpreted.

► Fixed an issue where progressing through the Mirror Dungeon during its weekly reset period (Thursday, 06:00 (KST)) causes progression issues.

► Fixed an issue where, for a certain enemy in Main Story Canto 9, relevant visual effects still occur even when a certain enemy's "[On Use] At 0+ SP, generate 1 Mang (望)" effect cannot be activated.

► Fixed an issue where, in Mirror Dungeon Shops, the currently owned Skill count may show an incorrect number under certain circumstances.

► Fixed an issue where certain Sinners' skill effect activation caused the typography text boxes to appear abnormally.

► Fixed an issue where certain E.G.O Gifts were missing highlights for changes between Enhancement levels.
※ Relevant E.G.O Gifts : The Spider's Thread, Omni-tool
