Major Changes

1. Unique Status Effects
Unique Status Effects, which are status effects that apply Burn or Bleed via indirect means, will now be each categorized as [Unique Status Effects].
We have added [Unique Status Effects] conditions for certain E.G.O Gift activation conditions so that relevant Identities can properly synergize with them.

※ This change is not limited to Burn or Bleed. If any new indirect ways of applying other Status Effects are added to the game, they will be categorized as [Unique Status Effects] as well.

▶ Unique Status Effects
- Nails
- Dark Flame (Added after the Jan. 11th Scheduled Update)

Major Changes

2. Mirror Dungeon: Certain E.G.O Gift activation conditions & effects updated

▶ Hellterfly’s Dream activation conditions
- Before : When inflicting Burn Potency to enemies with Burn
After : When inflicting Burn Potency or Unique Burn to enemies with Burn or Unique Burn

▶ Wound Clerid activation conditions
- Before : When hitting an enemy with a Skill that inflicts Bleed Potency
- After : When hitting an enemy with a Skill that inflicts Bleed Potency or Unique Bleed

▶ Fiery Down activation conditions
- Before : When hitting an enemy with a Skill that inflicts Burn Potency or a Lust Affinity Skill
- After : When hitting an enemy with a Lust Affinity Skill or a Skill that inflicts Burn Potency or Unique Burn

▶ Pinpoint Logic Circuit activation conditions
- Before : When hitting an enemy with an Envy Affinity Skill or a Skill that inflicts Burn Potency
- After : When hitting an enemy with an Envy Affinity Skill or a Skill that inflicts Burn Potency or Unique Burn

▶ Rusted Cutting Knife activation conditions
- Before : When inflicting Bleed Potency with Skill effects against non-Staggered enemies
- After : When inflicting Bleed Potency or Unique Bleed with Skill effects against non-Staggered enemies

▶ Soothe the Dead activation conditions & effects
Before : Encounter Start: This Gift activates when 5 or more Identities have Attack Skills that apply Burn Potency. (...) To Skills that inflict Burn Potency: Final Power +1, Coin Power +1, inflicted Burn Potency +1, inflicted Burn Count +1.
- After : Encounter Start: This Gift activates when 5 or more Identities have Attack Skills that apply Burn Potency, Count, or Unique Burn. (...) To Skills that inflict Burn Potency, Count, or Unique Burn: Final Power +1, Coin Power +1, inflicted Burn Potency +1, inflicted Burn Count +1.

Major Changes

▶ Bloody Mist activation conditions & effects
- Before : Encounter Start: This Gift Activates when 5 or more Identities have Attack Skills that apply Bleed Potency. (...) Skills that inflict Bleed Potency: Coin Power +2, damage dealt +100%
- After : Encounter Start: This Gift Activates when 5 or more Identities have Attack Skills that apply Bleed Potency, Count, or Unique Bleed. (...) Skills that inflict Bleed Potency, Count, or Unique Bleed: Coin Power +2, damage dealt +100%

▶ Enrapturing Trance activation conditions

- After : Encounter Start: This Gift activates when 5 or more Identities have Attack Skills that apply Rupture Potency or Count

▶ Black Sheet Music activation conditions
- Before : Encounter Start: This Gift activates when 5 or more Identities have Attack Skills that apply Sinking Potency
- After : Encounter Start: This Gift activates when 5 or more Identities have Attack Skills that apply Sinking Potency or Count
