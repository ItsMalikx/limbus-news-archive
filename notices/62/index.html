May 11th Scheduled Update Notice

2023-05-08

--------------------------------

Greetings, Dear Manager.

The server will enter maintenance from 10:00 [AM] through 12:00 [PM] on May 11th, 2023 (KST) for system optimizations and content updates.
Please make note of the maintenance schedule; we recommend that you refrain from entering combat or making in-app purchases and close the application before maintenance begins so that no data is lost.
Refer to below for details about the update.

Update Contents
---------------

1. New Content - Roll Call

[Event Period]
May 11 12:00 ~ Jun 1 05:59 (KST)

[Event Description]
- During the event period, you can receive log-in rewards each day upon entering the game, resetting at 6 AM (KST).
- Unclaimed rewards are lost the next day, so please make sure to redeem them in time.

2. New Content - Dante’s Notes

[Description]
- Dante's Notes will be added to the Theater with the May 11th scheduled update.

[Linked Events]
- During the Roll Call event period (May 11 12:00 ~ Jun 1 05:59 (KST)), a new entry will be added to Dante’s Notes each day.

3. Bug Fixes and improvements

- Added a button to move to the store page when an application update is needed (Android, iOS)
- Added words and phrases for the intro taglines unlocked by acquiring The One Who Shall Grip Sinclair and N Corp. Mittelhammer Don Quixote
- Fixed an issue where effects would linger during the animation for The One Who Shall Grip Sinclair's ‘Self-destructive Purge’
- Fixed an issue where the random targeting of R Corp. Ishmael's ‘Mind Whip’ did not apply properly
- Fixed an issue where support passives would activate twice under certain circumstances
- Fixed an issue where massive reductions to Speed due to Bind or other causes could set the unit's Speed to 0
- Fixed an issue where the 'back' button would be erroneously displayed on certain UI elements
- Fixed an issue where particle effects would linger when transitioning between the Window and Driverside tabs
- Fixed an issue where the effects of Spine artwork would cover the subtitles
- Fixed an issue where the E.G.O resource requirements-display for support passives would not be updated
- Fixed an issue where the 'back' button would disappear from E.G.O details tab in Mirror Dungeons
- Fixed an issue where the UI would malfunction after enlarging artwork in Identity or E.G.O details
- Fixed an issue where active status effect text would sometimes be omitted during unopposed attacks
- Fixed an issue where certain battle announcer dialogues were omitted during combat

Maintenance & Application Update Compensation
---------------------------------------------

- Content: 300 Lunacy
- Recipients: Every user whose game account was created before 09:59 May 18th, 2023 (KST)

Notes
-----

- Please update your game client to the newest version on the respective store (Google Play/App Store/Steam).
- Forcibly shutting down the game while it is downloading resources may cause the download to fail, so please be careful not to close the application during the download.
- You will not be able to play <Limbus Company> during maintenance. Please understand.
- The maintenance schedule may suddenly change depending on the status of maintenance. If such a change occurs, we will let you know in a follow-up notice.
- A resource download may be prompted after the build update. If you're using a mobile device, it is recommended to proceed with a Wi-Fi connection.
- If you're playing with a guest account, we recommend that you link your account with other platforms to prevent possible account loss.
- The maintenance compensation will be provided once per account. Please note that the distribution may be slow due to network delays or other causes.
- The update gifts are valid to claim for 7 days. Please redeem them within this timeframe.

Thank you.
