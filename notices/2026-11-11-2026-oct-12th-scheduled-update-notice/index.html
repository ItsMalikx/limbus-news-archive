Project Moon 10th Anniversary Pass Verification

The Project Moon 10th Anniversary Pass Verification button will become available after the Nov. 12th, 2026 Scheduled Update.

- The "Project Moon 10th Anniv. Pass Verification" button will appear only for Managers at Manager Level 50 or higher. It will also appear if you reach Manager Level 50 after the Scheduled Update.

- At the concert venue on Nov. 15th, an on-site staff member will personally enter the code to verify your pass.

- Only Managers whose passes have been verified by an on-site staff member can exchange their passes for concert entry wristbands and enter the venue.

※ Please do not enter the verification code yourself. If you complete verification on your own, you cannot be verified again and will be unable to exchange your pass for an entry wristband or enter the concert venue.

Removal of the "Individual Rate" Column from "Probabilities" UI

We have identified an issue in the "Probabilities" UI for "New Extraction" and "Target Extraction" where the "Individual Rate" column in the [Extract 1] and [10 Extractions (10th)] tables displays values that do not account for whether an Identity or E.G.O is featured with an increased rate.

The increased rates have been applied correctly in actual Extractions. The Extraction rates shown for each Identity and E.G.O under [List] in "Probability Details" have also been accurate.

Although actual Extraction rates and obtained results have not been affected, we will remove the "Individual Appearance Rate" column from the [Single Extraction] and [10 Extractions (10th)] Table in the Nov. 12th Scheduled Update to prevent confusion.

This change will not affect actual Extraction rates. You can continue to check the rate of each Identity and E.G.O under [List].

We apologize for the confusion.

Yi Sang & Don Quixote's Speech Mannerisms to be Reverted in JP Translation

Yi Sang and Don Quixote's speech mannerisms, which were changed with the Japanese Translation update on Oct. 29th, 2026 Scheduled Update, will be reverted to their pre-translation update versions.

Further details will be shared via an additional notice.
