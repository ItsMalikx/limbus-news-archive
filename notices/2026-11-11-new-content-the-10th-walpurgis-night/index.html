New Content - The 10th Walpurgis Night

November 11, 2026
EventsMirror DungeonNew Content

※ Some typos have been found in EN/JP notices and will be edited.
※ (20:05)EN/JP proofreading has been completed. Notice images have been updated accordingly.

Greetings, Dear Manager.

On Nov. 12th, 2026 (KST), new content 「The 10th Walpurgis Night」 will be added to the game.

- Event Period: 2026/11/12 12:00 ~ 11/26 10:00 (KST)

New Content

- Announcer : Hokma Announcer
- E.G.O [HE] : The Sword Sharpened with Tears Hong Lu
- 3★ : Wedge Office Fixer Don Quixote & Sinclair, Wedge Office Rep Outis

※ Notice: Extraction Rate Changes during the Event Period
During the Walpurgis Night Event, Extractable Announcers will be added to the game.

Due to this addition, the Extraction rates for certain Extractions will be changed while the Walpurgis Night event is active.

In compliance with the 「Voluntary Restraint Guidelines for Probabilistic Items」 of the Korea Association of Game Industry, the exact Extraction rates are disclosed in the in-game menu "Detailed Rates."

Please note this information as you play the game.

▶ Pair Identity Details

- Wedge Office Fixer Don Quixote and Wedge Office Fixer Sinclair are treated as a single 3★ Identity in the Extraction. Obtaining either Identity will always yield the other in the pair.

- Starlight acquisition via Extraction or Yisang[ldeality] Exchange also treats this pair as a single 3★ Identity, and obtaining this Identity via Extraction or Yisang[ldeality] Exchange will yield 300 Starlight.

- Obtaining an extra copy of this Identity pair will, instead of the usual rarity-based amount of Sinner Egoshards, yield 25 Sinner Egoshards of the current Season for each Sinner in the pair.

- In Yisang[ldeality] Exchange, it costs 200 Yisang[ldeality] to exchange for both Identities at once.

- Once the pair is added to the Dispenser, both Identities can be obtained together for a combined total of 400 Don Quixote and Sinclair Egoshards.

- Deploying "Wedge Office Fixer Don Quixote" in the Team Loadout menu will automatically deploy "Wedge Office Fixer Sinclair", and vice versa. If "Wedge Office Fixer Don Quixote" is swapped out for a different Don Quixote Identity, or is removed from Deployment, "Wedge Office Fixer Sinclair" will also be removed in tandem. The same applies in the reverse scenario where "Wedge Office Fixer Sinclair" is removed first instead of Don Quixote.

※ The Identity pair is treated as separate Identities for Uptying purposes, and each Identity costs the usual amount of Thread and Egoshards to Uptie a single 3★ Identity.

※ The Identity pair is treated as separate Identities for Façade Projection purposes, and each Identity costs its respective Threadskeins, Egoshards, and Thread to obtain.

※ Both Identities in the pair can be borrowed at once using the friend Support Identity system. However, the upgrade progress of each Identity will reflect the said friend's own.

※ When purchasing the Identity pair from the Dispenser, you must use at least 1 Egoshard from each Sinner in the pair. Please note that this Identity pair cannot be purchased from the Dispenser with Egoshards from only a single Sinner, Don Quixote or Sinclair's.

11/12 Linked Content - The Wedge in the Wake of Dawnbreak

Linked content 「The Wedge in the Wake of Dawnbreak」 will be introduced with the start of the Event.

- Event Period: 2026/11/12 12:00 ~ 11/26 10:00 (KST)
- Event Reward Exchange Period: 2026/11/12 12:00 ~ 12/3 10:00 (KST)
- Unlock Conditions: None

<Description>

- With the start of the Event, the 「[The Wedge in the Wake of Dawnbreak」 Event page will be added to the game.

- Complete special Missions (challenges) in the Easy and Hard difficulty Encounters to receive Mission Rewards.

<Event Progression>

- 「The 10th Walpurgis Night - The Wedge in the Wake of Dawnbreak」 Event is unlocked regardless of your Main Story progress.
※ Once the Event closes, however, the unlock condition will change to Clear Stage 9.5-26 and Chapter 10.

- The Wedge in the Wake of Dawnbreak has two difficulties: Easy and Hard.

- Completing these Encounters will consume an Enkephalin Module each. Clear the Easy difficulty to unlock the Hard difficulty.

- The following effects will be active in the Easy difficulty:

- If the Identity's level is lower than 65, raise their level to 65

- If Identities' Uptie Tiers or equipped E.G.O's Threadspinning Tiers are lower than Tier 3, raise them to Tier 3

- During the Event Period, fulfill Missions (challenges) in each of the Easy and Hard difficulties to obtain their Rewards from the 'Missions' menu.

<Event Combat Encounter>

- In "The Wedge in the Wake of Dawnbreak" Encounter, you will be participating in a Reception against certain individuals who have accepted the Invitation.

- The Encounter is entered with a 5-Identity Team Loadout, and the #1 Sinner in the Deployment Order will be imbued with the "Patron Librarian [Floor of History]" effect.

- Patron Librarian [Floor of History]: Increased Action Slots, changes to Skill composition, and various beneficial effects

- The Encounter will feature a fixed, special Announcer.

- In "The Wedge in the Wake of Dawnbreak" Encounter, you can use the "Retry Battle" function in the in-battle menu to start the Encounter again from the beginning.

- The Encounter ends once the Reception of a certain individual ends.

<Missions>

- View Missions (challenges) to complete in the [Missions] menu. Every mission provides a reward upon completion.

- There are Easy and Hard Missions, which can be completed in Easy and Hard difficulties, respectively.

- Once the Event closes on 2026/11/26 10:00 (KST), you can no longer complete your Missions.

- [Rewards Claim] will remain open for 3 weeks from 2026/11/12 12:00 ~ 2026/12/3 10:00 (KST).

- Even after the event fully ends, you will be able to experience "The 10th Walpurgis Night - The Wedge in the Wake of Dawnbreak" combat Encounters and view its story any time on the Drive tab and Theater - [Detour Tales].

- From 2026/11/12 12:00 ~ 2026/11/26 10:00 (KST), you can revisit the story but not the Encounter. The Combat Encounter will become available again after 2026/12/3 12:00 (KST) via the Driver side menu.
