2026, Feb. 12th Scheduled Update Notice

February 11, 2026
BalanceMain Story

Greetings, Dear Manager.

The server will enter scheduled maintenance from 10:00 [AM] through 12:00 [PM] on Feb. 12th, 2026 (KST).

Please make note of the maintenance schedule; we recommend that you refrain from entering combat or making in-app purchases and close the application before maintenance begins so that no data is lost.

Please refer below for details about the update.

Bug Fixes & Improvements

▶ (EN Only) Fixed a mistranslation regarding the Stack reduction condition of The Prince of La Manchaland Meursault's 'Focused Performance' effect

- Before: After attacking with Skill 3, or at Turn Start, if this unit does not have Hardblood Armor, lose 1 Stack

- After: Lose 1 Stack when:
1. After attacking with Skill 3
2. If this unit does not have Hardblood Armor at Turn Start

※ This is a text-only fix; no actual gameplay implementation was changed.

▶ Fixed an issue where the 'Base Power +(value)' typography text for Jeong's Office Rep Ishmael's 'Hand - Suit' effects appeared as though triggered even when it did not correspond to the Hand - Suit

※ This is a visual-only UI fix; no actual effect was confirmed to have been triggered.

▶ Fixed an issue in Refraction Railway where, upon re-entering a cleared Stage with an Identity of a higher level than the same one of a lower level used to clear it, the HP value was forcibly adjusted based on the previously used Identity

▶ Fixed an issue where repeated check failures for the option 'Pick a rose.' during Mirror Dungeon Abnormality 'Sign of Roses' Event could result in a server error

- Before: 'Pick a rose.' could be selected without a retry limit after check failures

- After: Failing the 'Pick a rose' check 3 times will no longer allow retries

▶ Fixed an issue where, if both Ally units and enemies died simultaneously when the battle still had remaining waves, the battle would proceed to the next wave without registering as a defeat, blocking game progression

▶ Fixed an issue where the camera position was incorrectly shifted downward upon first entering combat in Stage 8.5-4

▶ Fixed an issue where the prerequisite condition text for viewing Main Story Chapter 9 in the Theater was displayed as UNKNOWN

▶ (JP Only) Fixed a mistranslation of a proper noun in The House of Spiders: The Pinky Apprentice Sinclair's voiceline

- Before: 父親
- After: 親方

▶ Improvements made to address cases where, under certain conditions, SD characters could display abnormally during battle clear cinematics while progressing through Main Story Chapter 9

▶ Improvements made to the text float for Passives effects during combat so that summarized text is shown instead

※ Affected Identities and Skills: The Index Proselyte: [Paper Slip] Faust's 'Support Passive', Firefist Office Survivor Gregor's 'Combat Passives'

▶ Improvements made to reduce potential frame drops occurring during the calculation of Clash result predictions when chaining Skills automatically via the 'Win Rate' or 'Damage' buttons, or by manually assigning Skills to Clash with enemy Slots

▶ Fixed an issue where Passives information was displayed incorrectly on the Uptie screen when Uptiying Blade Lineage Mentor Meursault from Tier 3 to Tier 4

Notes

- Please update your game client to the newest version on the respective store (Google Play/App Store/Steam).
- Forcibly shutting down the game while it is downloading resources may cause the download to fail, so please be careful not to close the application during the download.

- If you're playing with a guest account, we recommend that you link your account with other platforms to prevent possible account loss.
- The maintenance compensation will be provided once per account. Please note that the distribution may be slow due to network delays or other causes.
- The update gifts are valid to claim for 7 days. Please redeem them within this timeframe.

Thank you.
