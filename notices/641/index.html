New Content

1. Window BGM Change function and the new Jukebox function

A new feature to replace the Window menu BGM with any track of your choice will be added.

The Jukebox function will allow you to listen to the BGM tracks owned.

[How to Change the BGM]

Change the Window menu BGM by navigating to the "Custom Settings" button (circling arrows button) in the upper-right corner of the Window screen and selecting the "Change BGM" tab. Customize the music via the "Set Window BGM" and "Set Sinner BGM" buttons.

※ Window BGM allows you to replace the default music played in the in-game screen with BGM tracks of your choice.

※ Up to 50 BGM tracks can be added to the Window BGM playlist.

※ Sinner BGM allows you to assign and play one BGM track for each Sinner on the Window screen.

※ On the upper-left corner of the PDA UI displayed on the Window screen, you can toggle between the "DEFAULT" and "SINNER" buttons to switch between the Window BGM and Sinner BGM.

[Using the Jukebox Function]

You can listen to the BGM you own via the "Jukebox" function in the Theater menu.

※ The playlist in the Jukebox is shared with the Window BGM. Any changes to your playlist will be applied to the Window BGM as well.

[Obtaining and Purchasing BGM Tracks]

12 BGM tracks are provided by default. All other BGM tracks can be obtained or purchased once their respective requirements are met.

Please refer to the list below for details on how to obtain each BGM track.

※ BGM Acquisition Methods

1. Main Story BGM (Chapter 1: The Outcast ~ Chapter 9: The Unsevering)
- All corresponding BGM tracks are obtained upon clearing the final stage of each Main Story Chapter

2. Intervallo BGM (Chapter 3.5: Hell's Chicken ~ Chapter 9.5-2 Mnestic Experience)
- Can be exchanged using event currency at the Reward Exchange during the event period
- Unowned BGM tracks can be obtained from the Reward Exchange during Intervallo BokGaks
- Once the event has closed, BGM tracks can be purchased from the Dispenser

3. Walpurgis Night BGM
- Can be obtained as event rewards during the event period
- Once the event has closed, BGM tracks can be purchased from the Dispenser after completing the corresponding Walpurgis Night battle

4. Refraction Railway BGM (Line 1 BokGak ~ Line 6)
- Automatically obtained upon clearing the Refraction Railway
- BGM tracks from closed Refraction Railways can be obtained by clearing their respective BokGaks

5. April Fools' BGM (A Midspring Night's Dream)
- Can be obtained by viewing the story of "A Midspring Night's Dream" during the event period
- Once the event has closed, BGM tracks can be purchased from the Dispenser

※ As of the Jul. 16th Scheduled Update, BGMs up to Chapter 9.5-2 "Mnestic Experience" are available for purchase only in the Dispenser (excluding Main Story and Refraction Railway).

※ The per-BGM Dispenser price is 25 Threads.

※ Accounts with clear records of each Refraction Railway up to before the Jul. 16th Scheduled Update will automatically receive the corresponding BGM tracks. (Example: Clear records for Refraction Railway Lines 2 and 4 will automatically yield the BGM tracks for Lines 2 and 4.)

※ BGM Tracks from Collaboration events cannot be purchased regardless of story-clear progress, and will not appear in the Dispenser.

※ Please note that BGM tracks for future Intervallo events will be available for exchange through their respective Reward Exchanges.
