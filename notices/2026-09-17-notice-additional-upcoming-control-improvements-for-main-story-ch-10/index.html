    Notice: Additional Upcoming Control Improvements for Main Story Ch. 10

    September 17, 2026
    Main StoryBalance

    Hello, this is Project Moon.

    On Sep. 17th, 2026, we shared [Notice: Upcoming Control Improvements for Main Story Ch. 10] with our users.
    Today, we would like to share additional quality-of-life changes that are coming to the game.

    - (Steam - PC Only) Keymapping/binding function
    For managers playing on PC, we are adding a keymapping function for Main Story Chapter 10.
    With this function, you can customize the game's controls to the settings that are most convenient for you.

    This function applies only to a certain stage in Main Story Chapter 10, and will not affect keyboard shortcuts for the rest of Limbus Company.

    The keymapping/binding function will also be updated with the Sep. 24th Scheduled Update.
    Please check the upcoming Scheduled Update notice for more information.

    Thank you.
