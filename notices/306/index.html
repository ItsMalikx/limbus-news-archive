2024, Nov. 14th Scheduled Update Notice

November 11, 2024
Identities & E.G.ONew ContentBalanceMain Story

Greetings, Dear Manager.

The server will enter scheduled maintenance from 10:00 [AM] through 12:00 [PM] on Nov. 14th, 2024 (KST).

Please make note of the maintenance schedule; we recommend that you refrain from entering combat or making in-app purchases and close the application before maintenance begins so that no data is lost.

Please refer below for details about the update.

New Content

1. New Target Extraction - [The Princess of La Manchaland Rodion & Fell Bullet Yi Sang] (11/14 12:00 ~ 11/28 10:00 (KST))
The following Identities will have increased odds of extraction
- E.G.O [HE] : Fell Bullet Yi Sang
- 3★ : The Princess of La Manchaland Rodion

※ Please note for your Extraction plans that 'Fell Bullet Yi Sang' will be designated as a Season 5 E.G.O.

2. Target Extraction [Meursault] (11/14 12:00 ~ 11/28 10:00 (KST))
Extraction rate increases for the following Identities and E.G.O
- E.G.O [HE] : Pursuance Meursault
- 3★ : Cinq West Section 3 Meursault, Dieci South Section 4 Director Meursault, Blade Lineage Mentor Meursault, R Corp. 4th Pack Rhino Meursault, N Corp. Großhammer Meursault, W Corp. L2 Cleanup Agent Meursault
- 2★ : Dead Rabbits Boss Meursault, Middle Little Brother Meursault, Rosespanner Workshop Fixer Meursault, Liu South Section 6 Meursault
- 1★ : LCB Sinner Meursault

3. New Identity & E.G.O Added
The following Identity & E.G.O are added to the list of extractable Identities & E.G.O.
- E.G.O [HE] : Fell Bullet Yi Sang
- 3★ : The Princess of La Manchaland Rodion

Major Changes

1. Added a function to hide portraits and names for Announcers with possible spoilers

※ Unveiling Conditions
(1) Obtaining the said Announcers
(2) Clearing relevant story points
(3) Selecting the spoilered area will show a pop-up notice, and the player can unveil the said Announcer via that method

Bug Fixes & Improvements

▶ Fixed an issue where the target affected by Rodion E.G.O - Sanguine Desire's "[Combat Start] The main target for this Skill does not lose Bleed Count for this turn" could still lose Bleed Count due to Meursault E.G.O - Yearning-Mircalla's "Activate the target's Bleed X times. (target loses Y Bleed Count)"

▶ Fixed an issue where, outside Chain Battles, the "Base factors increasing/decreasing Sanity" did not activate

▶ Fixed an issue where an unintended targeting designation could occur in the interactive cutscene after clearing the final Encounter of Stage 5-36

▶ Fixed an issue where certain Choice Events during the final Encounter of Stage 4-54 did not trigger

▶ Fixed an issue where the 'More Participants' tutorial popup could not be closed upon entering Stage 4-47 for the first time
- Issue: Pressing the 'Confirm' button did not close the popup window; clicking the area outside the popup window, however, did close the window

▶ Fixed an issue where, in Main Story Canto 7 - <Part 3>, a certain character's eye was depicted in an incorrect color
※ Relevant Episode : - Theater - Canto 7 - Episode 54

Known Issues

▶ An issue where, in Regular Encounters, using an AOE E.G.O Skill against a Defense Skill showed Clash values instead of 'Unopposed Attack' values

▶ An issue where relevant Announcers' spoiler redactions are not immediately removed
※ Directly removing them or restarting the game will remove the spoiler redactions.

Maintenance & Application Update Compensation

- Content: Lunacy x300
- Recipients: All users whose game accounts were created before 10:00 Nov. 21st, 2024 (KST)
- When: After Nov. 14th, 2024 (Thu) 12:00 (KST)
- Can be claimed once per account

Maintenance Compensation can be claimed until Nov. 21st (Thu) 2024, 10:00 (KST) via the in-game Mailbox. (Mail available for 7 days)

Notes

- Please update your game client to the newest version on the respective store (Google Play/App Store/Steam).
- Forcibly shutting down the game while it is downloading resources may cause the download to fail, so please be careful not to close the application during the download.
- You will not be able to play <Limbus Company> during maintenance. Please understand.
- The maintenance schedule may suddenly change depending on the status of maintenance. If such a change occurs, we will let you know in a follow-up notice.
- A resource download may be prompted after the build update. If you're using a mobile device, it is recommended to proceed with a Wi-Fi connection.
- If you're playing with a guest account, we recommend that you link your account with other platforms to prevent possible account loss.
- The maintenance compensation will be provided once per account. Please note that the distribution may be slow due to network delays or other causes.
- The update gifts are valid to claim for 7 days. Please redeem them within this timeframe.

Thank you.
