    Hotfix : "The Symbolic" Difficulty Nerf & "The Shadowed" Boss Text Improvement

    October 1, 2026
    Main StoryBalance

    Hello, this is Project Moon.

    The hotfix described in the [Notice: "The Symbolic" Difficulty Nerf & "The Shadowed" Boss Text Improvement] notice has finished deployment.

    Please refer to the information below.

    [ Hotfixes ]

    ▶ (The Symbolic) Simplified the conditions for triggering the event where a special assistive item can be obtained, and added an additional effect to the said item
    - Before: The event would be triggered after dying 3 times.
    - After: The event is triggered after dying once.
    - Additional Effect: In the Symbolic, Damage Dealt +350
    ※ Even if you have refused the item the first time, you can talk to the relevant NPC again to receive it.

    ▶ Main Story Chapter 10 - <Part 3> "The Shadowed" 5F Boss Passive text intuitiveness improved
    New Texts:
    - When the Sinner who has a certain buff wins a Clash against a certain Skill, the boss unit gains 1 unique status effect
    - When the boss unit reaches 5 Stacks of that unique status effect, it gains a new pattern; winning the Clash against the Skill in that new pattern will win the Encounter

    [ Hotfix ETA ]

    - 10.01 (Thu) 19:30 (KST)
    - There may be a delay of approximately 10 minutes.

    ※ The Hotfix will be deployed upon restarting the game, and does not require a separate client update. However, please note that additional in-game downloads may occur.

    [ Lunacy Refunds for Using the Stage Continue Function ]

    As the description explaining the boss unit's gimmick will be updated to be more direct and intuitive, we will be refunding any Lunacy spent on the Stage Continue function for that Encounter prior to the description change.

    - Eligible Recipients: All Managers who have used the "Stage Continue" function in the boss fight featured on 5F of Main Story - Canto X <Part 3> during the following period: 2026.10.01 12:00 (KST) ~ Before Hotfix deployment
    - Refund Details: All Lunacy spent on the "Stage Continue" function in the boss fight featured on 5F of Main Story - Canto X <Part 3> will be refunded
    - When: On the Oct. 8th Scheduled Update

    Ver. 1.116.1 that fixes the known issues identified in Ver. 1.116.0, updated Oct. 1st, will be deployed soon.

    As mobile platforms (AOS, iOS) require the update to go through each respective market review, the same updates cannot be deployed immediately in the case of the mobile environment.

    Therefore, this update will be deployed to Steam first, and we are currently preparing builds to make them available for Mobile platforms as well.

    Until the update is applied to the AOS and iOS platforms, we would like to ask our mobile users to access the game on Steam if possible.

    We will inform you via a separate notice as soon as updates become available on each AOS and iOS platform.

    We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience.
