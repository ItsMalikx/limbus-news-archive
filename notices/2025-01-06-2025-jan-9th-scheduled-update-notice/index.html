2025, Jan. 9th Scheduled Update Notice

January 6, 2025
Identities & E.G.OEventsNew ContentBalance

Greetings, dear Manager.

The server will enter scheduled maintenance from 10:00 [AM] through 12:00 [PM] on Jan. 9th, 2025 (KST). Please make note of the maintenance schedule; we recommend that you refrain from entering combat or making in-app purchases and close the application before maintenance begins so that no data is lost.

Bug Fixes & Improvements

▶ Added a function to stop Heathcliff Identities from activating Retreat abilities in certain Encounters featured in Canto 6
※ Explanation: This was added to prevent Heathcliff Identities from causing progression issues due to the Retreat ability that may cause other interactions to malfunction

▶ Fixed an issue where, if a unit with a certain effect Retreats or is defeated, the Substituted Backup unit's Skill Slots malfunction

▶ Removed the 'Deal +50% more damage' ability from the Coin attached to the enemy unit Peccatulum Superbiae?'s Defense Skill 'Counter'

▶ Fixed an issue where, in the Team Loadout menu for the 2nd ~ 4th Walpurgis Night Combat Encounters, the Stage-specific buff icon for the 1st Deployed Sinner did not appear

▶ Fixed an issue where, when moving from the Event menu to a different menu via the Uptie function, the screen would load incorrectly

▶ Added a "Don't see this message again" checkbox option to the Spoiler alert pop-up window

Known Issues after 2025. 01. 09 Scheduled Update

▶ An issue where the Buff Icon applied to the 1st Deployed Sinner in the Loadout UI is switched between the 2nd and the 4th Walpurgis Night Encounters

▶ An issue where choosing to exit the 4th Walpurgis Night Loadout UI with the [ ← ] button causes the game to softlock

- When : After Jan. 9th, 2025 (Thu) 12:00 (KST)

Notes

- Please update your game client to the newest version on the respective store (Google Play/App Store/Steam).
- Forcibly shutting down the game while it is downloading resources may cause the download to fail, so please be careful not to close the application during the download.
- You will not be able to play <Limbus Company> during maintenance. Please understand.
- The maintenance schedule may suddenly change depending on the status of maintenance. If such a change occurs, we will let you know in a follow-up notice.
- A resource download may be prompted after the build update. If you're using a mobile device, it is recommended to proceed with a Wi-Fi connection.
- If you're playing with a guest account, we recommend that you link your account with other platforms to prevent possible account loss.
- The maintenance compensation will be provided once per account. Please note that the distribution may be slow due to network delays or other causes.
- The update gifts are valid to claim for 7 days. Please redeem them within this timeframe.

Thank you.
