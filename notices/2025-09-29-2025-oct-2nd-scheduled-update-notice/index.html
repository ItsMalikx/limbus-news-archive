2025, Oct. 2nd Scheduled Update Notice

September 29, 2025
Identities & E.G.OMain StoryBalanceRewards

Greetings, Dear Manager.

The server will enter scheduled maintenance from 10:00 [AM] through 12:00 [PM] on Oct. 2nd, 2025 (KST).

Please make note of the maintenance schedule; we recommend that you refrain from entering combat or making in-app purchases and close the application before maintenance begins so that no data is lost.

Please refer below for details about the update.

Google Play Full Moon Campaign Commemorative Pack Notice

To commemorate Google Play's Full Moon Campaign, the following three upgrade resource packs will be available for sale at the shop.

※ The new Full Moon Campaign Commemorative Packages will be purchasable via Paid Lunacy regardless of the platform at the in-game Lunacy Shop.

▶ Purchasable Period : 2025/10/2 12:00 ~ 2025/10/16 10:00 (KST)

▶ Package Contents
(1) Full Moon 2025 Commemorative ID Upgrade Pack
- Contents: Takeoff Module - ID Uptie x1, Identity Training Ticket IV x140
- Price: Paid Lunacy x2500

(2) Full Moon 2025 Commemorative E.G.O Upgrade Pack
- Contents: Decaextraction Ticket x3, Takeoff Module - E.G.O Spinning [WAW] x1
- Price: Paid Lunacy x4150

(3) Full Moon 2025 Commemorative Complete Pack
- Contents: Decaextraction Ticket x4, Takeoff Module - ID Uptie x1, Takeoff Module - E.G.O Spinning [WAW] x1, Identity Training Ticket IV x140, Threads x100
- Price: Paid Lunacy x6750

※ Using the Takeoff Module - ID Uptie raises the selected Identity's Uptie Tier to 4.

※ When used, E.G.O [WAW] Upgrade Pack raises a selected E.G.O's Threadspinning Tier up to Tier 4. (Usable only on E.G.Os WAW Level or lower)

Bug Fixes & Improvements

▶ Fixed an issue where Blade Lineage Mentor Meursault's Support Passive "Swordplay of the Homeland" did not work as described
※ Relevant effect: "Combat start: 1 ally with the most Poise deals +15% damage on Critical Hit with Slash Skills"
- Before : Gave +15% damage boost regardless of whether they critically hit or not
- After : Gives +15% damage boost to damage on Critical Hit, as written

▶ Fixed an issue where, in Stage LC-PC-14" of Ch. 8.5-EX Pilgrimage of Compassion, if The Lord of Hongyuan Hong Lu used the E.G.O "Tears of the Tarnished Blood" and gains the power-binding Wedge effects, a certain trigger could cause him to never use the Skill "Tears of the Tarnished Blood - The End", which meant that he could indefinitely maintain the undying state

▶ Fixed an issue where having a certain debuff could softlock Stage LC-PC-11 in Ch. 8.5-EX Pilgrimage of Compassion

▶ Fixed an issue where, in Ch. 8.5-EX Pilgrimage of Compassion Stages featuring the Hand of the Saint unit, a certain trigger could cause the battle to never end

▶ Fixed an issue where, in a battle against a certain enemy in Stage LC-PC-14 in Ch. 8.5-EX Pilgrimage of Compassion, the Shield vfx could load in even when there was no shield

▶ Fixed an issue where certain units' buffs and Passives would not activate in the second phase of Stage 8.5-11
※ Affected Identities : The Lord of Hongyuan Hong Lu's "Leash-holder of All Heishou" and Full-Stop Office Fixer Heathcliff's "Pierce Power Up"

▶ Fixed an issue where, in Stage 8.5-4 days with Mandatory Classes, the "Mandatory Class" notification would not appear without any input from the player

▶ Fixed an issue where "Conditionally Activated E.G.O Gifts" list contained E.G.O Gifts that do not belong in that category
※ Affected E.G.O Gifts : The Chalice of Trickle-Down, Black Ledger

▶ Fixed an issue where 1) the player does not have enough Lunacy in the Extraction results screen to re-extract, 2) goes to a Lunacy purchase menu, then proceeds to claim it from the in-game Inbox, and 3) when a scene transition (for the Announcer acquisition scene) occurs, certain UI elements linger, obscuring the Announcer acquisition scene

▶ (JP Only) Fixed an issue where, in Mirror Dungeon Gift Fusion & Enhancement menus, certain E.G.O Gifts would load with their first lines cut off

▶ Fixed a UI issue that occurs when using Pack Search to swap out the Mirror Dungeon Theme Pack "Pilgrimage of Compassion" for a different Theme Pack

▶ Fixed an issue where, after getting defeated in a certain Stage of Ch. 8.5-EX Pilgrimage of Compassion, the defeat menu would briefly play the incorrect music

▶ Swapped out the background image for the Attendance Check event pop-up window to put a visual emphasis on the Day 7 reward
※ This does not change the actual reward; this only changes the background image of the UI.

▶ Fixed an issue where, in Mirror Dungeon of the Fathoms, viewing the list of Identities shows "Edit Team" instead of "Details"

▶ Fixed an issue the message that pops up when attempting to purchase an already-purchased Paid Lunacy Pack would show a description that does not match the context
- Before : Not Enough Paid Lunacy.
- After : This item has already been purchased.

▶ Fixed an issue where Choice Event check success probability flavor texts from Sinners did not match the actual probability
- Before : At "Very High", the flavor texts for "High" appeared.
- After : At "Very High", the correct flavor texts now load.

▶ Fixed an issue where the camera suddenly zooms in very close after Choice Events in Ch. 8.5-EX Pilgrimage of Compassion

▶ Fixed an issue where, in the Stage Info menu, certain enemies with overly long names would sometimes obscure their portraits on the UI

▶ Fixed an issue where, when using a Skill with 2+ Atk Weight, the targeting arrow's color would stop differentiating main targets and sub-targets

▶ (EN Only) Standardization for inconsistent Skill phrasing
- Before : 2 different phrases for the same function existed - At (Sum of X and Y)~, For every (Sum of X and Y)~ VS. At (X + Y)~, For every (X + Y)~
- After : Standardized to the following phrasing - At (X + Y)~, For every (X + Y)~

▶ Fixed an issue where dummy texts would appear in the text log of Stage LC-PC-11 in Ch. 8.5-EX Pilgrimage of Compassion

- If you're playing with a guest account, we recommend that you link your account with other platforms to prevent possible account loss.
- The maintenance compensation will be provided once per account. Please note that the distribution may be slow due to network delays or other causes.
