    (AOS, iOS) Notice: Game Update Following Crashing Issue in ver. 1.95.0

    January 9, 2026

    Hello, this is Project Moon.

    An issue that could block game progression was identified following the ver. 1.95.0 update on Jan. 8.
    As we have previously announced, the game will be updated to ver. 1.95.1 on AOS and iOS to address this issue.

    Please close the game completely and download the update for your application, and the special combat/battle cinematic issue that occurred on Ver. 1.95.0 will be fixed.

    [ 1.95.1 Version Update Schedule ]

    - Date: After Jan. 9th (Fri), 16:00 (KST)
    - Content: Fixed the issues in ver. 1.95.0, updated Jan. 8th, 2026

    ※ Ver. 1.95.0, which had the cinematic issue, will no longer be accessible after Jan. 10th (Sat), 00:00 (KST). Please update your game to ver. 1.95.1.

    [ Fixed Issues ]

    1. An issue where the game may crash when combat cinematics are played in certain combat Stages
    2. An issue where special battle cinematics do not play

    Please note that following a successful update installation, the version number displayed at the bottom-right of the title screen will show 1.95.1.

    If your application storefront does not display the option to update the game, then please check again at a later time as there may be delays depending on your device environments.

    We once again sincerely apologize for the inconvenience.
