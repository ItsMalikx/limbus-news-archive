2026, Nov. 19th Scheduled Update Notice

November 18, 2026
BalanceMain Story

Greetings, Dear Manager.

The server will enter scheduled maintenance from 10:00 AM through 12:00 PM on November 19th, 2026 (KST).

Please make note of the maintenance schedule; we recommend that you refrain from entering combat or making in-app purchases and close the application before maintenance begins so that no data is lost.

Please refer to the details below for the update.

Major Changes

1. Improved Text on the Unit Details Synergy Tab

The relevant Keywords will now appear above the list of Identities and E.G.O affected by each Synergy effect on the Identity details Synergy tab.

2. Added a Schemacraft Button

The button to access "Schemacraft" will be added to the Drive tab.
※ This button has been added temporarily to provide access to Schemacraft gamemode, which will arrive with a future update. We will provide further information in a separate notice when Schemacraft is updated.

(1) Added animations for the Wedge Office Identities recovering from Stagger
(2) Improved the animation of Skill 2, "Sparking Spear," for "Wedge Office Rep Outis"
※ The improved animation will differ from the one shown in the PV.
(3) Fixed an issue where certain effects for "Wedge Office Rep Outis" appeared at unnatural angles

Notes

- A resource download may be prompted after the build update. If you're using a mobile device, it is recommended to proceed with a Wi-Fi connection.
- If you're playing with a guest account, we recommend that you link your account with other platforms to prevent possible account loss.
- The maintenance compensation will be provided once per account. Please note that the distribution may be slow due to network delays or other causes.
