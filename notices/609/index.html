[New Content - Ch. 9.5: Mnestic Experience]

2026-06-10

-------------------------------------------

Greetings, Dear Manager.

With the Jun. 11th, 2026 (KST) Update, new content [Ch. 9.5: Mnestic Experience] will be added to the game.

► Duration
2026/6/11 12:00 (KST) ~ Before 7/9 (KST) Scheduled Update
► Event Rewards Exchange
2026/6/11 12:00 (KST) ~ Before 7/16 (KST) Scheduled Update
► Requirements
- Event Unlock Conditions: Clear Main Story 0-4
- Event Stage Unlock Conditions: Clear Main Story Chapter 9.5 - Twining Threads

Event Description
-----------------

- When the event starts, the [Ch. 9.5 - Mnestic Experience] Event Page will open.
- During the event period, you can earn [Photos] by clearing the Mnestic Experience Event Stages & the Mirror Dungeons.
- You can redeem various rewards from the [Reward Exchange] by spending the [Photos] that you have collected.

※ A new content update, “Reflectrial][lairtcelfeR - Mnestic Experience” is scheduled for Jun. 18th (Thu), allowing Managers to challenge the boss unit featured in [Ch. 9.5 - Mnestic Experience] at a higher difficulty.

Details will be provided via a future notice.

Event Gameplay
--------------

► Combat Encounters of Mnestic Experience
You can obtain [Photos] every time you complete a combat Encounter of Mnestic Experience Event. Each Encounter has a first-time reward, and with it, you can obtain even more [Photos].

The final Stage will have a higher Enkephalin-to-[Photos] ratio, and will be much more effective at collecting [Photos].

► Mirror Dungeons
During the Event Period, [Mnestic Experience] Theme Pack will have a fixed appearance on the 5th floor of both Mirror Dungeon Normal & Hard. The Theme Pack features exclusive Choice Events and E.G.O Gifts.

※ Once the event is over, the Mnestic Experience Theme Pack will no longer have a fixed appearance and may appear on different floors.

Clearing a Mirror Dungeon run will yield [Photos]. Selecting the [Mnestic Experience] Theme Pack will yield even more [Photos].

The Theme Pack on Hard Difficulty yields more Event currency than on Normal Difficulty.

※ Please note that Weekly Bonuses do not affect the amount of Event currency gained.

※ Please note that Mirror Dungeon runs started prior to the event will not yield Event currency.

► Main Story
Managers who have not met the Event unlock conditions can obtain [Photos] as a first-time reward from Main Story Combat Encounters.

Upon your first time clearing an Encounter, you can obtain [Photos] proportional to the amount of Enkephalin Spent, regardless of whether you have met the EX Clear Conditions or not.

※ You cannot obtain the event currency from stages that have already been completed.

※ You cannot obtain [Photos] from Luxcavations or the Refraction Railway.

Event Currency
--------------

In rare cases, clearing the Mnestic Experience Stages, the Mirror Dungeon, and the Main Story Stages will also yield [Photo Bundles].

[Photo Bundles] have the highest chance of dropping from the final Stage of the Mnestic Experience Event. However, the drop chance is the same across all other Encounters.

[Photo Bundles] are automatically converted to 20 [Photos] in the Results or the Rewards screen upon acquisition.

Event Bonus Units
-----------------

During the event period, deploying the following Identities on your team will boost the amount of [Photos] earned.
You can earn a maximum of 120% boost to the amount of event currency earned.

Obtain more [Photos] by completing Event Encounters or the Mirror Dungeon while equipped with Event Bonus units.

<Event Bonus Units>

► 40% Bonus
- 3★ : S Corp. Ch'unokkun Hong Lu, LCD OSIR Team Ishmael
- E.G.O [HE] : Shadow-vested Bladesinger [着影揮刀] Meursault

► 20% Bonus
- 3★ : Blade Lineage Mentor Meursault, Blade Lineage Salsu Faust, Blade Lineage Salsu Yi Sang, Blade Lineage Salsu Sinclair
- 2★ : Blade Lineage Salsu Don Quixote, Blade Lineage Salsu Outis

※ The Event Currency Bonus from each Event Identity and E.G.O is additive (rounded up).

※ Event Currency Bonus maxes out at 120% Bonus.

※ Event Bonus Units do not have to participate in a battle; you can earn the bonus currency simply by having them in your Team Loadout.

※ Event Currency Bonus applies to first-time clear rewards and Mirror Dungeon clear rewards as well.

Event Rewards
-------------

At the [Rewards Exchange], you can spend [Photos] you have collected to purchase various rewards.

※ You can no longer obtain [Photos] after you obtain enough [Photos] to purchase every item in the [Reward Exchange].

※ [Photos] will no longer be obtainable once the Event closes after the 2026/7/9 (KST) Scheduled Update.

※ [Reward Exchange] will be open for 5 weeks, from 2026/6/11 12:00 (KST) ~ 2026/7/16 (KST) Scheduled Update.

※ Even after the event fully ends, you will be able to experience the Mnestic Experience Combat Encounters or view its story anytime at Drive and Theater - [Detour Tales].
