    Notice: additional Known Issues Ver. 1.85.0 Updated Aug. 28th

    August 28, 2025

    Hello, this is Project Moon.

    We would like to inform you about additional issues that have been identified in Ver. 1.85.0.

    Please continue reading for more information.

    [ Known Issues ]

    - (AOS) An issue where Cultivation Stage 8.5-4 cannot be progressed properly on certain devices

    - An issue where, in auto-battle Stages for Jia Xichun, if the Clashes are chained in the first turn, the Clashing noise won't stop playing repeatedly
    ※ If the Clashing noise plays during combat:
    In the Chaining phase, hover over the Skill slots above character portraits.
    ※ If the Clashing noise won't stop playing outside combat:
    Please restart the game.

    - An issue where, in Floor 5 of MD Hard, selecting Long Battle Theme Packs won't give the ""+3 Levels"" effect

    - An issue where, when viewing the story log in Stage 8.5-4, the scroll bar on the right won't show

    - An issue where, in rare cases, pressing individual ""Claim Achievement"" or ""Claim All"" could cause the Error Code 76615.
    ※ Please select the ""Close"" option, and you should be able to progress.

    We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience.
