    Notice: Additional Fixes & Adversity Info in 2025.09.04 (KST) Scheduled Update

    September 3, 2025
    Balance

    Hello, this is Project Moon.

    In this notice, we would like to inform you regarding certain issues that were missing from Ver. 1.85.2(Updated Sep. 4th) Scheduled Update Notice this week, and additional information regarding the EXTREME Mode.

    Please continue reading for more information.

    [Bug Fixes & Improvements]

    - Adjusted a certain "Achievement" completion condition for the "Spring Cultivation" Minigame

    - Before : Take every teacher's class. [X/13]
    - After : Take every teacher's class. [X/12]

    [Additional information regarding EXTREME Mode]

    As shared in the MD Parallel Superposition EXTREME Changes & Update Schedule Notice, the "Multiplies the amount of E.G.O Resources consumed when using E.G.O Skills" Adversity is currently not selectable.

    Therefore, certain MD Achievements are currently incompletable as of now in Ver. 1.85.2.

    [ Achievements incompletable in Ver. 1.85.2 ]

    - Select 1 or more "Ego Interference" and clear the Mirror Dungeon
    - Clear the Mirror Dungeon at 50+ Adversity Score
    - Clear all Achievements

    ※ As of Ver. 1.85.2, the highest achievable Adversity Score is 45.

    As shared in our previous notice, we will be adjusting the logic to affect only the E.G.O Resource consumption multiplication and nothing else before adding it back to the game, and the above Achievements will be completable once that change is deployed.

    We will share a more concrete schedule with you via a separate notice when they are applied.

    We sincerely apologize for the missing information and the lack of detail in our previous notice.
    We will try to deploy these changes as soon as possible to minimize any inconvenience that may come from these.

    Thank you.
