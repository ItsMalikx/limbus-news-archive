Known Issues in Mar. 12th ver. 1.100.1 Scheduled Update

March 11, 2026

Hello, this is Project Moon.

We have identified several issues following the Mar. 12th Scheduled Update.
Please continue reading for more details.

[ List of Issues ]

► An issue where battle progression is blocked when The House of Spiders: The Middle Apprentice Ishmael's Façade is projected onto an Ishmael Identity
► An issue where game progression is blocked when an enemy unit uses the "Imperfect Imitation" Skill during combat in the Mirror Dungeon
► (Mobile) An issue where the image of Heishou Identities that launch follow-up attacks following The Lord of Hongyuan Hong Lu's Skill appears darkened during combat
► (Mobile) An issue where the Uptie animation is displayed abnormally when Uptying an Identity to Tier III

※ We kindly ask our Managers to refrain from using The House of Spiders: The Middle Apprentice Ishmael's Façade until the issue is fixed.

We are currently investigating the cause of these issues and will inform our Managers via a separate notice once identification is complete.

We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience.
